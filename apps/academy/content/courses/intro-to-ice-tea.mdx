# 🌱 Introduction to ICE Tea Network
<TrackBadge variant="default" />

Welcome to the ICE Tea Network — a decentralized, abolitionist project built to protect and support communities facing surveillance, raids, and criminalization.

---

<Callout type="info">
ICE Tea isn't a single organization — it's a distributed network of people, tools, and tactics working together for community safety.
</Callout>

---

## What Is ICE Tea?

ICE Tea is a mutual aid network that helps communities respond to immigration enforcement and police activity. It provides tools for:

- Reporting ICE and law enforcement sightings
- Dispatching trusted volunteers to witness and support
- Training community members in field and digital safety
- Coordinating care, legal, and logistics roles during actions

The platform and protocols are designed to be **secure**, **decentralized**, and **scalable** — built for grassroots organizers, not bureaucracies.

---

## Our Values

We are:

- **Abolitionist** — We believe in dismantling systems of policing, detention, and borders.
- **Decentralized** — We don’t rely on a central command. Trust is distributed.
- **Community-led** — Locals know best. We train people to protect their own.
- **Security, safety, Surveillance Protection** — Safety isn’t optional. We use encrypted tools and protect sensitive data from surveilliance..
- **Mutual aid–rooted** — We offer support, not charity. Everyone gives and receives as they are able.

## Who Makes Up ICE Tea?

There’s no “ICE Tea HQ.” Instead, the network is made of:

- 🧍‍♀️ **Field responders** (witnesses, legal observers, medics)
- 🧠 **Dispatchers** (trusted coordinators who activate responders)
- 🛠 **Tool stewards** (people who build and secure the platform)
- 💞 **Care teams** (supporting people impacted by enforcement)

You don’t need to be a tech expert or a legal scholar to help. What matters is **trust, training, and readiness**.

---

## Roles Within the Network

While ICE Tea is known for dispatching support, it also includes:

- **Observers** — Witness and document police/ICE activity
- **Care Stations** — Provide physical safety, water, support
- **Runners / Rideshare** — Help with movement and exit
- **Digital Support** — Manage maps, reports, and comms
- **Legal Coordination** — Help track arrests or violations

## A Note on Trust

Trust is earned — not assumed. To receive access to certain tools or roles, you must complete specific trainings. These include:

- 💬 Role quizzes
- 🔐 Digital security pledges
- ✅ Dispatch certifications

This keeps the network safe, scalable, and hard to infiltrate.
