## Background

When communication lines failed across the Rim, engineers became storytellers—keeping crews aligned with nothing but shared signals and stubborn persistence. Beacons flickered over ravines and rooftops, guiding mutual aid fleets to caches that mattered most. In the end, resilience wasn’t a switch; it was a rhythm.

This Season calls for quiet heroics: repair what’s broken, relay what matters, and pull others back into range.
