## What Is a Dispatch Call?

A dispatch call is a coordinated alert to trusted volunteers about urgent or verified presence — typically involving:

- ICE or law enforcement presence
- Community members being followed or detained
- Planned sweeps or suspected raids
- Requests for legal observers, care, or documentation

Calls are sent via **Signal** and show up in your **dispatch map** or notification thread.

---

## What to Expect in a Call

Dispatch messages follow a standard format:

```
🚨 CALL FOR COMMUNITY SUPPORT 🚨

📍 Location: [Area or intersection]
📋 Action: [Roles needed or support requested]
🧠 Notes: [Short summary or context]
🕓 Timing: [Now / 30 min / tonight]
📲 Reply in Signal thread if available
```

You may also get a link to a secure map or encrypted chat. Do **not** repost or forward messages unless directed.

## How to Respond

If you're available and willing:

1. ✅ **Reply promptly** in the thread (or via check-in link)  
   > “Available. Can be on-site in 20min.”  
2. 🧠 **State your role or capacity**  
   > “Can do Legal Observing, no car.”  
3. 🚙 **Coordinate meet-up or coverage zone**  
4. 🤫 **Avoid chatter or emoji spam** — keep the thread clear for updates

---

## If You Can’t Go

Letting others know is just as important:

- "Unavailable but tracking thread"
- "Out of town, can’t join"
- "Watching from afar, can do report cleanup after"

This helps dispatchers focus and prevents ghost-pinging.

---

## Preparing for a Call-Up

Keep a **go kit** ready with:

- 🧥 Jacket / scarf for blend-in visibility
- 📱 Phone with Signal + portable charger
- 🧾 Notepad or encrypted note app
- 🧃 Water + snacks
- 🪪 KYR card / badge if applicable

Also:

- 💬 Have your Signal name match your field alias  
- 🔕 Silence all alerts but dispatch threads  
- 🧭 Review where you are in relation to the call area

---

## Trust & Coverage

Only trusted volunteers with Level 2 status or above are activated. You might:

- Cover an area solo or in pairs  
- Check-in by signal emoji ("👀 here 4 blocks")  
- Rotate out with backup after a few hours

All responders are expected to:

- Log observations (securely or verbally)
- Respect community consent
- Never escalate

---

## When NOT to Respond

Skip the call if:

- You’re feeling unstable or burnt out  
- You’re unsure about legality or role fit  
- You’re already in a high-risk environment  
- You’re tired and likely to make mistakes

<Callout type="warning">
A shaky ally can do more harm than an absent one. Know your limits.
</Callout>

<Callout type="hint">
Documentation hygiene: Use minimal, time-bound retention. Avoid storing raw chat logs or sensitive dispatch notes in shared apps. Prefer encrypted, access-controlled notes and delete temporary files after review. ART does not support in-app dispatch log archiving; use secure external tools if needed and always get explicit consent before sharing plans or protocols.
</Callout>

---

## After the Call

Once off the scene:

- ✅ Mark yourself as “clear” in the thread  
- 📝 Log any important observations privately  
- ❤️ Take care of your nervous system — eat, hydrate, decompress  
- 💬 DM the dispatcher with a 1-2 sentence debrief if relevant
