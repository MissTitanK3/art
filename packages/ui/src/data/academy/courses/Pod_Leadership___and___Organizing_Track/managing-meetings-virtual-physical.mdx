# 🗣️ Managing and Conducting Meetings (Virtual & Physical)
<TrackBadge variant="pod-leadership" />
<TrackBadge variant="movement-strategy" />

<Callout type="important">
**Meetings are systems, not conversations.**  
They require rhythm, containment, and trust to create clarity without burnout or leaks.
</Callout>

## 1. Meeting Fundamentals

Every meeting needs:
- **Purpose:** why you are meeting.  
- **Agenda:** what must be resolved.  
- **Facilitator:** who controls flow and time.  
- **Scribe:** who records only outcomes.  
- **Close:** clear end and follow-up.  

> Unclear beginnings waste time.  
> Unclear endings waste trust.

---

## 2. Virtual Meeting Management

### Tools
- Use **Signal**, **Jitsi**, or **Matrix/Element** for encrypted sessions.  
- Disable AI transcription, cloud recording, and voice assistants.  
- Prefer **audio-only** when bandwidth or safety is limited.  

### Practices
- Begin with a **sound check and presence check**.  
- Ask: “If not in privacy, please mute or relocate.”  
- Share screens only for essential visuals.  
- Use a **shared timer** to regulate pacing.  

---

## 3. Physical Meetings

### Space Setup
- Choose neutral rooms — no cameras or smart devices.  
- Provide **Faraday pouches** or phone-drop tables.  
- Arrange **circle or horseshoe** seating for visibility and equity.  
- Erase whiteboards before departure.  

### Entry & Exit
- State norms up front: confidentiality, time, and signals.  
- Start on time; late arrivals wait until break.  
- End with **recap, commitments, and confirmation**.  

---

## 4. Surveillance Awareness

### Identify
- Hidden mics, IoT devices, or wearables.  
- Bluetooth and Wi-Fi activity nearby.  
- Apps that auto-store audio.  

### Mitigate
- Disable internet or use **Faraday containers**.  
- Rotate meeting times and locations.  
- Confirm no open network devices.  

<Callout type="warning">
If the room connects to the internet, the room can leak your meeting.
</Callout>

---

## 5. Facilitation Dynamics

### Rhythm
Meetings have an energy wave: calm open → build engagement → peak → calm close.  

- Pause every 25–30 minutes.  
- Transition clearly: “We’re moving from updates to decisions.”  

### Regulation
- Maintain neutral tone.  
- Slow pace when tension rises.  
- Acknowledge emotion, redirect to structure:  
  > “Let’s record that concern and revisit after outcomes.”

### Grounding Tools
- **Talking piece:** controls who speaks.  
- **Stack:** facilitator tracks raised hands.  
- **Step up / step back:** frequent speakers yield space.  

---

## 6. Ensuring All Voices Are Heard

1. **Structured rounds:** each speaks once before open floor.  
2. **Reflective listening:** facilitator paraphrases to confirm.  
3. **Silence:** 5–10 seconds after prompts.  
4. **Temperature checks:** “+” = agree, “–” = concern.  

<Callout type="info">
Use inclusive phrasing like “any additions?” instead of “anyone else?” to reduce pressure.
</Callout>

---

## 7. The Closing Sequence

Every meeting ends the same:

1. Summarize decisions.  
2. Confirm actions, owners, and deadlines.  
3. Ask: “Any **questions, comments, concerns, or compliments** before we close?”  
4. Schedule next meeting and facilitator.  
5. Confirm secure handling of notes or recordings.  

> Consistent closing builds accountability and psychological safety.

---

## 8. Documentation & Information Hygiene

- Capture **outcomes, not conversation**.  
- Store notes in **encrypted, access-controlled folders**.  
- Send summaries via secure channel only.  
- Delete temporary files once reviewed.  

<Callout type="hint">
Follow [Documentation Safety](/courses/documentation-safety) and [After-Action Data Hygiene](/courses/after-action-data-hygiene).  
Avoid cloud-recorded meetings or persistent chat logs.
</Callout>

---

## 9. Accessibility & Inclusion

- Schedule with timezone and rest awareness.  
- Offer captioning, summaries, and visual notes.  
- Use neutral lighting and minimal background motion for video calls.  
- Ask explicitly: “Is this pace comfortable for everyone?”  

---

## 10. Final Checklist

- [ ] Purpose and agenda set  
- [ ] Facilitator, scribe, and timekeeper confirmed  
- [ ] Security sweep complete  
- [ ] Pacing plan defined  
- [ ] All voices heard  
- [ ] Closed with recap and confirmation  

<Callout type="success">
**Good meetings protect time, trust, and truth.**  
Strong containers prevent leaks and preserve energy for real work.
</Callout>
