# 🌐 Inter-Pod & Regional Coordination
<TrackBadge variant="pod-leadership" />
<TrackBadge variant="logistics" />

<Callout type="info">
This course is in **Level 4: Zone Lead (Admin Dispatcher)**.  
It focuses on **linking multiple pods across counties and regions**, setting up **fallback systems**, and maintaining mutual aid coordination during **large-scale or prolonged events**.  
</Callout>

Ensure you understand access roles and trust signatures for cross‑pod collaboration. See <a href="/courses/admin-tools">Admin Tools & Access Roles</a> and align with <a href="/courses/data-ethics-pledge">Data Ethics Pledge</a> for inter‑pod data handling.
</Callout>

## Why It Matters

No single pod can handle **large-scale raids, multi-day protests, or natural disasters** alone.  
Linking pods with **trusted communications, shared resources, and fallback agreements** ensures the network stays operational and resilient.

Plan comms fallbacks across pods: <a href="/courses/dealing-with-chaos">Dealing With Chaos</a>, <a href="/courses/comms-collapse">Comms Collapse</a>, and <a href="/courses/app-free-tactics">App‑Free Tactics</a>. ## What You'll Learn

1. **Regional Pod Mapping** – Identifying pods, trusted partners, and support zones across your region.  
2. **Trust-Building Protocols** – Using **slow trust practices and verification** to avoid infiltration or extractive dynamics.  
3. **Fallback Coordination** – Establishing **shared staging areas, housing, and resupply networks**.  
4. **Cross-Pod Comms** – Using **encrypted channels, radios, or offline networks** to link pods securely.  
5. **Load Balancing** – How to distribute volunteers and resources during overlapping incidents.  
6. **Reciprocal Agreements** – Drafting simple, **clear expectations and trust protocols** with other pods.

---

## Trust-Building & Verification

- **Start small:** Test collaborations with **low-risk exchanges** (supply runs, shared medics) before deep integration.  
- **Verify contacts:** Cross-check new pods via **two trusted references** before adding to secure networks.  
- **Balance power:** Avoid extractive dynamics by tracking **resource and labor flows** between large and small pods.

<Callout type="info">
Language access matters for cross‑pod trust. Include volunteers holding the <a href="/courses/translator-micro-badge">Translator Micro‑badge</a> or <a href="/courses/asl-interpreter-micro-badge">ASL Interpreter Micro‑badge</a> in planning and MOUs.
</Callout>

---

## Quick Action Steps

1. Maintain a **private contact list** of regional pods (encrypted digital + paper backup).  
2. Pre-designate **fallback staging and supply points** for major incidents.  
3. Use **multi-channel comms** (Signal, LoRa, radios) with **clear protocols for backups**.  
4. Draft **Reciprocal Agreements (MOUs)** so everyone knows expectations and limits in advance.  
5. **Run at least one regional drill** per year (communications, staging, load balancing).

<Callout type="hint">
Keep shared docs minimal and time‑bound. Follow <a href="/courses/documentation-safety">Documentation & Safety</a> and <a href="/courses/after-action-data-hygiene">After‑Action Data Hygiene</a> for inter‑pod retention.
</Callout>

## Sample Reciprocal Agreement (Outline)

- **Resource Sharing:** Define what each pod can offer in emergencies (medical, food, housing).  
- **Comms Protocols:** List primary and backup channels for urgent alerts.  
- **Boundaries:** Clarify what’s not guaranteed (bail funds, legal defense).  
- **Trust Practices:** Agree on cross-verification and reporting suspicious behavior.  
- **Duration:** Review and renew every 6–12 months.

## Crisis Simulation (Practice Exercise)

*"Scenario: A raid disables three pods in your region. Using your pod map:*  
1. Identify fallback supply and housing points.  
2. Assign pods to host displaced members.  
3. Activate backup comms channels.  
4. Redistribute volunteer pools to cover uncovered areas."

---

## Load Balancing Guidelines

- **Triage by urgency and capacity:** Focus first on life-safety and time-sensitive calls.  
- **Maintain a cross-trained volunteer pool:** Keep a list of members who can **deploy to support overwhelmed pods**.  
- **Document requests and responses:** Prevent double coverage or missed needs.

---

## When NOT to Coordinate

- If a pod is under **active surveillance or infiltration**, pause formal links until cleared.  
- If coordination would expose **sensitive community members**, default to isolation.

---

## Risks & Things to Watch

- Over-sharing can **expose pods to infiltration or surveillance**.  
- Poor planning can cause **resource depletion or burnout**.  
- Trust takes time — **do not shortcut verification**, even during crises.

<Callout type="warning">
Never centralize PII from multiple pods. Use compartmentalization and device hygiene from <a href="/courses/digital-security-basics">Digital Security Basics</a>.
</Callout>
