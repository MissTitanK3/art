# 🌐 Inter-Pod & Regional Coordination
<TrackBadge variant="pod-leadership" />
<TrackBadge variant="logistics" />

<Callout type="info">
Level 4: **Zone Lead / Admin Dispatcher**  
This course trains regional leads to coordinate multiple pods, establish fallback networks, and maintain resilience through crises, large-scale raids, or disasters.
</Callout>

<Callout type="hint">

## Why It Matters

No single pod can manage sustained or multi-zone operations alone.  
Cross-pod collaboration builds **redundancy, continuity, and resilience** across the network.

<Callout type="info">
Plan comms fallbacks with [Dealing With Chaos](/courses/dealing-with-chaos), [Comms Collapse](/courses/comms-collapse), and [App-Free Tactics](/courses/app-free-tactics).  
For transport, use [Runners & Rides](/courses/runners-and-rides).  
For activation, see [Dispatch Coordination](/courses/dispatch-coordination) and [Responding to Dispatch Calls](/courses/responding-to-dispatch-calls).
</Callout>

## What You'll Learn

1. **Regional Pod Mapping** — Identify pods, trusted partners, and fallback zones.  
2. **Trust Protocols** — Use slow verification and non-extractive relationships.  
3. **Fallback Coordination** — Share staging, housing, and resupply networks.  
4. **Cross-Pod Comms** — Maintain encrypted, redundant channels.  
5. **Load Balancing** — Redistribute volunteers during overlapping incidents.  
6. **Reciprocal Agreements (MOUs)** — Formalize mutual expectations and boundaries.

---

## Trust-Building & Verification

- Start with **low-risk collaboration** before sharing infrastructure.  
- Verify contacts through **two independent references**.  
- Track **labor and resource flow** to prevent extraction or burnout.  
- Include **language and accessibility roles** for true inclusion.

<Callout type="info">
Integrate [Translator Micro-Badge](/courses/translator-micro-badge) and [ASL Interpreter Micro-Badge](/courses/asl-interpreter-micro-badge) holders into regional planning for equitable participation.
</Callout>

---

## Quick Action Steps

1. Maintain a **secure regional contact list** (encrypted + paper backup).  
2. Pre-designate **fallback staging areas** for resupply or housing.  
3. Use **multi-channel comms**: Signal, LoRa, radio, or in-person relays.  
4. Draft **Reciprocal Agreements (MOUs)** with clear scope and expiration.  
5. Run **one annual regional drill** testing coordination and comms fallback.

<Callout type="hint">
Use minimal data retention. Follow [Documentation Safety](/courses/documentation-safety) and [After-Action Data Hygiene](/courses/after-action-data-hygiene).
</Callout>

---

## Reciprocal Agreement (Outline)

- **Purpose & Scope** — Shared goals and regional context  
- **Resources** — Medical, supply, transport, or housing capacity  
- **Comms Plan** — Primary and backup channels  
- **Boundaries** — What’s out of scope (legal, funds, housing limits)  
- **Verification** — Contact references and rotation intervals  
- **Duration** — Review every 6–12 months  

---

## Simulation: Regional Crisis Drill

*Scenario:* A raid disables three pods in your region.  
Tasks:
1. Activate fallback housing and supply zones.  
2. Assign pods to cover uncovered districts.  
3. Use backup comms (LoRa, radio, runner).  
4. Document response and recovery timeline.

---

## Load Balancing

- Prioritize **life-safety** and time-sensitive calls.  
- Keep a **cross-trained mutual aid roster** for surge events.  
- Log resource flows to avoid duplication.  
- Rotate exhausted pods off duty within 24–48 hours.

---

## When Not to Coordinate

- Active surveillance or infiltration risk.  
- Coordination reveals undocumented or targeted members.  
- Trust protocol gaps unresolved.  
Default to **containment over coordination** until safe.

---

## Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|
| Over-sharing data | Compartmentalize access |
| Surveillance | Rotate channels & devices |
| Resource depletion | Enforce rest and boundary policies |
| Trust shortcuts | Require slow verification |

<Callout type="warning">
Never merge databases across pods.  
Use decentralized, encrypted channels per [Digital Security Basics](/courses/digital-security-basics).
</Callout>
