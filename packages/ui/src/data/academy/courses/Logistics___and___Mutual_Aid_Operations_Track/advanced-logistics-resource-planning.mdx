## Overview

Long-term operations depend on logistics that **don’t collapse under pressure**.  
This course teaches how to distribute resources, forecast needs, rotate supplies, and manage transport under surveillance risk.  
The goal: a system that’s **redundant, discreet, and ethical**.

---

## Learn & Do

### Why Logistics Matter
Without resilient logistics, even the strongest pods will:
- Burn out under supply strain  
- Lose trust from resource failures  
- Expose caches or routes to hostile monitoring  

Pods thrive when logistics are treated as **care infrastructure**, not inventory.

---

### Quick Action Steps

1. Identify **3 regional staging points** for supplies across 2+ neighborhoods.  
2. Create a **rotation plan** (weekly for perishables, monthly for meds, quarterly for water).  
3. Build a **driver roster** with backups and day/night coverage → [Runners & Rides](/courses/runners-and-rides).  
4. Use **offline + encrypted logs** (paper + Tella or VeraCrypt).  
5. Test **alternate routes** monthly to prevent predictable patterns.

---

### Scaling Operations

Use a **Tiered Resource Model**:

| Tier | Focus | Example |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | On-person | Med kit, radio, snacks |
| 2 | Pod-level | 72-hour go-bag per member |
| 3 | Regional | Bulk caches (food, fuel, med) |

<Callout type="hint">
Always plan for **20% over expected demand** for high-risk supplies like fuel and meds.
</Callout>

---

### Storage & Distribution: The 3-2-1 Rule

- **3** separate cache sites  
- **2** different neighborhoods  
- **1** outside your main ops zone  

**Best practices:**
- Label generically (“Maintenance Supplies”)  
- Store in dark or temperature-stable areas  
- Assign **zone leads** for rotations → [Dispatch Coordination](/courses/dispatch-coordination)

**Rotation Schedule**
- Weekly → Perishables  
- Monthly → Fuel + Meds  
- Quarterly → Water, cache integrity check  

---

### Transportation Networks

**Principles:**
- Maintain driver redundancy  
- Mix vehicle types (vans, bikes, EVs)  
- Keep markings removable or generic  
- Train for **dead-drop protocols**  

If compromised:
1. Reassign to backup route  
2. Rotate vehicles  
3. File secure debrief → Admin or Safety Team  

<Callout type="info">
Bike couriers and runners remain the most resilient form of transport in surveillance-heavy areas.
</Callout>

---

### Risk Mitigation

| Threat | Response |
|---------|-----------|
| Repeated nearby unmarked vans | Relocate supplies, randomize delivery timing |
| Social media geotags leak locations | Re-train on metadata and digital hygiene |
| Predictable rotation cycles | Randomize schedule and handoff teams |

<Callout type="warning">
Static supply hubs = high visibility.  
Distribute, disguise, and rotate often.
</Callout>

---

### Worksheets & Tools

| Tool | Use |
|------|-----|
| Supply Calculator | People × Days × 1.2 buffer |
| Route Grid | Plan Primary / Alternate / Emergency paths |
| Storage Audit | Check signage, access, and plausible cover |
| Mesh Comms | Bridgefy, Meshtastic, or radio backup |
| Encryption | Tella, VeraCrypt, paper duplicate |

<Callout type="hint">
Use both **digital encryption** and **physical separation** for full redundancy.
</Callout>

---

### Practical Scenarios

- **Portland 2020:** Medics used bikes to bypass police checkpoints.  
- **Minnesota Freedom Fund:** Operated 3 separate cache chains to prevent seizure.  
- **Local pods:** Rotating every 90 days prevented tracking patterns.  

These examples prove that logistics isn’t about perfection — it’s about **adaptive systems**.

---

### Certification Drill

**Scenario:**  
Main storage loses power midwinter. Critical meds require refrigeration.

**Decide:**  
a) Move to backup site with cold storage  
b) Distribute to vetted homes temporarily  
c) Prioritize insulin and cold-chain meds for redistribution  

Then: Identify **3 alternate transport routes** if one vehicle is compromised.
