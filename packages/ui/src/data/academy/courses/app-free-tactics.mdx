# 🧭 App-Free Community Defense Tactics
<TrackBadge variant="default" />

<Callout type="info">
📵 Not everyone wants to — or can — use tech. This course trains you in **analogue coordination**, so you can build power and respond to threats **without phones, apps, or surveillance-prone tools**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="hint">
Pair with:
- <a href="/academy/bystander-support">Bystander Support</a> (filming ethics and de-escalation)

</Callout>

## 🧠 Why This Matters

Many experienced organizers:
- Don't carry phones during actions  
- Avoid Signal due to past infiltration or trauma  
- Don’t want to rely on platforms they can't audit  

Others may not have access to:
- Smartphones  
- Consistent internet  
- Secure private spaces to use tech  

🛠️ This course helps you create real-world, app-free workflows that still support ART goals — and make the network stronger.

---

## 🎯 What You’ll Learn

- How to join a pod without an app  
- How to coordinate dispatch and alerts using paper, runners, and visuals  
- How to sync across tech/no-tech lines without leaking data  
- What to carry for analogue outreach or defense  
- How to activate without revealing your identity or phone

## 🧰 The App-Free Toolkit

| Item | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| 📝 Pod contact sheet (Signal handles, not phones) | Sync with trusted members in-person only |
| 📜 KYR flyers + Pod starter guides | Recruitment + safety education |
| 🚶‍♂️ Human runners | Replace digital comms with real-time alerts |
| 📍 “Pod dropbox” | Shared location for notes, signups, updates |
| 🎒 Backpack CDC kit | Deploy mutual aid without tech |
| 🧭 Code phrases or hand signals | Trigger field action discreetly |

<Callout type="hint">
Use a **burner clipboard** or folder for physical info. Leave no identifying info behind.
</Callout>

## 🏗️ How to Join Without Tech

1. **Meet your pod in person**  
   - Mutual aid drop  
   - Outreach event  
   - Community Defense Center

2. **Exchange handles, not numbers**  
   - Share only Signal name / sticker pack  
   - Or create a pod name you can reference later

3. **Use a written intake sheet**  
   - “Alias”, city/region, interest level  
   - Mark if they prefer no phone contact

4. **Leave with:**
   - KYR pocket card  
   - Schedule of next in-person meetup  
   - Drop-in location if urgent support is needed
   
   Related: <a href="/academy/kyr-basics">KYR Basics</a>

## 🔁 Analogue Dispatch Models

### 📦 Static Dropboxes

- Waterproof bin or locker at a community center or business  
- Flyers, updates, sign-up sheets, written dispatch logs  
- Use lock/key or passphrase to access

### 🚶‍♀️ Runners

- Trained person walks or bikes between pods  
- Shares alerts by word of mouth or printed update  
- Should carry no phone, or a burner if needed

Coordinate hand-offs with <a href="/academy/dispatch-coordination">Dispatch Coordination</a> to avoid duplication.

### 🗣️ Shout Chains / Public Markers

- Use coded phrases, chalk marks, or small signage to signal nearby threat (e.g. “⚠️ ICE NEAR” or upside-down flyer)

## 🔐 Hybrid Pod Structure (Tech + No-Tech)

| Role Type      | Tool Use Allowed | Tasks |
|----------------|------------------|-------|
| 📱 Tech Podder | Signal + dispatch | Log threats, run Watch |
| 📵 App-Free Podder | No phone | Walk neighborhoods, hand out flyers, be visible |
| 🧭 Coordinator | Burner/airgapped device | Sync both types together |

⚠️ Each pod should designate **an analogue-first fallback method** in case all comms fail.

Learn more about fallback patterns: <a href="/academy/comms-collapse">Comms Collapse</a>, <a href="/academy/mesh-networks-intro">Mesh Networks Intro</a>

## 🧠 Safety Rules for No-Tech Ops

| Do | Don't |
|-----|-------|
| Use only first names or aliases | Collect full names or real numbers |
| Meet in public + safe locations | Linger in suspicious or unsafe areas |
| Use flyers with minimal contact info | Use full org names or addresses |
| Rotate physical materials weekly | Leave long-term records in dropboxes |
| Carry a backpack CDC kit | Use personal vehicles if you’re known |

See also: <a href="/academy/bystander-support">Bystander Support</a>

## 🧾 App-Free Alert Sample

**Visual Poster Format (for bulletin boards or mail drops):**

> 🚨 ICE PRESENCE REPORTED  
> **Area:** South Hill – 122nd St  
> **When:** Friday afternoon  
> **What to do:**  
> - Share KYR flyers  
> - Watch from distance  
> - Report verbally to trusted pod  
>  
> _No phones required. Check your local dropbox for updates._

## 🧪 Practice Scenario

🎯 _You’re walking your neighborhood and hear from a shopkeeper that ICE was just seen on South Tacoma Way._

✅ Action Plan:

1. Write it down: location, date, time, details  
2. Walk/bike to nearby pod contact or dropbox  
3. Verbally share with known pod or leave written note  
4. If runner or outreach team is nearby, hand off the update  
5. If you use a burner phone, **turn it off before and after use**

## 🧳 App-Free Deployment Pack

Every analogue pod member should carry:

- [ ] Folded flyers with KYR info  
- [ ] Printed pod sign-up forms  
- [ ] Sharpie + clipboard  
- [ ] Disposable gloves / hand sanitizer  
- [ ] Basic pod identity card (e.g. “ART Field Pod 7”)  
- [ ] Bandana or patch to signal role (if needed)  
- [ ] Printed instructions: what to do in case of ICE activity

## 💡 Remember

You don’t need a phone to show up.  
You don’t need to register to protect people.  
You don’t need permission to resist.

## Final Notes

- All tech systems should have **analogue fallback plans**
- Offline organizers must be **respected and supported** — not seen as less informed
- Community defense begins where the people are — and not all people are online
