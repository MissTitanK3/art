# 🗃️ After-Action Reviews & Data Hygiene
<TrackBadge variant="observation-legal" />
<TrackBadge variant="court-support" />

<Callout type="important">
⏱ **24-Hour Rule:** Begin debrief and data triage within **24 hours** of an action.  
Unreviewed data becomes a liability.
</Callout>

<Callout type="hint">

## Overview

Pods generate photos, videos, logs, and notes during actions.  
Mishandling this data can endanger members if devices are seized, hacked, or subpoenaed.  
Post-action reviews turn mistakes into training while protecting everyone involved.

> Debriefs build skill. Data hygiene preserves safety.

---

## Core Practices

### 1️⃣ Structured Debriefs

Hold a **blame-free debrief** within 24–48 hours.

**Agenda:**
1. Timeline Review – What happened, in order  
2. Gaps – What failed or confused?  
3. Successes – What worked?  
4. Action Items – Who follows up, by when  

**Note-Taking Rules:**
- Use role labels only (“Medic 1,” “Observer 2”).  
- Write on **paper** or **encrypted pads** (Signal Notes, VeraCrypt).  
- Destroy notes after 30 days unless legally required.  

<Callout type="info">
The goal is clarity, not confession.
</Callout>

---

### 2️⃣ Data Triage Flow

<Mermaid chart={`graph TD
A[Raw Data Collected] --> B{Needed for Legal?}
B -->|Yes| C[Encrypt & Transfer to Legal Team]
B -->|No| D{Training Value?}
D -->|Yes| E[Anonymize & Store Securely]
D -->|No| F[Wipe or Physically Destroy]
`} />

| Step | Action |
|------|---------|
| Encrypt early | Move all files off personal devices |
| Verify chain | Record who accessed what |
| Delete fast | 72-hour purge for non-essentials |

---

### 3️⃣ Secure Storage

| Data Type | Method | Retention |
|------------|---------|-----------|
| Video / photos | VeraCrypt USB + backup | 90 days max |
| Comms logs | Encrypted local copy only | 30 days |
| Medical notes | Paper in locked safe | 1 year (max) |

> Never sync to cloud or unverified drives.

---

## Risk Mitigation

| Threat | Response |
|--------|-----------|
| Device seizure | Use burner phones; enable auto-wipe |
| Subpoena for logs | Avoid cloud backups; use Signal or Matrix |
| Leak during debrief | Hold in person or encrypted call (Signal, Jitsi) |
| Habitual over-filming | Record only when essential for proof |

<Callout type="warning">
The safest data is **data never collected**.
</Callout>

## Tools & Templates

**Debrief One-Pager**
- 15 min: Timeline review  
- 15 min: 3 Wins / 3 Gaps  
- 10 min: Next steps  

**Data Hygiene Checklist**
- Day 0: Move all media to encrypted storage  
- Day 1: Anonymize faces, strip metadata  
- Day 3: Legal review + purge non-essentials  

**Chain-of-Custody Log**
| Field | Description |
|--------|--------------|
| Description | Item / data name |
| Collected By | Role or ID |
| Time / Location | Collection context |
| Storage Method | Where + how stored |
| Accessed By | Who opened it |
| Purpose | Legal / training / other |

## Certification Scenario

**Situation:**  
After a raid, your pod has 12 GB of footage, medical forms with names, and Signal logs.

**Tasks:**  
1. Apply the **Data Triage Flow**.  
2. Draft a short debrief agenda without naming individuals.  
3. Identify which files require chain-of-custody forms.
