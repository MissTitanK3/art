## Why It Matters

False ICE alerts or fake police presence drain resources, trigger panic, and erode trust.  
Disinformation often targets mutual aid and dispatch systems to sow confusion or surveillance exposure.

Effective response = fast, quiet verification and transparent correction without feeding the chaos.

## Verification Matrix

| Report Type | Minimum Verification | Tools / Methods |
|--------------|----------------------|-----------------|
| ICE Sightings | Visual proof + trusted witness | Encrypted image/video + timestamp |
| Police Presence | Two pods confirm independently | LoRa, radio, GPS |
| Viral Posts | Reverse search + time check | InVID, EXIF data, Google Lens |

**Golden Rule:** No alerts without two separate confirmations.

<Callout type="hint">
System limitations:
- Video = secure link only, not upload.  
- No map geofences — use zone labels.  
- Logs exist for accountability; no full audit export.  
- Notifications are region-scoped.  
- Restrict verification notes to trusted access roles.
</Callout>

## Spotting Disinformation

### Bot / Troll Indicators
- Generic usernames, repetitive phrasing  
- Burst posting or off-timezone activity  

### Narrative Flags
- Fear or outrage framing  
- Contradictory or unverifiable “eyewitness” stories  
- Attempts to discredit pods or leadership  

### Deepfake or Edit Clues
- Lip-sync mismatch, distorted shadows  
- Metadata date/location mismatch  
- Pixelated overlay around focus areas  

---

## Counter-Messaging Protocol

1. **Level 1 — Quiet Correction**  
   Notify trusted pods; correct internally without public notice.  
2. **Level 2 — Neutral Public Statement**  
   > “No verified reports of [incident] at this time. Verification ongoing.”  
3. **Level 3 — Escalated Response**  
   Admins coordinate formal correction or public release.

Never call out individuals publicly unless cleared by leadership and legally reviewed.

---

## Secure Information Sharing

- Don’t share pod movements, verification methods, or times.  
- Use encrypted backchannels (Signal, Matrix, PGP).  
- Rotate who verifies and how, to break patterns.  

<Callout type="warning">
Store only temporary verification data. Delete raw files after review.  
Always get consent before using others’ images or stories.
</Callout>

## Simulation Drill

**Scenario:** Viral post claims a police checkpoint on Main Street.  
1. Verify with ground teams.  
2. Reverse-search image, validate time.  
3. Determine proper response level.  
4. Draft neutral update or flag false.

---

## Quick Action Steps

- Always double-verify before alerting.  
- Keep language neutral and factual.  
- Rotate verification shifts during peak flow.  
- Escalate only with confirmed intel.

---

## Risks & Red Lines

- Never forward unverified reports — even privately.  
- Never confirm operations while denying rumors.  
- Avoid predictable patterns in how you verify or message.  

<Callout type="important">
Operational secrecy protects everyone.  
Verification is collective defense — not speculation.
</Callout>
