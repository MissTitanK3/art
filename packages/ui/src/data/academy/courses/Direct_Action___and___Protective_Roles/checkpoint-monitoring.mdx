# 🚧 Checkpoint Monitoring
<TrackBadge variant="field-safety" />
<TrackBadge variant="observation-legal" />

<Callout type="info">
This course prepares you to **witness, document, and report** checkpoint presence while protecting yourself and others.  
Operate legally, minimize confrontation, and prioritize consent and safety.
</Callout>

---

## Overview
Checkpoint monitoring is solidarity work that exposes profiling and abuse while maintaining calm, legal observation.  
You’ll learn how to:
- Identify checkpoints and plan safely  
- Document without interfering  
- Handle interactions professionally  
- Report findings securely and ethically  

---

## Learn & Do

### 1. Understanding Checkpoints
Checkpoints are official stops where police or immigration officers question travelers or inspect vehicles.  
Common types:
- Border Patrol highway checkpoints  
- DUI or license checkpoints  
- Joint task force stops (ICE + local police)  

> 🎯 Your purpose: **observe, document, and deter harm—not intervene.**

---

### 2. Legal Basics

| Action | Legal? | Guidance |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Record from public space | ✅ Yes | Stay off roadway, don’t block movement |
| Cross checkpoint line | ❌ No | Never enter controlled zones |
| Speak to detainees | ⚠️ Caution | Only if approached voluntarily |
| Livestream on site | ⚠️ Avoid | Upload later, scrub metadata |

<Callout type="warning">
Laws vary by state. Confirm local recording distances or observer restrictions before deployment.
</Callout>

---

### 3. Preparation Checklist

| Item | Purpose |
|------|----------|
| Camera or phone | Record safely from public area |
| Notebook | Log events, times, and details |
| Partner | Witness and backup |
| KYR flyers | Share with civilians after, not during |
| De-escalation training | Manage public or officer engagement |

✅ Disable face/fingerprint unlock  
✅ Preload legal hotline or dispatch contact  
✅ Keep devices charged and encrypted  

<Callout type="info">
For device hardening and metadata safety, review <a href="/courses/digital-security-basics">Digital Security Basics</a>.
</Callout>

### 4. Safe Positioning
- Remain **on public property**—sidewalk or easement  
- Avoid standing behind or beside vehicles  
- Stay visible but neutral  
- Work in pairs with **nonverbal signals:**  
  👍 OK 🟨 Move 👎 Disengage  

If separated, regroup at a predetermined point.

<Callout type="warning">
Never film inside checkpoints or block traffic. The goal is observation, not confrontation.
</Callout>

---

### 5. What to Document
Record:
1. **Time & Location** — GPS, intersection, start/end.  
2. **Agencies** — ICE, CBP, local police, markings.  
3. **Vehicles** — Type, number, who’s stopped.  
4. **Actions** — Searches, tone, verbal exchanges.  
5. **Bias or Abuse** — Profiling, harassment, use of force.

<Callout type="hint">
Blur faces and plates before sharing. Use ObscuraCam or secure blur tools.
</Callout>

---

### 6. Interacting with Officers or Civilians
**If approached by officers:**
> “I’m a legal observer on public property.”  
Stay calm, record, and do not argue.

**If civilians approach:**
- Offer KYR flyers, not advice.  
- Never tell someone to flee.  
- Direct to legal aid or dispatch support.  

---

### 7. Red Flags and Response

| Situation | Recommended Action |
|------------|--------------------|
| Officer questions you | Film, answer minimally, disengage |
| Camera removed/confiscated | Note time, description, badge |
| Person detained | Record details, inform dispatch |
| Aggressive bystanders | Withdraw calmly, regroup |
| Media arrives | Step back or coordinate quietly |

---

### 8. After Monitoring
1. Upload footage to secure, encrypted storage.  
2. Label clearly (e.g., `2025-06-22_Tucson_ICE_Stop.mp4`).  
3. Remove all metadata before sharing.  
4. File report internally with trusted leads.  

If incident includes detentions:
- Coordinate with [Court Support](/courses/court-support)  
- Connect with [Legal Follow-through & Jail Support](/courses/legal-followthrough-jail-support)
