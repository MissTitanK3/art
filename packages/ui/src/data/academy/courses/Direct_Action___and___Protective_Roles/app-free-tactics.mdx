# 🧭 App-Free Community Defense Tactics
<TrackBadge variant="default" />

<Callout type="info">
This course teaches **analogue coordination** for mutual aid and defense when phones or apps aren’t safe or available.  
Build community systems that stay effective even when tech fails or surveillance rises.
</Callout>

---

## Overview
Not everyone has access to secure digital tools. Some refuse them for safety, privacy, or principle.  
This module trains you to:
- Operate fully offline while maintaining coordination and safety  
- Use low-tech tools like paper, runners, and coded visuals  
- Blend analogue and digital pods without exposing personal data  

App-free infrastructure keeps movements functional when networks collapse or repression spikes.

---

## Learn & Do

### 1. Why App-Free Tactics Matter
Phones and apps fail through:
- **Surveillance:** metadata, location, infiltration  
- **Access gaps:** no devices, power, or connectivity  
- **Burnout:** tech dependence or digital trauma  

Offline systems protect participation and autonomy.

---

### 2. The Analogue Toolkit

| Item | Use |
|------|-----|
| 📝 Contact sheet (aliases only) | Link pods safely offline |
| 📜 KYR & safety flyers | Outreach and education |
| 🚶 Human runners | Hand-carry alerts and updates |
| 📍 Dropboxes | Exchange written reports or rosters |
| 🎒 Field backpack | Basic aid, maps, flyers, tools |
| 🧭 Hand signals / code phrases | Non-verbal coordination |

<Callout type="hint">
Label materials by alias, not name. Rotate or destroy old lists regularly.
</Callout>

---

### 3. Joining a Pod Without Tech
1. Meet in person at mutual aid or defense events.  
2. Share only **Signal handles or pseudonyms**, never phone numbers.  
3. Fill a written intake: alias, city, skill area, contact preference.  
4. Leave with printed KYR info, next meeting time, and fallback site.

---

### 4. Analogue Dispatch Models

**Static Dropboxes:**  
Weatherproof bins for flyers, logs, or alerts.  
Locked, rotated, and cleared weekly.

**Runners:**  
Trusted walkers or cyclists who deliver notes or warnings.  
Carry no personal data; may use burners if needed.

**Visual Codes:**  
Pre-set marks, chalk, or signage for nearby alerts.  
Example: triangle = meeting safe; circle = ICE presence.

<Callout type="warning">
Never post physical markers on private property. Keep codes simple and deniable.
</Callout>

---

### 5. Hybrid Pod Structure

| Role | Tool Use | Function |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Tech Podder | Signal / Dispatch | Log reports, relay alerts |
| App-Free Podder | No device | Local visibility & outreach |
| Coordinator | Burner / offline laptop | Sync both groups safely |

Each pod needs a **fallback method** (runner, radio, or dropbox) for total outage.

---

### 6. Safety Rules

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| Use aliases only | Collect real names |
| Meet publicly | Share home addresses |
| Use minimal-info flyers | Include full org contacts |
| Rotate paper weekly | Store long-term records |
| Carry basic CDC kit | Use personal IDs at ops |

Related: [Bystander Support](/courses/bystander-support)

### 7. Analogue Alert Example

> 🚨 **Community Alert**  
> **Area:** Hilltop / 23rd Ave  
> **When:** Friday afternoon  
> **Action:**  
> - Share KYR flyers  
> - Stay visible, observe safely  
> - Report verbally to local pod  

No phone required. Updates available at nearest dropbox.

---

### 8. Practice Scenario
_You hear ICE was seen on South Tacoma Way._

✅ Do:
1. Record details on paper.  
2. Deliver note to pod dropbox or runner.  
3. Verbally brief trusted contact.  
4. Power off any burner device after use.

Learn more: [Runners & Rides](/courses/runners-and-rides), [Responding to Dispatch Calls](/courses/responding-to-dispatch-calls)
