# 🚙 Vehicle Specialist Micro-Badge
<TrackBadge variant="logistics" />
<TrackBadge variant="field-safety" />

<Callout type="info">
This **Micro-Badge** certifies volunteers as **vehicle operators, maintainers, and logistics coordinators** for pods.  
You are responsible for **safe transport, reliable staging, and discreet movement of people and supplies** in field or mutual aid operations.
</Callout>

---

## Why It Matters

Pods depend on reliable vehicles for **supply lines, staging, and safe evacuation**.  
A trained Vehicle Specialist prevents breakdowns, avoids traps, and supports **rapid, secure mobility** under unpredictable conditions.

---

## Core Responsibilities

| Focus | Objective |
|--------|------------|
| **Vehicle Readiness** | Maintain clean, functional, stocked vehicles |
| **Route Planning** | Identify safe, redundant paths with rally points |
| **Passenger & Cargo Safety** | Protect occupants and sensitive supplies |
| **Secure Coordination** | Operate quietly with encrypted communication |

---

## Vehicle Prep & Maintenance

**Daily Inspection**
- Tires, fluids, lights, brakes  
- Fuel and coolant levels topped  
- Interior clear of identifiers or personal items  

**Emergency Kit (required)**
- First-aid supplies  
- Window breaker + seatbelt cutter  
- Jumper cables, tire iron, jack  
- Blanket, water, flashlight  
- Fire extinguisher and basic tools  

<Callout type="hint">
Keep vehicle logs minimal. Do not store personal names or routes in writing.  
See <a href="/courses/documentation-safety">Documentation & Safety</a>.
</Callout>

---

## Route Planning & Threat Awareness

**Threat Factors**
- Police stations or checkpoints nearby  
- License plate reader zones  
- One-way choke points or bridges  

**Tools**
- Offline maps (downloaded GPS)  
- Secure route sharing through encrypted messenger  
- Visual map codes (no names or addresses)  

**Pre-Deployment Prep**
1. Plan **two alternate routes** minimum.  
2. Identify **safe rally points** and exits.  
3. Confirm **dispatch check-ins** before launch.  

---

## Specialized Transport Protocols

**Medical Evacuation**
- Park nose-out for rapid departure.  
- Load patient feet-first (airway visible).  
- Use smoothest route avoiding enforcement zones.  

**Sensitive Cargo**
- Conceal contents.  
- Split across multiple vehicles if possible.  
- Exchange only at **secure, low-visibility sites**.

---

## Security & Contingencies

- Rotate routes and departure times.  
- Use unmarked “clean” vehicles.  
- Watch mirrors for repeated tails.  
- Keep burner phone in glove box for dispatch only.  

**If Compromised**
- Abort delivery; contact dispatch using coded phrase.  
- Divert to rally point or pre-set safe zone.  
- Leave vehicle only if life is at risk.

<Callout type="warning">
Never lead a tail to a staging or aid location.
</Callout>

## Convoy & Coordination Basics

- Use minimal radio chatter; confirm positions by signal.  
- Keep **2–3 car lengths** spacing.  
- Stagger headlights if operating at night.  
- Confirm everyone’s **rally code and fallback order** before rollout.

---

## Quick Action Steps

1. Keep all documents current (license, registration, insurance).  
2. Check vehicle readiness before each shift.  
3. Pre-plan three routes: **primary, alternate, emergency**.  
4. Use encrypted channels for dispatch and logistics.  
5. Debrief after each run for improvements.

---

## Risks & Red Lines

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|
| Overexposure or tailing | Change timing and route regularly |
| Vehicle failure | Maintain daily inspection log |
| Overloading | Stay within manufacturer weight limits |
| Location leaks | Use secure, short-term comms only |

---

## Scenario Drill

**Situation:**  
You’re on a resupply when a tail appears near a checkpoint.

**Response:**  
1. Take alternate route B.  
2. Signal dispatch with code phrase “Blue Two.”  
3. Divert to quiet location and assess.  
4. Transfer supplies only under cover or to verified contact.

## Certification Checklist

- [ ] Can perform basic maintenance and emergency repairs.  
- [ ] Knows two backup routes and rally points per operation.  
- [ ] Maintains stocked emergency kit.  
- [ ] Completes at least one field simulation or convoy drill.
