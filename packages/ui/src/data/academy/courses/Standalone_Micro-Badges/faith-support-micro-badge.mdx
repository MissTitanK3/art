# 🙏 Spiritual & Moral Support Micro-Badge
<TrackBadge variant="community-care" />
<TrackBadge variant="court-support" />

<Callout type="info">
This **Micro-Badge** certifies volunteers as **non-denominational spiritual or moral support providers** for pods.  
You offer **grounding, comfort, and steady presence** during actions and recovery — **without preaching or pressure**.  
No religious credentialing required, only **trust, empathy, and calm**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="hint">
If language access is needed, coordinate with <a href="/courses/translator-micro-badge">Translator Micro-Badge</a> or <a href="/courses/asl-interpreter-micro-badge">ASL Interpreter Micro-Badge</a>.
</Callout>

---

## Why It Matters

During high-stress or traumatic moments, people often need **spiritual or moral grounding** — not answers, just presence.  
You provide **comfort and calm** without taking control. This role sustains trust and collective focus in chaos.

---

## What This Role Is (and Is Not)

✅ **This Role Involves:**
- Guiding **simple grounding** (breathwork, silence, song, optional prayer)  
- Listening without judgment  
- Offering presence in debriefs or healing circles  

❌ **This Role Does Not Include:**
- Proselytizing or evangelizing  
- Replacing therapy or mental health care  
- Mediating violent conflict  

<Callout type="info">
Support must remain **consent-based and data-safe**.  
Follow <a href="/courses/community-consent">Community Consent</a>, <a href="/courses/data-ethics-pledge">Data Ethics Pledge</a>, <a href="/courses/documentation-safety">Documentation & Safety</a>, and <a href="/courses/after-action-data-hygiene">After-Action Data Hygiene</a>.
</Callout>

## Core Skills

| Skill | Description |
|--------|--------------|
| **Grounding Practices** | Short, repeatable calm techniques (breathing, silence, rhythm) |
| **Consent Language** | Ask permission before touch or prayer |
| **Non-Denominational Framing** | Avoid faith labels unless invited |
| **Emotional Regulation** | Stay grounded under others’ distress |

---

## Sample Grounding Scripts

> “Would you like a moment of silence or grounding together?”  
> “I’m here if you want to talk or just sit quietly.”  
> “Would breathwork, stillness, or prayer help right now?”

Keep tone low, slow, and invitational.  
Never interpret silence as rejection — it may be relief.

---

## Quick Action Protocol

1. Identify yourself as **neutral support**.  
2. Carry **small grounding aids** (water, fidget tool, calming scent, notebook).  
3. Wear **neutral, non-religious markers** (plain purple band, small badge).  
4. Get **verbal or visual consent** before engaging.  
5. Stay through **stabilization or proper handoff**.

## Common Risks

| Risk | Prevention |
|------|-------------|
| Over-spiritualizing distress | Keep focus on calm, not meaning |
| Triggering trauma with faith talk | Ask first, avoid “should” language |
| Becoming a symbolic target | Dress neutrally; fade into team rhythm |
| Emotional burnout | Debrief, rest, and maintain boundaries |

<Callout type="warning">
If your presence increases tension, step back.  
Coordinate with <a href="/courses/deescalation-basics">De-escalation Basics</a> or <a href="/courses/bystander-support">Bystander Support</a>.
</Callout>

## Case Example

**Scenario:**  
At a vigil, a participant begins hyperventilating.

**Response:**  
1. Ask permission: “Would you like to breathe together for a moment?”  
2. Mirror slow breathing; maintain quiet eye contact.  
3. Stay close until they re-center.  
4. Signal for Care Support if symptoms persist.

## Certification Checklist

- [ ] Can lead **grounding practices** (silence, breathwork, song, optional prayer).  
- [ ] Offers support **without imposing belief**.  
- [ ] Steps back when escalation occurs.  
- [ ] Participates in **after-action care** sessions.  
- [ ] Has reviewed at least one **trauma-informed resource** (e.g., *Trauma Stewardship*).  

<Callout type="hint">
Badge renewal required annually to maintain trauma-informed practice.
</Callout>
