# 🛡️ Survivor Escort Micro-Badge
<TrackBadge variant="court-support" />
<TrackBadge variant="community-care" />

<Callout type="info">
This **Micro-Badge** certifies volunteers to **escort and support survivors or witnesses** through legal spaces, court hearings, and high-stress public environments.  
Your goal: protect calm, consent, and safety — not to intervene or speak for the survivor.
</Callout>

---

## Why It Matters

Legal systems and public institutions can retraumatize.  
A trained escort ensures survivors move **confidently, safely, and without unnecessary exposure** while maintaining their agency and dignity.

---

## Core Responsibilities

| Focus | Practice |
|--------|-----------|
| **Soft Navigation** | Move quietly through checkpoints and halls; avoid visibility. |
| **Trauma-Informed Support** | Monitor stress signals; ground before escalation. |
| **Consent First** | Always ask before touch, movement, or conversation. |
| **Legal Coordination** | Communicate only through authorized counsel. |

---

## Trauma-Informed Practices

**Grounding Phrases**
> “Would you like me beside or behind you?”  
> “We can stop or leave at any time.”  

**Non-Verbal Cues**
- Nods, hand squeezes, or code phrases signal distress.  
- Pre-set exit words avoid confusion in tense spaces.  

**Regulation**
- Speak slowly, breathe evenly.  
- Maintain clear posture — not protective, not passive.  

<Callout type="warning">
Never initiate touch or step between parties without explicit consent or immediate safety risk.
</Callout>

---

## Courthouse Navigation

**Before Entry**
- Check restricted items (phones, metal tools, sprays).  
- Identify bathrooms, quiet zones, and exits.  
- Confirm **legal team rendezvous point**.  

**During Escort**
- Keep body angle toward exits.  
- Stay steady, avoid rushing.  
- Limit chatter; let silence work.  

**After Hearing**
- Guide to decompression area.  
- Offer water, grounding aid, or quiet reflection.  
- Debrief briefly with survivor and, if consented, legal staff.  

---

## Threat & Stress Response

1. **Spot:** Tremors, blank stare, fast breathing.  
2. **Ground:** Use the 5-4-3-2-1 sensory scan or counting breaths.  
3. **Buffer:** Place yourself between threat and survivor if safe.  
4. **Exit:** Use pre-planned phrase to withdraw discreetly.  
5. **Report:** Notify lead or counsel of any incident immediately.

## Quick Action Steps

1. Gain **verbal consent** for all guidance.  
2. Keep **route maps, exit plans, and calm spaces** ready.  
3. Coordinate with **legal or court support pods**.  
4. Maintain composure regardless of confrontation.  
5. Log incidents discreetly after debrief.  

---

## Risks & Red Lines

| Risk | Boundary |
|------|-----------|
| Overstepping autonomy | Ask before acting |
| Public visibility | Keep low profile |
| Media contact | Decline comment; redirect to counsel |
| Sharing details | Confidentiality absolute |

<Callout type="info">
If you are approached by press or opposing counsel, state:  
> “I’m a volunteer escort. All inquiries go through the legal representative.”
</Callout>

---

## Scenario Drill

**Situation:** Opposing party nears survivor after verdict.  
**Response:**  
1. Step calmly between.  
2. Ground: “We can leave when you’re ready.”  
3. Guide to safe space or exit.  
4. Notify legal contact quietly.

---

## Certification Checklist

- [ ] Escorts survivors/witnesses with calm, privacy, and consent.  
- [ ] Understands courthouse layout and de-escalation paths.  
- [ ] Demonstrates trauma-informed, non-directive support.  
- [ ] Executes exit plans and post-escort debriefs.  
- [ ] Respects legal confidentiality at all times.
