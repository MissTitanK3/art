# 🔐 Admin Tools and Oversight
<TrackBadge variant="pod-leadership" />

Admin-level dispatchers are trusted with sensitive tools and major responsibilities. This module covers managing dispatches, adjusting access roles, recording trust signatures, coordinating coverage, and maintaining accountability through activity reviews.

---

## Core Admin Permissions (today)

Level 4 (Admin) users can:

- ✅ **Create and edit dispatches**
- 🔐 **Adjust user access roles** (e.g., promote to Verified/Admin Dispatcher)
- 🤝 **Record trust signatures** (document who vouched for whom and when)
- �️ **Review recent activity** (dispatch updates, approvals)
- 🧭 **Coordinate coverage** (assign leads/areas; label a coordinator’s zone)
- 📋 **Approve or remove reports** (if your region enables admin moderation)

What you cannot do in-app yet (use policy or server-side workflows instead):

- ⛔ A full, in-app **audit log viewer** for every action (server logs only)
- ⛔ One-click **CSV export** of all records (use Supabase admin or request a limited export)
- ⛔ Map-drawn **geofence radii** as “zones” (use coordination labels/shifts instead)

---

## Creating & Editing Dispatches

Admins can manually initiate new dispatches, bypassing normal triage. This is allowed only when:

- The situation is **urgent** (active raid, medical crisis, imminent threat)
- Another dispatcher requests backup
- A trusted source confirms verified intel

### Manual Dispatch Tips

- Use precise **location data** (cross streets, GPS if safe)
- Clearly flag **urgency and timing**
- Add **internal notes** (visible to trusted roles only)
- Avoid overuse; these tools should not replace collective triage

<Callout type="important">
Always follow up with the responding dispatcher to confirm closure, updates, and after-action notes.
</Callout>

## Access Roles and Trust Signatures

Dispatch access is controlled by an access role set on a user’s profile (region-specific). Common roles include:

- Dispatcher (basic)
- Verified Dispatcher (can coordinate and assign)
- Admin Dispatcher (full coordination + admin tools)

In addition to access roles, Admins can record “trust signatures” that document a vouch (who vouched, for whom, in what capacity). A trust signature:

- Does not automatically change someone’s access role
- Creates a transparent trail of endorsements for accountability

Promotions should meet prerequisites agreed by your region:

- **Level 1+**: Completed Response Training  
- **Level 3**: At least 3 months of activity, Dispatch Certification, 2 peer referrals  
- **Level 4**: Invitation by core team, conflict resolution training

<Callout type="warning">
Never promote someone as a favor or without full review.  
Record a trust signature and **log the promotion reason** (e.g., “Certified + crisis leadership experience”).
</Callout>

## Coordinating Coverage (labels, not geofences)

Coverage is coordinated through assignments and labels, not map-drawn geofences. Today you can:

- 🌐 Label a dispatcher’s “coordination zone” (e.g., Neighborhood/City)
- 📍 Assign temporary coverage for marches, vigils, or emergency deployments
- 🧭 Track shifts/coverage windows in scheduling tools (if your region enables them)

Best practices:

- Avoid overlapping coverage without coordination — designate a **primary lead and backup**  
- Tag coverage for **language capacity** (e.g., "Spanish-speaking leads available")  
- Reduce late-night paging unless critical; pre-plan quiet hours and escalation paths

---

## Report Approvals and Removal

Admins can:

- Review flagged or duplicate reports  
- Approve valid submissions  
- Remove spam, harmful, or false data

Always:

- Document **why a report was rejected** in the audit log  
- Use **transparent reasoning** to maintain trust  
- Refer disputes to the **Accountability Team** (never resolve alone if contested)

---

## Activity Review, Exports, and Audits

From the Admin Dashboard and server logs, you can:

- 🕓 Review recent activity (dispatch updates, approvals, role changes recorded by admins)
- 🔍 Conduct periodic audits of major responses to identify gaps
- 📊 Request limited exports via a server-side workflow (no in-app CSV export yet)

<Callout type="warning">
Exports can be subpoenaed. Do not include names, addresses, or sensitive identifiers in exported notes. Prefer aggregated stats and redact PII.
</Callout>

---

## Compromised Accounts

If you suspect a breach:

1. Revoke access immediately (via Admin Panel)  
2. Notify the core team and affected leads  
3. Audit recent logs for misuse  
4. Reset passwords
5. File a brief report in the Admin SOS channel (Signal)

---

## Accountability & Community Standards

Admin roles are about stewardship, not control. You must:

- Avoid editing dispatches except for accuracy or emergency reasons  
- Never promote or demote without full review  
- Join the **Monthly Admin Sync** to share updates and resolve tool issues  
- Report tool bugs or access friction via [this form](/admin-feedback)

---

## Summary

Admins hold the keys to critical infrastructure. Use this access to **support your teams, protect the network, and uphold trust.**

<Callout type="success">
Admins aren’t bosses — they’re anchors.  
Lead with clarity, transparency, and care.
</Callout>
