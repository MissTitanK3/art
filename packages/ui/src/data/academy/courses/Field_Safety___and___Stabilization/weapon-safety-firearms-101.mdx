# 🔫 Weapon Safety & Firearms 101

<TrackBadge variant="direct-action" />

<Callout type="critical">
**Never point a weapon at anything you do not intend to destroy.**  
This course teaches recognition, distance, and safe-handling literacy — not combat or tactical training.
</Callout>

---

## Overview

Mutual-aid and field pods sometimes encounter firearms in tense environments.  
This module provides foundational literacy to:
- Identify weapons safely  
- Reduce panic or mishandling  
- Understand legal limits  
- Build collective protocols for risk reduction  

The goal is **incident prevention and calm response**, not engagement or use.

---

## Learn & Do

### 🧠 Universal Safety Rules

1. Treat every firearm as loaded.  
2. Keep your finger off the trigger until ready to fire.  
3. Never aim at anything you don’t intend to destroy.  
4. Be sure of your target and what’s beyond it.  

Repetition under calm conditions prevents dangerous reflexes under stress.

---

### 🔐 Safe Handling & Storage

- Verify chamber clear before transfer.  
- Use safeties and chamber flags.  
- Store ammunition separately.  
- Lock firearms during transport.  
- Maintain a pod **Safety Officer** log for each transfer.  

> Familiarity reduces fear — but **responsibility prevents harm.**

---

### 🕵️ Recognizing Firearms in the Field

| Type | Typical Range | Common Use |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| Handgun | 5–25 m | Personal defense, law enforcement |
| Rifle | 50–400 m | Hunting, long-range threat |
| Shotgun | 5–50 m | Riot control, close defense |

If weapons are observed:
- Count how many, note type, bearing, and distance  
- Stay calm, do **not** confront  
- Report to dispatcher or Safety Marshal  

<Callout type="hint">
Train to recognize without touching. Observation is a defensive skill, not an invitation to handle.
</Callout>

---

### ⚖️ Legal & Ethical Boundaries

- Firearm laws vary by state — review statutes before handling.  
- Never accept or carry if unlicensed or prohibited.  
- No open carry within aid or community zones.  
- Report violations discreetly — **never post images online**.  

<Callout type="warning">
Safety literacy ≠ authorization.  
Only licensed individuals may provide lawful armed security where permitted.
</Callout>

---

### 🕊 De-escalation Around Armed Individuals

1. Keep hands visible and posture calm.  
2. Maintain at least 6 feet of distance.  
3. Use neutral language: “We’re stepping back for safety.”  
4. If escalation occurs, disengage and notify dispatch.  

<Callout type="info">
For deeper practice, see [De-escalation Basics](/courses/deescalation-basics) and [Field Safety](/courses/field-safety).
</Callout>

---

### 🧰 Field Integration & Drills

Pods near potential weapons should:
- Assign a **Safety Marshal** for every 5–10 volunteers  
- Conduct **weapon-sighting drills** (spot → cover → communicate → report)  
- Carry trauma kits with tourniquets and hemostatic gauze  
- Review calm-response protocols monthly  

---

### 🧳 Gear Checklist

- Eye and ear protection  
- Chamber flags and locks  
- Trauma kit (CAT tourniquet + pressure dressing)  
- Field notebook for logs  
- Laminated summary of local firearm laws
