# 🧨 Burner Phones & Security Culture (2025 Edition)
<TrackBadge variant="tech-comms" />
<TrackBadge variant="movement-strategy" />

<Callout type="info">
This course covers how to **choose, set up, use, and dispose of burner phones safely**, while embedding them in a **security culture** that includes physical OPSEC, digital hygiene, and disciplined community norms.
</Callout>

<Callout type="important">
Burners are a tool, not a cloak. Carriers can correlate devices by towers, timing, and co-travel with your primary phone. Treat burners as a way to <strong>reduce</strong> exposure, not eliminate it. Pair this with <a href="/academy/digital-security-basics">Digital Security Basics</a> and <a href="/academy/app-free-tactics">App‑Free Tactics</a>.
</Callout>

---

## Why It Matters

Burner phones **limit exposure** if confiscated, hacked, or tracked.  
They support **operational security** through compartmentalization, anonymity, and metadata hygiene.  
But they are **not foolproof** — they only work when integrated into a culture of **discipline, threat modeling, and secure routines**.

---

## Fundamentals: What & Why

- **Burner phones** are prepaid devices bought anonymously, stripped of personal accounts, and used only for a specific window or operation.  
- Use cases:
  - High-risk protests where tracking is likely.  
  - Cross-border travel or heavily surveilled areas.  
  - Temporary communication channels separate from primary devices.

---

## Burner Lifecycle & Acquisition

1. **Buy anonymously** (cash only, avoid loyalty cards or linked locations).  
2. **Setup securely:**
  - Remove biometrics (fingerprint, face unlock).  
  - Enable a strong passcode and full-device encryption.  
  - Disable location services, advertising IDs, and background app refresh.  
  - Turn off Wi‑Fi/Bluetooth scanning and Nearby/auto-connection features.  
  - Disable cloud backups/sync.
3. **Install minimal apps only** (e.g., Signal), no personal logins or cloud sync.  
4. **Use for one operation**, then **wipe or physically destroy** — never reuse burners for new actions.

---

## Integration into Security Culture

- Keep burners **off and in airplane mode** when not in active use.  
- Avoid **co-travel correlation**: don’t carry a burner powered on alongside a personal phone; ideally leave your personal device powered off and elsewhere.  
- **Strip metadata** from all images and files before sharing (ObscuraCam, Scrambled EXIF). See <a href="/academy/documentation-safety">Documentation Safety</a>.  
- Pair burners with **burner SIMs and numbers** — no crossover with personal devices.  
- Consider **app-free backup channels** (meet points, paper codes, radio) from <a href="/academy/app-free-tactics">App‑Free Tactics</a> and off‑grid comms (<a href="/academy/mesh-networks-intro">Mesh Networks Intro</a>, <a href="/academy/comms-collapse">Comms Collapse</a>).  
- **Debrief after use** to update threat modeling and refine protocols — see <a href="/academy/after-action-data-hygiene">After‑Action Data Hygiene</a>.

---

## Threat Modeling & Red Flags

- Burner phones can **draw attention** at borders or checkpoints (anomaly factor).  
- Always combine with **clean car principles, rotating routes, and unlinked identities**.  
- Know the **limits of burners** — if seized, assume all contents are compromised.

<Callout type="warning">
Device identifiers (IMEI/MEID) and SIM identifiers (IMSI/ICCID) are unique. Purchasing or activating SIMs with ID, credit card, or known addresses links them to you. Even if you never log in to personal accounts, <strong>co-location</strong> with your everyday phone can create a connection.
</Callout>

---

## Ethical & Community Norms

- Burners should **never leak private info** about others.  
- Share **best practices and refresher trainings** regularly to keep pods aligned.  
- Revisit zines and guides annually to stay updated with evolving threats and tools.

<Callout type="hint">
Know your rights when questioned about devices and passwords. See <a href="/academy/kyr-basics">Know Your Rights (KYR) Basics</a>.
</Callout>

---

## Burner Phone Checklist

- [ ] Purchased anonymously with cash.  
- [ ] SIM purchased for cash; avoid registered/ID‑linked activations.  
- [ ] Biometrics removed, strong passcode enabled.  
- [ ] Device encrypted, location services disabled.  
- [ ] Wi‑Fi/Bluetooth scanning and Nearby features disabled.  
- [ ] Minimal apps installed (secure messaging only).  
- [ ] Stored in airplane mode until deployment.  
- [ ] Metadata scrubbed before sharing media.  
- [ ] No co-travel with your primary phone during use.  
- [ ] Cloud backups/sync disabled; ad ID reset.  
- [ ] Used once, then wiped or destroyed.  
- [ ] Logged in debrief for OPSEC review.
