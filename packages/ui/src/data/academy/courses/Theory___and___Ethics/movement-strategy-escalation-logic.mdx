# 🗺️ Movement Strategy & Escalation Logic
<TrackBadge variant="movement-strategy" />
<TrackBadge variant="direct-action" />

<Callout type="info">
Use clear, approachable language. Keep steps small and predictable.  
This course teaches pods how to **decide when to escalate, de-escalate, or withdraw** so actions stay aligned with strategy, community safety, and consent — not adrenaline.
</Callout>

---

## Overview
Escalation without purpose or preparation can **burn out pods**, invite repression, and damage trust.  
Escalation done with clear goals and consent can **shift power, protect communities, and win visibility**.  
This course builds shared frameworks for tactical discipline, collective consent, and risk-aware action planning.

---

## Learn & Do

### 1. Risk & Impact Analysis (STAR Framework)
Use the **STAR** method before every escalation:
- **Safety:** Assess physical and legal risks for all involved.  
- **Timing:** Is the political or media moment favorable?  
- **Alignment:** Does this serve the community’s goals?  
- **Resources:** Can your pod handle potential fallout (legal, financial, emotional)?

**Escalation Matrix**

| Tactic | Visibility | Risk | Preparation Needed |
|---------|-------------|------|--------------------|
| Sit-in | High | Medium | Legal briefing & medics |
| Banner Drop | Low | Low | Scout safe anchor points |
| Road Blockade | Very High | High | Arrest support & logistics |
| Vigil or March | Medium | Low | Route or permit coordination |

---

### 2. Consent & Collective Buy-In
**Ladder of Engagement**
1. **Inform:** Everyone knows the plan and potential risks.  
2. **Consult:** Gather feedback before finalizing.  
3. **Co-Create:** Shape the plan collaboratively when possible.  
4. **Lead:** Only pre-consenting members take higher-risk roles.

**Red Lines**
Pre-define limits before actions:
- Police escalation past a set threshold  
- Any serious injury or medical emergency  
- Media narrative becoming dangerous (doxxing or smear campaigns)

---

### 3. Scenario Mapping & Contingency Planning
- **Pre-Mortem:** “If this action fails, why?” Identify weak points.  
- **Branching Plans:** “If police push, we shift to X; if crowd thins, we withdraw.”  
- **Contingency Timers:** Reassess every 15 minutes during actions.

### 4. Communication & Safety Signals
**Hand Signals**
- 🔄 Flat hand rotation = De-escalate  
- ✊ Fist pump = Escalate to next step  
- 🙆 Hands on head = Emergency extraction  

**Coded Phrases**
- “Package is ready” = Prepared to escalate  
- “Need coffee” = Police movement toward site  

<Callout type="hint">
Documentation hygiene: Avoid storing raw strategy logs.  
Keep only encrypted, time-limited notes and obtain consent before sharing plans or debriefs.
</Callout>

---

### 5. Tactical Discipline
- Practice the **3-phase escalation model:** defensive → disruptive → withdrawal.  
- Use **15-minute reassessments** in fluid situations.  
- Always follow with **debrief, legal coordination, and emotional check-ins** after escalation.

Never pressure anyone into a risk they did not consent to.
