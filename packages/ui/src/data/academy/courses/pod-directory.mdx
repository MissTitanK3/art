# 🌍 General Pod Directory
<TrackBadge variant="pod-leadership" />

<Callout type="info">
Language access: For inclusive pod formation and outreach, pair this directory with the Translator and ASL Interpreter micro‑badges. Use interpreters or visual summaries as needed, and ensure all pod members know how to request support in their preferred language.
- Translator Micro‑Badge → /academy/translator-micro-badge
- ASL Interpreter Micro‑Badge → /academy/asl-interpreter-micro-badge
</Callout>

This directory lists **open, moderated Signal pods** that serve as regional entry points. These aren’t command centers — they help new folks find or start local pods in their area.

If you're forming a new pod, these spaces are a good place to ask questions, meet mutuals, or share intentions.

---

## How to Use This

- **Looking for a pod?** Start here, find your region, and reach out.
- **Starting a pod?** Join your regional General Pod and let folks know.
- **Already in a pod?** Connect with your regional General Pod so others can find you.

<Callout type="important">
Most pods listed here are **entry-level** and may be moderated, loosely vetted, or unvetted. Trust is built *locally and relationally*, and many pods use layered access, like outer and inner chats, based on in-person connection.
</Callout>

<Callout type="hint">
Documentation hygiene: Use minimal, time-bound retention. Avoid storing raw chat logs or sensitive pod notes in shared apps. Prefer encrypted, access-controlled notes and delete temporary files after review. ART does not support in-app pod directory archiving; use secure external tools if needed and always get explicit consent before sharing pod details or contacts.
</Callout>


---

## Pod Types (Quick Reference)

| Pod Type            | Access          | Purpose |
|---------------------|------------------|---------|
| **General Pod**     | Open or modded   | Entry-level space to ask questions, get routed, or connect |
| **Regional Pod**    | Lightly vetted   | Coordination across neighborhoods, ops planning |
| **Neighborhood Pod**| Heavily vetted   | Trusted field teams for urgent or local responses |
| **Ops Pod**         | Invite-only      | Specialized teams for dispatch, legal, medics, etc. |

Pod Structure:

### [Name] General Pod
- **Platform**: Signal
- **Link**: _Request via @[name]-trusted-mod_
- **Contact**: @[name]-trusted-mod
- **Covers**: Name of towns, counties or region
</Callout>
---
<Callout type="info">
🧠 **Note on Trust & Access**  
Each pod sets its own boundaries. Some use layered access — an “outer” general chat and a “core” vetted inner chat. This helps maintain safety while building community. Always ask what trust practices are in place.
</Callout>

<Callout type="important">
Consent & legal: Always ask before sharing pod details, contacts, or feedback. Pair this directory with Documentation Safety, Data Ethics Pledge, Legal Observer, and After‑Action Data Hygiene for best practices.
</Callout>
