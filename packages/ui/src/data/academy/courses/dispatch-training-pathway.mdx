# 📚 ART Dispatch Training Pathway
<TrackBadge variant="default" />

This pathway guides you from onboarding as a new team member to becoming a trusted admin dispatcher. It’s designed to build shared values, develop practical skills, and strengthen our community’s ability to respond to threats safely and effectively.

<Callout type="hint">
Access and trust increase over time. For how access roles and trust signatures work, see <a href="/academy/admin-tools">Admin Tools & Access Roles</a>.
</Callout>

---

## Level 1: Team Member Onboarding

<Callout type="info">
This level is for **anyone joining ART Network** — no dispatch or ops experience required.
</Callout>

**Goal:** Build shared language, values, and awareness. No operations yet.

### Modules:
1. **Introduction to ART Network**  
   What ART is, how it works, and our abolitionist, decentralized values. Overview of roles across the network.

2. **Know Your Rights + Reporting Basics**  
   How to identify and report ICE/law enforcement presence safely. What legal witnessing means.

3. **Digital Security for Field Teams**  
   Basics of secure communication (Signal, Proton), device safety, and what not to post. See <a href="/academy/digital-security-basics">Digital Security Basics</a> and <a href="/academy/documentation-safety">Documentation Safety</a>.

---

## Level 2: Field Coordination Basics (Basic Dispatcher)

<Callout type="hint">
You must complete Level 1 to be activated in any field-facing role.
</Callout>

**Goal:** Build trust and skills to serve in active field roles like Legal Observer, Witness, Care Station, or De-escalator.

<Callout type="info">
Graduates of Level 2 are considered **Basic Dispatchers** — they can view the dispatch map and participate in field coordination, but **cannot create new dispatches**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
Language access matters in the field. If interpretation is needed, consider volunteers with the <a href="/academy/translator-micro-badge">Translator Micro‑badge</a> or <a href="/academy/asl-interpreter-micro-badge">ASL Interpreter Micro‑badge</a>.
</Callout>

### Modules:
4. **Responding to Dispatch Calls**  
   How to respond to call-ups, accept roles, and prepare for a field deployment.

5. **Field Role Training**  
   Role-specific practices, team dynamics, logging field data securely, and staying grounded.

<Callout type="hint">
Dispatch accepts photo attachments. For video, share secure links (no direct uploads). Keep retention short and follow <a href="/academy/after-action-data-hygiene">After‑Action Data Hygiene</a>.
</Callout>

---

## Level 3: Dispatcher Certification (Verified Dispatcher)

<Callout type="important">
This level grants access to **create new dispatches**, coordinate field teams, and filter sensitive reports.
</Callout>

**Goal:** Train and test for trusted dispatchers who coordinate real-time responses under pressure.

<Callout type="info">
Graduates of Level 3 are considered **Verified Dispatchers** — they can activate others, create dispatches, and hold trust for coordination in live situations.
</Callout>

### Modules:
6. **Dispatch Coordination**
   Receiving and filtering reports, assigning field roles, and maintaining calm in high-pressure ops.

7. **Trust and Ethics in Dispatch**  
   Deciding who gets activated, minimizing harm, and protecting vulnerable info.

8. **Dealing With Chaos**  
   What to do when police lie or plans fall apart. Covers escalation protocols, emotional regulation, and aftercare.

<Callout type="info">
When conditions are chaotic or networks fail, anchor to: <a href="/academy/dealing-with-chaos">Dealing With Chaos</a>, <a href="/academy/comms-collapse">Comms Collapse</a>, and <a href="/academy/app-free-tactics">App‑Free Tactics</a>. For transport/handoffs, see <a href="/academy/runners-and-rides">Runners & Rides</a>.
</Callout>

---

## Level 4: Admin Dispatcher / Zone Lead

<Callout type="warning">
Only members who complete Level 3 and are approved by admins may access this level.
</Callout>

**Goal:** Manage backend dispatch tools, coverage zones, and mentorship.

### Modules:
9. **Admin Tools and Oversight**  
   Creating and managing dispatches, assigning trust levels, and reviewing sensitive activity logs (server‑side). Avoid claims of in‑app CSV export.

10. **Mentoring New Dispatchers**  
   Teaching ops calm and clarity, giving feedback, and building a culture of sharpness and care.

---

## Requirements & Progression

| Role                     | Required Level | Can View Dispatch Map | Can Create Dispatch | Certification?     |
|--------------------------|----------------|------------------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Team Member              | Level 1        | No                     | No                  | No                  |
| Field Responder          | Level 2        | Yes                    | No                  | Yes (Role Quiz)     |
| Dispatcher (Verified)    | Level 3        | Yes                    | ✅ Yes               | Yes                 |
| Admin Dispatcher / Lead  | Level 4        | Yes                    | ✅ Yes               | Yes + Admin Approval|

---

<Callout type="success">
Start at your level. Move forward when you’re ready. We support growth at your pace — with care, clarity, and community.
</Callout>
