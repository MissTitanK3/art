# ART Data Ethics & Security Pledge
<TrackBadge variant="default" />

As a member of the ART network, I commit to the following principles in all handling of data, communication, and community information.

This is not a checkbox. It’s a living agreement rooted in collective care and radical responsibility.

<Callout type="info">
Put this pledge into practice with: <a href="/courses/documentation-safety">Documentation Safety</a>, <a href="/courses/after-action-data-hygiene">After‑Action Data Hygiene</a>, and <a href="/courses/digital-security-basics">Digital Security Basics</a>. For roles and revocable access, see <a href="/courses/admin-tools">Admin Tools & Access Roles</a> and org‑wide <a href="/courses/collective-safety-protocols">Collective Safety Protocols</a>.
</Callout>

---

## 1. Data Belongs to the Community

- All data — personal, logistical, tactical, or informal — is **shared property** held in trust by those acting in **solidarity with impacted communities**.
- It is **not** the property of ART, any org, or individual volunteer.
- I will only access, store, or share data when it serves the **clear safety, empowerment, or consent** of the people it concerns.

**Examples of right use:**
- Securely sharing dispatch details with a verified legal observer during a response.  
- Encrypting a report that contains identifying info before storage.

**Examples of misuse:**
- Downloading a full report log “just in case.”  
- Looking up user activity without a reason directly tied to care or coordination.

---

## 2. Minimize, Encrypt, Forget

- I will **collect only the minimum data** needed to perform my role.
- I will **encrypt or compartmentalize** sensitive information by default — not as an afterthought.
- I will **delete what’s no longer needed**, and never hoard data “just in case.”

<Callout type="hint">
Hoarding data makes you a risk. Encryption makes you a shield.
</Callout>

---

## 3. Consent Is Ongoing

- I will not share personal stories, footage, or report data without **explicit, informed, revocable consent**.
- If the context, platform, or risk changes, I will **re-check consent**, even if previously given.
- I will respect “no” as a full answer — even if I disagree.

---

## 4. No Surveillance, No Collaboration
- I will never use ART systems for **surveillance, profiling, or internal policing**.
- I will not share data, access logs, or metadata with law enforcement, immigration agencies, or outside orgs — *under any circumstances*.

<Callout type="warning">
ART is not neutral. We are not data brokers. We are not “working with law enforcement.” Ever.
</Callout>

---

## 5. Decentralized Accountability

- I recognize that some roles carry **systemic or structural power**.
- While I occupy a role, I will act in ways that are:
  - **Transparent**  
  - **Justifiable**  
  - **Revocable by the community**
- Power is temporary and only held with ongoing community consent.

---

## 6. We Break and Fix Things Safely

- I will **report and help fix** any data breach, misuse, or risk — even if accidental.
- I will help **design systems to fail safely**, never at the expense of vulnerable people.
- I **consent to participate in** a **community-driven restorative justice model** if my actions cause harm.

<Callout type="important">
Even well-intentioned mistakes can cause serious harm. We don’t shame — but we don’t ignore harm either.
</Callout>

---

## Commitment

By continuing to participate in ART systems, I accept this pledge not as a checkbox — but as an **ongoing, evolving responsibility to my community**.

I understand that trust is earned, power is temporary, and consent is sacred.
