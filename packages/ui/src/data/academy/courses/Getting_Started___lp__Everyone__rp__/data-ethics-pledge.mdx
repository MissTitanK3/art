# ART Data Ethics & Security Pledge
<TrackBadge variant="default" />

<Callout type="info">
This pledge is a living agreement rooted in collective care and radical responsibility.  
It guides everyone who handles information within ART systems.
</Callout>

---

## Overview

As a member of the ART network, you commit to protecting community data, privacy, and safety.  
This pledge ensures that information is handled with consent, security, and accountability — never as surveillance or control.

## Learn & Do

### 1. Data Belongs to the Community

- All data — personal, tactical, or operational — belongs collectively to the communities it protects.  
- It is **not** the property of ART, any org, or any individual.  
- Access or sharing must serve **safety, consent, and empowerment** only.

✅ **Right use:**
- Securely sharing dispatch details with verified observers  
- Encrypting sensitive reports before saving  

❌ **Misuse:**
- Downloading full report logs “just in case”  
- Checking user data without a clear safety purpose  

---

### 2. Minimize, Encrypt, Forget

- Collect only what’s necessary  
- Encrypt by default  
- Delete data that’s no longer needed  

<Callout type="hint">
Hoarding data makes you a risk. Encryption makes you a shield.
</Callout>

---

### 3. Consent Is Ongoing

- Never share personal content without **explicit, informed, revocable consent**  
- Re-check consent if context or risk changes  
- Respect “no” without argument  

---

### 4. No Surveillance, No Collaboration

- Never use ART tools for profiling, monitoring, or internal policing  
- Never share data, access logs, or metadata with law enforcement or outside orgs  

<Callout type="warning">
ART is not neutral. We do not cooperate with state surveillance or policing — ever.
</Callout>

---

### 5. Decentralized Accountability

- Some roles carry structural power  
- Act with transparency, justification, and community oversight  
- Power is temporary and depends on ongoing consent  

---

### 6. We Break and Fix Things Safely

- Report any data misuse, breach, or vulnerability — even accidental  
- Help systems fail safely, not at others’ expense  
- Participate in restorative justice when harm occurs  

<Callout type="important">
Mistakes happen. Hiding harm causes more harm.  
Accountability is collective, not punitive.
</Callout>
