# 📱 Signal Messenger Basics & Accessibility
<TrackBadge variant="digital-security" />
<TrackBadge variant="community-care" />

<Callout type="info">
Signal keeps messages private and communication simple.  
This guide is built for people new to smartphones or digital privacy — including older volunteers, organizers, or community members.
</Callout>

---

## 🧭 Overview

Signal looks and feels like any text app — but with **end-to-end encryption** protecting every message, photo, and call.  
If you’ve ever sent a text or made a phone call, you already know most of what to do.

You’ll learn:
- How to install and set up Signal  
- How to navigate every screen  
- How to send, call, mute, and block safely  
- How to make Signal easier to see and use  

> “Privacy doesn’t mean secret — it means control over who hears you.”

---

## 🧩 1. Opening Signal and the Home Screen

When you open Signal, you’ll see:

| Area | What it Shows |
|------|----------------|
| 🔍 **Search Bar** | Find people or words in your chats |
| 💬 **Chats List** | All recent conversations |
| ✏️ **New Message Icon** | Start a new chat or group |
| 📷 **Camera Icon (optional)** | Quick photo or video message |

If your screen looks empty, don’t worry — it just means you haven’t messaged anyone yet.

Tap the ✏️ pencil in the lower corner to start.

---

## 💬 2. Sending Messages and Photos

1. Tap a contact name or number.  
2. Type your message.  
3. Tap the blue **send arrow** (➡️).  

To send a **photo or file**:
- Tap the 📎 **paperclip** or **camera** icon beside the typing box.  
- Choose your photo, then press **Send**.

🧠 Tip: Signal does not upload to the cloud.  
Your messages and photos stay only on your phone unless you delete or back them up manually.

---

## 🔔 3. Notifications & Muting Conversations

If a group chat gets noisy, you can **mute it** — meaning you’ll still get messages, but without sound or vibration.

**To mute a chat:**
1. Open the chat.  
2. Tap the person or group name at the top.  
3. Tap **Mute Notifications**.  
4. Choose how long (1 hour, 8 hours, 1 week, or always).  

To **unmute**, do the same and tap **Unmute**.

You can also tap the **bell icon 🔔** at the top right of a chat to toggle mute on or off.

---

## 🎙 4. Voice and Video Calls

To **start a call**:
1. Open a chat.  
2. Tap 📞 for a voice call, or 🎥 for a video call.  
3. Wait for the person to answer.

### Muting during a call:
While on a call, look for:
- **🎙 Microphone icon** → Tap it once to mute yourself (it turns gray or shows a line through it).  
- Tap it again to **unmute**.
- If you don’t see it, tap the screen once to show call controls. 

🧠 Tip: Muting is useful when you’re in a noisy place or don’t want background sounds to interrupt.

To **end the call**, tap the **red phone icon**.

---

## 📸 5. Sending Voice Notes and Photos

### Voice notes:
Hold down the 🎤 **microphone** next to the text box, speak, then release when done.

If you prefer, tap and **slide up** to lock the recording hands-free — then press **Send**.

### Photos:
Tap the 📷 camera icon → take a picture → tap the arrow to send.

> Signal automatically blurs faces if you choose “Blur faces” in the photo preview (helpful for protest or aid photos).

---

## 👥 6. Creating and Managing Groups

Groups are useful for pods, families, or event coordination.

To **create** one:
1. Tap ✏️ → **New Group**.  
2. Select members → tap **Next**.  
3. Type a group name → **Create**.

To **mute a group**: tap its name → **Mute notifications** → choose a duration.  
To **leave a group**: tap its name → scroll down → **Leave group**.

<Callout type="warning">
Always ask permission before adding someone to a group — especially if it’s used for organizing or location updates.
</Callout>

---

## 🧱 7. Using Reactions and Emojis

- Hold your finger on any message.  
- Choose an emoji (❤️ 👍 👎 😄 😢 🔥).  
- Tap again to remove it.

This helps acknowledge messages without cluttering the chat.

---

## 🧭 8. Adjusting Text, Colors, and Accessibility

Signal supports built-in accessibility settings.  

| Need | Action |
|------|---------|
| Larger text | Go to your phone **Settings → Accessibility → Font Size** |
| Higher contrast | **Signal Settings → Appearance → Dark / Light / High Contrast** |
| Easier reading | **Signal → Settings → Chats → Use system font size** |
| Voice typing | Tap the **microphone key** on your keyboard to dictate messages |

🧠 Tip: Try adjusting one setting at a time so changes feel familiar.

---

## 🔒 9. Privacy & Safety Options

Open Signal → tap your **profile picture or initials** → **Privacy**.  

Key options:
- **Screen Lock** – adds PIN/fingerprint before opening app.  
- **Disappearing Messages** – auto-deletes chats after a set time.  
- **Read Receipts** – toggle off if you don’t want others to see when you read.  
- **Typing Indicators** – toggle off if you prefer privacy.

Always confirm with your pod what retention time you should use (e.g. 1 day or 1 week).

---

## 🧹 10. Keeping Signal Clean and Simple

- Delete old chats once resolved.  
- Archive inactive conversations (swipe left → **Archive**).  
- Pin your top contacts (press and hold → **Pin**).  
- Keep your display uncluttered — clarity improves focus under stress.

> Simplicity is safety. If you can find what you need quickly, you’ll act calmly in a crisis.

---

## 🧰 11. Common Troubleshooting

| Issue | Try This |
|--------|-----------|
| “I can’t hear them on a call” | Make sure you’re **unmuted** and volume is up |
| “They can’t hear me” | Tap the 🎙 again to unmute |
| “Group messages don’t show” | Ask if you were re-added, or check your internet |
| “Signal seems frozen” | Close the app fully and reopen it |
| “I see a lock symbol next to a message” | That means it’s **encrypted** — safe to read |

If all else fails: restart your phone. Signal reloads automatically.
