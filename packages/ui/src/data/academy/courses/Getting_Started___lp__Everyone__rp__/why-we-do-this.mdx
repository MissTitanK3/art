# Why We Do This
<TrackBadge variant="default" />

<Callout type="warning">
ART Academy is not just a training space — it stands in a long lineage of mutual defense, resistance, and care.
</Callout>

---

## Overview

Federal immigration enforcement operates through fear, surveillance, and control.  
It disproportionately harms immigrants, asylum seekers, Black and Brown communities, Indigenous nations, and working-class families.  
Where the State fails to protect, communities must — and do — protect each other.

ART exists to make that protection organized, ethical, and decentralized.

---

## Learn & Do

### The Problem

- ICE and similar agencies target marginalized people with near impunity.  
- Their actions create lasting trauma and generational instability.  
- Institutional accountability is rare; community response is essential.

> When systems harm, we build our own safety networks.

---

### The Response

We believe in **organized, collective response** as a moral obligation:

- Bear witness to State violence.  
- Provide direct, practical support to those affected.  
- Build systems of care and accountability outside the State.  

ART’s mission is not only reactive — it is *prefigurative*: we practice the world we want to live in.

---

### Our Roots

ART Academy is grounded in:

- 📖 **Know Your Rights** education  
- 🧰 **Mutual Aid infrastructure**  
- 🧭 **Abolitionist principles** — dismantling harm while building care systems  
- 🧑🏽‍🤝‍🧑🏾 **Community-led organizing**  

These ideas come from generations of resistance movements, including:

- 🕊 **Compañeras Latinas** and sanctuary networks of the 1980s  
- ✊🏾 **Black liberation movements** advocating self-determined safety  
- 🪶 **Indigenous land and border defense** organizing against State violence  

We now use encrypted communication, decentralized pods, and non-State coordination to continue this lineage safely and effectively.

---

### Our Commitments

- 🚫 No collaboration with law enforcement.  
- 🛡️ Prioritize the safety of the most vulnerable.  
- 🌱 Dismantle harmful systems while protecting people *now*.  

<Callout type="success">
If you value dignity, solidarity, and collective action — you belong here.
</Callout>

### Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|----------|
| **Mutual Aid** | Voluntary support based on solidarity, not charity |
| **Abolitionist** | A person or framework dedicated to ending oppressive systems like ICE |
| **Sanctuary Network** | Safe community spaces for protection and care |
| **Solidarity** | Standing *with* others, not above them |
| **Decentralized** | Organized without a single point of failure; resilient by design |
