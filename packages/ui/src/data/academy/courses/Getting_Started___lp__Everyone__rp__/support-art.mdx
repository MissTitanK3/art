# Support ART
<TrackBadge variant="default" />

<Callout type="info">
ART is a **community-powered alert, verification, and response network** that helps people **spot, track, and respond** to ICE and law enforcement activity.  
We don’t rely on the state — we build our own systems.
</Callout>

To keep the network running and allow full-time coordinators and Dispatch Admins to focus on training, outreach, and scaling, we need **$3,000 per month as a survival baseline**, with any additional support going directly into **growing the network, not salaries**.

---

## Why We’re Raising Funds

- Families face **raids, harassment, and intimidation** every day.  
- Most people **don’t know their rights** or **can’t organize fast enough** when threats appear.  
- Existing tools are **centralized, corporate, or leave vulnerable people behind**.

ART fills that gap — but scaling this to cover more regions takes **dedicated time, training, and resources**.

---

## What It Actually Costs to Live in Olympia, WA

Olympia is **not cheap**, with housing and transportation well above the national average. Based on current averages (Payscale.com):

| Expense                        | Monthly Estimate        |
|--------------------------------|--------------------------|
| **Median Rent**                | $1,847                  |
| **Energy Bill**                | $187.90                 |
| **Phone Bill**                 | $177.11                 |
| **Gas (50 gallons @ $4.17)**   | $209                    |
| **Groceries (basic staples)**  | ~$500 (+5% markup)      |
| **Healthcare (average visits & Rx)** | ~$300           |
| **Transportation (car upkeep, bus, insurance)** | ~$350 (+29% markup) |
| **Miscellaneous (clothing, emergencies, buffer)** | ~$500 |

**Estimated Monthly Survival Total:** ~**$3,800–$5,000/month** (before taxes or savings).

While $3,000/month keeps the lights on, reaching **$5,000/month allows us to cover real costs** without crisis.  
Anything beyond $5,000 isn’t for salaries — it fuels **network growth**.

---

## Funding Tiers

- **Baseline Survival: $3,000/month**  
  Keeps ART online, supports one Admin, and launches **1–3 pods per month**.

- **Stability: $5,000/month**  
  Covers Olympia survival costs with a small buffer, while allowing for **consistent pod launches and outreach**.

- **Growth Beyond $5,000/month**  
  Extra funds go toward:  
  - Launching **more pods** each month  
  - Printing **multi-language flyers and emergency cards**  
  - **Subsidizing Starter Kits** for low-income or high-risk pods  
  - Building a **regional team** (part-time Dispatch Admins, tech support, and training staff) so the network can scale sustainably.

---

## How Funds Are Used

### Core Tech Infrastructure
- Hosting, mapping tools (Leaflet), databases (Supabase), and backend for Watch, Dispatch, and the Academy (currently $100/month at minimal use).

### Community Training & Certification
- Expanding ART Academy with multi-language, accessible courses so more people can step up.

### Launching Local Pods
- Starter Kits to get pods running in new areas, equipped to recruit, organize, and respond.

### Paid Dispatch Admins
- Coordinators who run Dispatch, onboard new pods, and ensure volunteers are trained and ready.

---

## Dispatch Admin Growth Roadmap

### Phase 1: Core Launch – **$3,000/month (Survival)**

- Fund **one full-time Dispatch Admin (core coordinator)** at a bare minimum level.  
- Keeps Watch, Dispatch, and Academy running.  
- Launches **2 new pods per month**.  
- Onboards **50+ new volunteers monthly**.

### Phase 2: Stable Operations – **$5,000/month**

- Covers actual Olympia survival costs without constant financial strain.  
- Supports consistent pod launches, volunteer training, and tech upgrades.

### Phase 3: Regional Expansion – **Beyond $5,000/month**

Funds beyond core survival pay for:  
- 2–4 **part-time Regional Dispatch Admins** ($1,500/month each).  
- 1 **Academy/Training Coordinator** ($2,000/month).  
- 1 **Tech Support (part-time)** ($1,000/month).  

This supports ~**15–20 states, 50+ pods, and hundreds of trained volunteers**.

## What a Pod Starter Kit Includes

Each new pod (2–10 people) receives:

### Physical Materials
- 500 **Know Your Rights flyers** (QR codes for Watch/Dispatch).  
- **Tabling basics** (signage, laminated info sheets, clipboards).  
- **Pod contact cards** (customizable).  
- **Emergency hotline list template** (legal/mutual aid).  
- **Outreach banner** (“Join Your Local Community Defense Pod”).  
- **Optional radio kit** (2 handhelds).  
- **Stickers/buttons** for visibility.

### Digital Resources
- **Pod Creator Mentor in ART Academy** (training on organizing, tech setup, and security).  
- **Recruitment and onboarding templates**.  
- **Guides for setting up Signal groups and hotlines**.

**Cost per Pod Kit:**  
- **$500** – Standard kit for 5–10 volunteers.  
- **$1,000** – Includes radios, banner, and full tabling campaign.
