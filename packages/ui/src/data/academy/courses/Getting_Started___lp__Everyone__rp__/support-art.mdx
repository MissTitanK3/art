# Support ART
<TrackBadge variant="default" />

<Callout type="info">
ART is a **community-powered alert, verification, and response network** that helps people **spot, track, and respond** to ICE and law enforcement activity.  
We don’t rely on the state — we build our own systems.
</Callout>

---

## Overview

To keep the network running and allow full-time coordinators and Dispatch Admins to focus on training, outreach, and scaling, we need a **baseline of $3,000 per month**.  
Anything above that fuels **growth — not salaries**.  

This course explains how ART sustains itself, what the funds cover, and how your contribution builds power across regions.

---

## Learn & Do

### Why We’re Raising Funds

- Families face **raids, harassment, and intimidation** daily.  
- Many communities **don’t know their rights** or **can’t mobilize fast enough**.  
- Existing tools are **corporate, centralized, or exclusionary**.  

ART fills the gap — a decentralized, privacy-first alternative that relies on volunteer labor and public support.

### How Funds Are Used

#### Core Tech Infrastructure
Hosting, maps, databases, and backend systems for Watch, Dispatch, and Academy (~$100/month at low load).

#### Community Training & Certification
Developing multi-language courses to certify volunteers and pod leads.

#### Launching Local Pods
Starter Kits equip new pods to recruit, organize, and respond.

#### Paid Dispatch Admins
Coordinators onboard new pods, train volunteers, and manage regional coverage.

---

### Dispatch Admin Growth Roadmap

#### Phase 1 — **$3,000/month (Survival)**
- One full-time Dispatch Admin  
- Launch 2 new pods/month  
- Support 50+ new volunteers monthly  

#### Phase 2 — **$5,000/month (Stable)**
- Covers Olympia living costs without crisis  
- Expands consistent training and tech maintenance  

#### Phase 3 — **Beyond $5,000 (Regional Expansion)**
Funds additional:
- 2–4 part-time **Regional Dispatch Admins** ($1,500 each)  
- 1 **Academy/Training Coordinator** ($2,000)  
- 1 **Tech Support Staff** ($1,000)  

→ Supports ~15–20 states, 50+ pods, and hundreds of volunteers.

### Pod Starter Kits

Each pod (2–10 people) receives:

**Physical Materials**
- 500 *Know Your Rights* flyers (QR to Watch/Dispatch)  
- Tabling signage + laminated sheets  
- Pod contact cards  
- Emergency hotline templates  
- Outreach banner (“Join Your Local Community Defense Pod”)  
- Optional: 2 handheld radios  
- Stickers/buttons for visibility  

**Digital Resources**
- **Pod Creator Mentor** through ART Academy  
- **Recruitment & onboarding templates**  
- **Signal group setup and hotline guides**  

| Kit Type | Cost | Includes |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Standard | $500 | Flyers + signage + materials |
| Expanded | $1,000 | Full kit with radios and outreach gear |
