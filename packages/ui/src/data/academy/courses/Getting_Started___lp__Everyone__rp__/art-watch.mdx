## What Is Community Watch?

Community Watch is a mobile-friendly tool that allows anyone to:

- 🚨 Report suspected ICE/CBP/police or other harmful state activity
- 🗺️ View real-time, verified and unverified alerts on a public heatmap
- 🛡️ Stay informed and safe — no account or app required

Your identity is never required.

---

## What You Can Report

Use Community Watch to report:

- 🚓 **Unmarked vehicles** that look like ICE, CBP, DHS, or police
- 🧍‍♂️ **Agents** detaining, questioning, or surveilling people
- 🚧 **Suspicious activity** near homes, workplaces, or gathering spots
- 🕒 **Time and direction** of movements

> ¿No estás segurx si son agentes? Reporta con tanto detalle como puedas.

## Recognizing ICE Activity

- **Vehicles**: SUVs/vans with government or no plates
- **Agents**: Blue/black vests labeled “POLICE ICE,” “HSI,” or “CBP”
- **Behavior**: Stopping pedestrians, entering homes/businesses, taking photos or watching from cars

---

## What Happens After You Report?

1. ✅ Your report goes through **automated spam filtering**
2. 👀 Verified volunteers **review for clarity and safety**
3. 🗺️ If approved, it appears in **Community Watch**; zone admins may label it as **Confirmed Watch** and can create a **Dispatch** from it.

> Most reports are reviewed within 30 minutes.

---

## How to Stay Safe While Reporting

<Callout type="warning">
Never risk your safety to gather a report. It’s okay to wait and submit later.
</Callout>

- 🛑 **Do not approach** or confront agents  
- 🎥 Only record if it’s safe — and disable location metadata if possible  
- 🔐 Use secure browsers or devices when reporting from sensitive areas  

---

### What Is Metadata?

Metadata is hidden information stored in photos and videos — like the **time, date, location, and device used**. This data can reveal where you were, when you were there, and what device you used — even if you don’t share that info directly.

---

### How to Remove Metadata

- **iPhone**: Share using **AirDrop** or **Messages**, not iCloud or the Photos app  
- **Android**: Use “Share as file” or install apps like **Scrambled Exif**  
- **Desktop**: Open the image → take a **screenshot** → share the screenshot instead  
- **Best practice**: Always **blur faces** and remove background details if they aren’t needed

<Callout type="info">
📷 If you’re not sure how to strip metadata, the easiest option is to take a screenshot — screenshots don’t carry the original hidden data.
</Callout>
