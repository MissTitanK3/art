# Community Watch Basics
<TrackBadge variant="default" />

<Callout type="info">
Community Watch is a **community-powered alert system**, not surveillance. It helps neighbors protect each other — never institutions.
</Callout>

---

## Overview

Community Watch lets anyone report and view suspected ICE, CBP, or police presence in real time — without needing an account or app.  
It protects communities by sharing trusted, anonymized information.

## Learn & Do

### What Is Community Watch?

It’s a public tool to:
- 🚨 Report ICE/CBP/police presence  
- 🗺️ View verified and unverified alerts on a live map  
- 🛡️ Stay informed without sharing your identity  

Your report helps others act safely and collectively.

---

### What You Can Report

Report:
- 🚓 Unmarked or government SUVs/vans  
- 🧍 Agents detaining or questioning people  
- 🚧 Suspicious patrols near homes or workplaces  
- 🕒 Time, direction, and behavior details  

> ¿No estás segurx si son agentes? Reporta con tanto detalle como puedas.

### Recognizing ICE Activity

- **Vehicles:** White/black SUVs, vans with federal or no plates  
- **Agents:** Vests labeled “POLICE ICE,” “HSI,” or “CBP”  
- **Behavior:** Detaining, questioning, photographing, or watching from cars  

If unsure, describe what you see — not what you assume.

---

### What Happens After You Report

1. ✅ Automated spam filter runs.  
2. 👀 Verified volunteers review for clarity and safety.  
3. 🗺️ Approved reports appear on the Community Watch map.  
4. 🔁 Dispatchers may create field responses from confirmed reports.

> Most reports are reviewed within 30 minutes.

---

### How to Stay Safe While Reporting

<Callout type="warning">
Never risk your safety to capture a report. It’s always okay to wait and submit later.
</Callout>

- 🛑 Don’t approach agents  
- 🎥 Record only when safe, with metadata removed  
- 🔐 Use secure browsers or devices for sensitive uploads  

---

### Metadata: Hidden Risk

Metadata is invisible data (time, date, GPS, device ID) stored in photos and videos.  
It can reveal your exact location or identity.

**To remove metadata:**
- **iPhone:** AirDrop or Messages (avoid iCloud sharing)  
- **Android:** “Share as file” or use *Scrambled Exif*  
- **Desktop:** Take a screenshot of the image before sharing  

<Callout type="info">
📷 The simplest protection: take a screenshot before uploading. Screenshots strip hidden metadata.
</Callout>
