## Overview

A clear report helps others stay safe and gives dispatchers reliable information for field coordination.  
This course shows how to capture only what’s needed, without exposing yourself or others to risk.

---

## Learn & Do

### What to Include in a Report

A strong report answers five key points:

- 📍 **Location** – Street address, intersection, or landmark  
- 🚗 **Vehicles** – Type, color, make/model, plate (if safe)  
- 👮 **Agents** – Number, uniform markings, or visible behavior  
- 🕒 **Time** – Exact or approximate time and direction of movement  
- 📸 **Media** – Photo or video *only if safe to take*

<Callout type="info">
ART does not support photo uploads directly in public reports.  
Videos should be linked securely (no direct upload).  
Blur faces and identifiers before sharing per [Documentation Safety](/courses/documentation-safety).
</Callout>

> The more specific and concrete your report, the more actionable it becomes.

---

### Example Reports

✅ **Good:**  
> “3 ICE agents in navy jackets at Main St Walmart, 2 PM. Black unmarked SUV, partial plate 83Z.”

❌ **Vague:**  
> “ICE downtown maybe earlier?”

---

### Safety Before Submission

<Callout type="warning">
Always ensure your safety **before** submitting any report.
</Callout>

**Safety checklist**
- In a secure location, away from active agents  
- Device locked (PIN, passcode, or biometrics)  
- Able to report unseen or anonymously  
- If unsafe: memorize details and submit later  

---

### Taking Encrypted Photos or Notes

You can use your phone safely with small adjustments:

✅ Use your device’s native camera with lock enabled  
✅ Use **Signal** to take or send encrypted photos  
🧊 Avoid syncing with Google Photos, iCloud, or shared folders  
🔒 Never unlock your phone for agents — you have the right to stay silent  

<Callout type="info">
Apps like **ObscuraCam** or **Simple Mobile Tools Camera** help blur faces and save locally, not to the cloud.
</Callout>

<Callout type="hint">
Need interpretation help? Coordinate with volunteers certified in the  
[Translator Micro-badge](/courses/translator-micro-badge) or  
[ASL Interpreter Micro-badge](/courses/asl-interpreter-micro-badge).
</Callout>

### How Your Report Gets Verified

1. 🧠 **Automated Review** filters spam and duplicates  
2. 👀 **Human Verification** checks accuracy and safety  
3. 🗺️ Verified reports appear on the Community Watch map  

<Callout type="success">
You don’t need to be a dispatcher — your clear, safe observation already strengthens the network.
</Callout>

---

### After You Submit

If verified:
- ✅ It may become a **Dispatch** for field response  
- 📢 Local volunteers can be notified (region-configured)  
- 🔐 All data is encrypted and never shared outside ART systems  

---

### What **Not** to Do

| ❌ Don’t… | Because… |
|-----------|-----------|
| Approach or confront agents | You could be detained or harmed |
| Record if unsafe | Safety > footage |
| Share rumors or screenshots | Spreads panic and wastes resources |

---

### Step-by-Step Quick Guide

1. 👀 **Observe** from a safe distance  
2. 🧾 **Record** details (and media if safe)  
3. 📱 **Submit** via Community Watch  

👉 [Submit a Report](https://watch.alwaysreadytools.org/)

> You don’t need credentials — just clarity, care, and courage.

---

### Tool Comparison

| Tool | Purpose | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| **Community Watch (public)** | Report and view map alerts | No login required |
| **Confirmed Watch** | Verified internal layer | Maintained by dispatchers |
| **ART Dispatch** | Field coordination | Requires login |
