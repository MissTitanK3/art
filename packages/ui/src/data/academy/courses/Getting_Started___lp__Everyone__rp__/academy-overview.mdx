## Overview

Welcome to the ART Academy.  
This is your starting point to learn the tools, roles, and responsibilities that keep communities safe through coordination, preparedness, and care.

The Academy helps you:
- 📚 Gain practical skills  
- 🛡️ Build confidence in real-world operations  
- ✅ Earn verified and certified trust within ART systems  

Courses may be **Qualified** (self-paced learning with attestation) or **Certified** (instructor-reviewed with testing and role unlocks).

---

## Learn & Do

### How Certification Works

Your learning journey has three main stages:

| Level | How to Complete | Unlocks | Typical Time |
|-------|------------------|----------|---------------|
| **Verified** | Complete intake form (skills, interests) | Observer roles, basic access | ~5 min |
| **Qualified** | Read course and mark as complete | Field support, dispatch visibility | 10–30 min per course |
| **Certified** | Pass reflection/test and coordinator review | Leadership or sensitive data roles | Varies by role |

<Callout type="info">
Related: [Trust & Ethics in Dispatch](/courses/trust-and-ethics-in-dispatch) and  
[Data Ethics Pledge](/courses/data-ethics-pledge) set baseline expectations for integrity and safety.
</Callout>

### Role Categories

After completing relevant tracks, you can qualify for:

- 🧤 **Field Support** – On-site safety and logistics  
  → [Field Role Training Hub](/courses/field-role-training-hub), [Field Safety](/courses/field-safety)
- 📞 **Dispatch & Coordination** – Remote logistics, communication, and recordkeeping  
  → [Dispatch Training Pathway](/courses/dispatch-training-pathway)
- 👥 **Community Liaison** – Language access and outreach  
  → [Translator Micro-Badge](/courses/translator-micro-badge), [ASL Interpreter Micro-Badge](/courses/asl-interpreter-micro-badge)
- 🖥️ **No-Contact Roles** – Admin, data, or tech infrastructure  
  → [Admin Tools](/courses/admin-tools), [Digital Security Basics](/courses/digital-security-basics)

### Understanding TrackBadges

Each course carries one or more **TrackBadges** to show which area it supports.

| Track | Focus |
|--------|-------|
| **default** | General foundational learning |
| **observation-legal** | Legal observing and documentation |
| **field-safety** | Personal and team safety in the field |
| **community-care** | Emotional or material support |
| **direct-action** | Tactics and strategy for active resistance |
| **tech-comms** | Technical and communications systems |
| **logistics** | Resource and operations management |
| **court-support** | Legal advocacy and courtroom solidarity |
| **pod-leadership** | Leadership and group organization |
| **movement-strategy** | Campaign and network-scale planning |
| **translator / asl** | Accessibility and interpretation |
| **faith-support** | Faith-based and spiritual accompaniment |
| **survivor-escort** | Support and protection for survivors |
| **vehicle-specialist** | Mobile operations and maintenance |

These badges help learners identify where each lesson fits in their growth path.
