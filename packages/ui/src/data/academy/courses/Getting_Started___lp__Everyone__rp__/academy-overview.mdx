## What Is the ART Academy?

The Academy is a training space designed to:

- 📚 Equip you with actionable knowledge  
- 🛡️ Prepare you for field and support roles  
- ✅ Verify your understanding for dispatch trust  

Courses are either **Qualified** (read-and-attest) or **Certified** (test-based with reviews).  
Both help us build coordinated, safe responses to ICE and law enforcement activity.

---

## How Certification Works

Your progress moves through three stages. Each stage builds trust and unlocks new roles.

| Level         | How to Complete                         | Unlocks                                         | Typical Time |
|---------------|------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| **Verified**  | Fill out intake form (skills, interests) | Observer roles, basic updates                   | ~5 minutes   |
| **Qualified** | Read course, mark as complete            | Field support, dispatch visibility, prep roles  | 10–30 mins/course |
| **Certified** | Complete test/reflection, coordinator review | Lead roles, sensitive data access, coordination tools | Varies by role |

## Roles You Can Be Certified For

After completing the needed tracks, you can be certified for:

- 🧤 **Field Support** – On-site, safety-conscious roles  
	(see: **[Field Role Training Hub](/courses/field-role-training-hub)**, **[Field Safety](/courses/field-safety)**)  
- 📞 **Dispatch & Coordination** – Remote communications, logistics, and recordkeeping  
	(see: **[Dispatch Training Pathway](/courses/dispatch-training-pathway)**)  
- 👥 **Community Liaison** – Outreach, language access, and local connections  
	(see: **[Translator Micro‑Badge](/courses/translator-micro-badge)**, **[ASL Interpreter Micro‑Badge](/courses/asl-interpreter-micro-badge)**)  
- 🚫 **No-Contact Roles** – Admin, tech, research, or secure back-end operations  
	(see: **[Admin Tools](/courses/admin-tools)**, **[Digital Security Basics](/courses/digital-security-basics)**)

Each role links to specific training.

## Callout Types in the Academy

We use different callout styles to keep courses clear and scannable:

### 🌀 Info  
<Callout type="info">  
For guidance and general tips.  
**Example:** "This tool is most useful for quick field assessments."  
</Callout>

### ⚠️ Warning  
<Callout type="warning">  
Highlights risk or safety concerns.  
**Example:** "Never share undocumented individuals’ names or locations publicly."  
</Callout>

### ✅ Success  
<Callout type="success">  
Marks milestones or completion.  
**Example:** "Your profile is verified and ready for dispatch calls."  
</Callout>

### 💡 Hint  
<Callout type="hint">  
Offers shortcuts or optional advice.  
**Example:** "Use the ‘#urgent’ tag only for active ICE sightings."  
</Callout>

### 🔥 Important  
<Callout type="important">  
Marks **non-optional actions or vital safety steps**.  
**Example:** "Never act alone during in-person field interventions."  
</Callout>

### 📚 Rabbit Hole  
<Callout type="rabbit-hole">  
Optional deep dives for extra learning.  
**Example:** "Explore the history of community copwatching movements."  
</Callout>
