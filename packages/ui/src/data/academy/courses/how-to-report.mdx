# How to Report ICE Presence
<TrackBadge variant="default" />

<Callout type="info">
Reporting takes less than 2 minutes and helps protect your entire community. Over 75% of successful field responses start with a clear report from someone like you.
</Callout>

---

## What to Include in a Report

A strong report answers:

- 📍 **Location** – Street address, intersection, or nearby landmark
- 🚗 **Vehicles** – Type, color, make/model, license plates (if safe)
- 👮 **Agents** – Number of ICE agents or law enforcement officers
- 🕒 **Time** – Time of event and direction of movement
- 📸 **Media** – Photo/video evidence (only if safe to capture)

> The more specific and clear your report, the more actionable it is.

### Example Reports

**✅ Good:**  
"3 ICE agents in navy jackets at Main St Walmart, 2pm. Black unmarked SUV, partial plate 83Z."

**❌ Vague:**  
"ICE downtown maybe earlier?"

---

## Safety Before Submission

<Callout type="warning">
Always make sure you’re safe **before** reporting.
</Callout>

### Safety Checklist

- Are you in a **secure location** with no active agents nearby?  
- Is your **device locked** (PIN, passcode, or biometric)?  
- Can you **document or submit without being seen or tracked**?  
- If not — **memorize key details** and wait to submit when safe.

---

### What to Use for Encrypted Documentation

Most people use their phone camera — that's okay. Here's how to do it more securely:

- ✅ **Use your phone’s native camera app** with your screen lock turned on (PIN or biometrics = basic encryption)
- ✅ **Use apps like [Signal](https://signal.org)** to take and send photos — they encrypt everything by default
- 🧊 Avoid uploading to Google Photos, iCloud, or shared folders  
- 🔒 **Don’t unlock your phone for agents** — you have the right to stay silent

<Callout type="info">
Want extra protection? Apps like **Simple Mobile Tools Camera** or **ObscuraCam** let you blur faces and store locally, not in the cloud.
</Callout>


---

## How Your Report Gets Verified

Every report goes through:

1. **Automated Review** – Filters out spam, duplicates, and vague entries
2. **Human Verification** – A trusted volunteer checks accuracy and context

<Callout type="success">
You don’t need to be a dispatcher to help — just share what you **see**, clearly and safely.
</Callout>

---

## After You Submit

If verified:

- ✅ It’s added to the **ART Dispatch**
- 📢 Volunteers in that area may be alerted
- 🔐 Your data is **encrypted** and **never shared** with ICE or corporations

---

## What **Not** to Do

| ❌ Don’t… | Because… |
|----------|----------|
| Approach or confront ICE agents | It puts you and others in danger |
| Film if you feel unsafe | Safety comes before footage |
| Share secondhand rumors | They spread panic and waste resources |

---

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. 👀 **Observe** from a safe distance  
2. 📱 **Record** details (or media, if safe)  
3. 🧾 **Report** via the online form  

👉 [Submit a Report](https://watch.alwaysreadytools.org/)

> If you can witness, you can report. You don’t need to be an expert — just a neighbor who cares.
