# 🛑 Escalation & Evacuation Protocols
<TrackBadge variant="direct-action" />
<TrackBadge variant="field-safety" />

<Callout type="important">
**Golden Rule:** Evacuate *before* you think you need to. Late exits cause most field injuries.
</Callout>

---

## Overview
When tensions rise—police movements, counter-protesters, or sudden violence—teams without preplanned exits often freeze or scatter.  
This course trains you to:
- Recognize early escalation cues  
- Trigger evacuations decisively  
- Coordinate fallback routes and buddy systems  
- Conduct post-action checks with care and OPSEC discipline  

---

## Learn & Do

### 1. Threat Indicators (5-Minute Warning Signs)

| Police or Crowd Behavior | Pod Response Tier |
|---------------------------|-------------------|
| Line formation shifting to wedge | **Yellow Alert:** Test exits |
| Gas masks or uniform swaps | **Orange Alert:** Move vulnerable |
| Kettling or flank attempts | **Red Alert:** Execute escape plan |

---

### 2. Evacuation Triggers

**Non-negotiable:**  
- Tear gas, pepper spray, or chemical weapons  
- Live or rubber ammunition  
- Encirclement or kettling  

**Discretionary:**  
- Half of comms down (Signal, radio)  
- Medical emergency with no aid access  

<Callout type="info">
During exits, safety > filming.  
If documentation is required, avoid faces and follow [Documentation Safety](/courses/documentation-safety) and [After-Action Data Hygiene](/courses/after-action-data-hygiene).
</Callout>

---

### 3. Mapping Exit Routes

Plan **three fallback paths** before any action:

```
PRIMARY: Street → Alley → Safehouse
SECONDARY: Park → Path → Community Hub
TERTIARY: Disperse → Transit → Regroup Later
```


Mark routes with temporary, non-identifying symbols:

```
▲ = Safe route
▼ = Blocked or dangerous
```


---

### 4. Field Communication Protocols

**Voice Commands**
```
"Bird in flight" → Prepare to move
"Sunset now" → Disperse immediately
"Medic echo" → Follow medic line out
```


**Radio/Signal Format**
```
[PRIORITY] [LOCATION] [NEED]
RED – 5th/Broadway – KETTLE
YELLOW – SE Park – MEDIC
```


<Callout type="hint">
If networks degrade, switch to runners or brevity codes.  
See [App-Free Tactics](/courses/app-free-tactics) and [Digital Security Basics](/courses/digital-security-basics).
</Callout>

---

### 5. Extraction Drills (Monthly)

- **Blindfold Navigation:** Train spatial memory to fallback points.  
- **Runner Relay:** Pass evacuation orders manually.  
- **Blackout Escape:** Practice silent movement without light or comms.  

<Callout type="warning">
Never regroup at the dispersal point.  
Fallback must be 300+ meters away to avoid secondary arrests.
</Callout>

---

### 6. Vulnerability Prioritization

Evacuation order:
1. Disabled participants, medics, legal observers  
2. General participants  
3. Marshals/safety leads  

Assign buddy pairs to prevent anyone being left behind.

<Callout type="info">
For language or disability access, include volunteers with [Translator Micro-badge](/courses/translator-micro-badge) or [ASL Interpreter Micro-badge](/courses/asl-interpreter-micro-badge).
</Callout>

---

### 7. Gear Checklist

- Reflective/glow tape (night ops)  
- Whistle (3 short bursts = danger)  
- Burner metro cards (sealed case)  
- Temporary UV markings (removable)  

<Callout type="warning">
Markings must be removable and non-identifying.  
Erase all after dispersal and never post photos of routes.
</Callout>

---

### 8. After-Action Musts

- Headcount within 15 minutes at fallback point  
- Provide water, warmth, grounding for trauma  
- Clear comms logs and temporary files per OPSEC  

Coordinate care and follow-through via:  
[After-Action Data Hygiene](/courses/after-action-data-hygiene) · [Crisis Debriefing & Mutual Care](/courses/crisis-debrief-care) · [Care & Emotional Support](/courses/care-support) · [Court Support](/courses/court-support) · [Legal Follow-through & Jail Support](/courses/legal-followthrough-jail-support)

---

### 9. Final Readiness Checklist

- [ ] Recognize escalation signals  
- [ ] Practiced no-comms escapes  
- [ ] Memorized 3+ fallback points  
- [ ] Trained at least 2 backups for exits  

<Callout type="success">
Smooth exits save lives.  
Practice until calm, silent movement is instinct.
</Callout>
