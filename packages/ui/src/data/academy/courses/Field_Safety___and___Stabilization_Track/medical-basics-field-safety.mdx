## Overview

Crowds, police, and unpredictable field conditions create **high medical risk**.  
Emergencies like severe bleeding, panic attacks, dehydration, or gas exposure can escalate quickly.  
This module prepares you to stabilize people until trained medics or EMS arrive.

You will learn:
- Bleeding control (Stop the Bleed)
- CPR and airway positioning
- Field triage systems (RPM)
- Chemical exposure decontamination
- Essential field kit assembly

---

## Learn & Do

### 🩸 Stop the Bleed Essentials
- Bright spurting = **arterial**, apply tourniquet  
- Dark steady = **venous**, use pressure and packing  
- Use improvised materials only if no kit is available  
- Note tourniquet time visibly (limb or forehead)  

**Tourniquet Rules**
- Apply only when direct pressure fails  
- Do not loosen or remove once applied  
- Expect pain — it means it’s tight enough  

---

### 🩺 Triage Basics (RPM System)

1. **Respiration:** Over 30/min → Red  
2. **Pulse:** Weak/absent radial → Red  
3. **Mentation:** Can’t follow simple commands → Red  

| Category | Status | Action |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| 🟥 Red | Critical | Immediate aid |
| 🟨 Yellow | Stable | Delayed |
| 🟩 Green | Walking wounded | Reassure + assist |
| ⬛ Black | No signs of life | Do not expend field resources |

---

### ☣️ Chemical Exposure Response

**Tear Gas vs. Pepper Spray**
- Gas = airborne, causes coughing/tearing  
- Spray = surface burning on skin or eyes  

**Decon Steps**
1. Move to clean air  
2. Remove contaminated clothing  
3. Flush eyes with water or saline  
4. Never use milk, oils, or lotion  

Assist with prescribed inhalers for asthma symptoms.

### 🧰 What to Carry

- Gloves, gauze, tourniquet, saline  
- Eye protection and masks  
- Trauma shears and emergency blanket  
- Pocket reference card  

---

### ⚡ Skills Retention Plan

- Practice Stop the Bleed and CPR quarterly  
- Pair up with a medic buddy for review  
- Keep a list of who’s trained in your pod  
- Carry printed skill cards and refresh notes  

---

### ⚠️ Risks & Red Lines

- Wrong tourniquet placement can cause harm  
- Never exceed your training level  
- Always ask consent and record what you do  

<Callout type="important">
Consent & legal: Always request consent if possible, have a witness, and document care actions for legal and medical handoff.
</Callout>

---

### 🧭 Scenario Drill

> *A volunteer collapses with severe leg bleeding during a march.*  
1. Confirm scene safety  
2. Check consciousness and breathing  
3. Apply pressure or tourniquet  
4. Signal for medic/dispatcher  
5. Document care given
