# 🫂 Crisis Debriefing & Mutual Care
<TrackBadge variant="community-care" />
<TrackBadge variant="pod-leadership" />

<Callout type="info">
⚠️ Crises don’t end when the event does. This module teaches structured, consent-based debriefs that rebuild trust, surface lessons, and support pod resilience.
</Callout>

---

## Overview
Crisis debriefing helps groups process arrests, burnout, exposure, or internal conflict before harm spreads.  
Without it, pods fracture quietly. With it, they recover stronger and more coordinated.  
You’ll learn how to:
- Call a debrief at the right time  
- Facilitate safe, structured reflection  
- Balance honesty with boundaries  
- Reconnect as a pod after trauma  

---

## Learn & Do

### 1. When to Call a Crisis Debrief

| Event | Debrief Needed? |
|-------|------------------|
| Arrest or police threat | ✅ Immediately |
| Raid or surveillance | ✅ Offline preferred |
| Burnout or withdrawal | ✅ Soon as safe |
| Conflict or harm disclosure | ✅ Always |
| Failed or unsafe action | ✅ With structure |

If there’s emotional charge, confusion, or harm, debrief — don’t delay.

---

### 2. Setting Structure and Safety

**Roles**
- **Facilitator:** Keeps time, enforces safety rules  
- **Scribe (optional):** Records themes, not names  
- **Supporter:** Available for 1:1 check-ins  
- **Participants:** Speak from “I”, not “you,” unless consented  

**Ground Rules**
- No blame, no fixing  
- Speak for yourself only  
- No forced sharing or recording  
- Breaks allowed anytime  
- What’s said stays private unless safety demands action  

<Callout type="hint">
For consent and recordkeeping norms, see [Community Consent](/courses/community-consent), [After-Action Data Hygiene](/courses/after-action-data-hygiene), and [Documentation Safety](/courses/documentation-safety).
</Callout>

---

### 3. Sample Debrief Flow
1. 🔐 **Safety Check:** Phones off, secure location  
2. 💬 **Check-In:** “How are you arriving today?”  
3. 🎯 **Purpose:** “We’re here to process, not to assign blame.”  
4. 🧠 **What Happened:** Round-based or journaling first  
5. ⚠️ **What Felt Unsafe or Unclear:** Gather themes  
6. ❤️ **What Support Is Needed:** Offers, requests, rotations  
7. ✅ **Action Agreements:** Pause, rotate, escalate, adapt  
8. 🌱 **Closing:** Appreciation and next check-in time  

If legal exposure occurred, coordinate with [Court Support](/courses/court-support) and [Legal Follow-through & Jail Support](/courses/legal-followthrough-jail-support).

---

### 4. Practicing Mutual Care

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| Ask: “What would support look like?” | Ask: “What’s wrong with you?” |
| Offer options | Force discussion |
| Make space without pressure | Assume silence means safety |
| Keep debrief structured | Trauma-dump in chats |

Mutual care means **presence with boundaries**, not emotional labor without consent.

---

### 5. Signs of a Healthy Debrief
- Everyone invited, nobody forced  
- Each voice heard without interruption  
- Grounding tools nearby (water, fidgets, quiet space)  
- Follow-up check-ins assigned  
- Ops changes discussed separately  

---

### 6. Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Backfires |
|----------|------------------|
| “Moving on” too fast | Unprocessed harm resurfaces later |
| Blaming one person | Misses systemic roots |
| Avoiding hard truths | Normalizes silence |
| Shaming breakdowns | Destroys trust |
| Skipping recovery planning | Leaves pods brittle |

If deeper structural risks appear, pause and review [Collective Safety Protocols](/courses/collective-safety-protocols).

---

### 7. Recovery in Practice
Recovery begins when pods can:
- Speak about events without re-traumatizing  
- Adjust roles, not personalities  
- Re-enter ops safely  
- Honor pacing and rest needs  
- Keep communication open and shame-free  

---

### 8. Recovery Checklist

- [ ] Everyone affected invited to debrief  
- [ ] Each person checked in 1:1  
- [ ] Patterns identified, not just individuals blamed  
- [ ] Clear action items or pauses chosen  
- [ ] Support offers documented or fulfilled  
- [ ] Nobody isolated or shamed  
- [ ] Next follow-up scheduled  

<Callout type="info">
Hold an operations review separately to adjust systems, signals, or device setups.
</Callout>
