## 🧠 Why This Matters

Crises break trust — even if nobody says it.  
If you don’t process them together, you:

- Fracture pods silently  
- Repeat preventable mistakes  
- Burn out your strongest people  
- Increase long-term risk to everyone  

> If the group never recovers, it wasn’t just the system that broke you — it was neglect.

## 🔥 When to Call a Crisis Debrief

| Event | Debrief Needed? |
|-------|------------------|
| Arrest or police threat | ✅ Immediately |
| Raid, surveillance, or targeting | ✅ Yes, offline preferred |
| Burnout, shutdown, or withdrawal | ✅ As soon as safe |
| Conflict between members | ✅ Yes, with boundaries |
| Someone speaks up about harm | ✅ Always |
| Failed or unsafe action | ✅ With caution and care |

---

## 📋 How to Structure a Safe Debrief

### Roles:

- **Facilitator**: Keeps time, ensures safety  
- **Scribe (optional)**: Logs themes, not names  
- **Supporter**: Available for side 1:1 check-ins  
- **Participants**: Speak from “I”, not “you” unless given consent

---

### Ground Rules:

- No blame, no fixing  
- Speak only for yourself  
- No forced participation  
- No recording  
- Breaks allowed anytime  
- What’s said stays in the room (unless harm requires action)

---

## 🧪 Sample Debrief Flow

1. 🔐 **Safety Check**: Phones off, location secure  
2. 💬 **Check-In Round**: “How are you arriving today?”  
3. 🎯 **Purpose**: “We’re here to process X together, not to assign blame.”  
4. 🧠 **What Happened?** (Round-based, or journaling then sharing)  
5. ⚠️ **What Felt Unsafe or Unclear?**  
6. ❤️ **What Support Is Needed Now?**  
7. ✅ **Action Agreement**: If needed — rotate, pause, escalate, repair  
8. 🌱 **Closing**: Appreciation, care offers, next check-in time

## 🫂 Mutual Care ≠ Emotional Labor Dump

You don’t have to be a therapist to care for people.

| Do | Don’t |
|----|------|
| Ask “What would support look like right now?” | Ask “What’s wrong with you?” |
| Offer options, not advice | Push them to talk if they freeze |
| Make space without pressure | Assume silence = safety |
| Debrief with structure | Trauma-dump in group chats |

---

## ❤️ Mutual Care Can Look Like:

- Bringing water, snacks, grounding tools  
- Saying “You don’t need to talk — just be here.”  
- Sitting in silence with someone  
- Offering rest, rides, follow-ups  
- Asking how you might have contributed to harm  
- Checking in days or weeks later  

<Callout type="hint">
Some teams designate a non‑clinical support role for the debrief. See <a href="/courses/care-support">Care & Emotional Support</a>.
</Callout>

## 📉 Mistakes People Make After Crisis

| Mistake | Why It Backfires |
|---------|------------------|
| “Moving on” too fast | Leaves wounds unspoken and festering |
| Blaming or isolating one person | Misses systemic or shared issues |
| Ignoring gut feelings | Leads to repeated harm |
| Shaming breakdowns | Forces people to hide trauma |
| Not naming harm | Leaves survivors doing all the emotional labor |

<Callout type="important">
If the event revealed systemic safety issues, pause operations and revisit <a href="/courses/collective-safety-protocols">Collective Safety Protocols</a> before resuming.
</Callout>

## 🧠 What Recovery Looks Like

Recovery is not “getting over it.”  
It’s when your pod…

- Can talk about what happened without flinching  
- Adjusts structure, not just feelings  
- Feels safe rotating or returning to action  
- Knows who to call next time it goes wrong  
- Doesn’t punish people for needing time

---

## 🔁 Crisis Recovery Checklist

- [ ] Everyone affected was invited into a debrief  
- [ ] At least one pod member has checked in 1:1 with each person  
- [ ] Patterns or breakdowns were identified (not just blamed)  
- [ ] Action items were agreed on (pause, rotate, escalate, adapt)  
- [ ] Support needs were documented or followed up on  
- [ ] No one was isolated or shamed  
- [ ] Next debrief or check-in is scheduled

<Callout type="info">
Schedule a brief ops review separate from the emotional debrief to adjust roles, signals, or devices as needed. Keep it practical and short.
</Callout>
