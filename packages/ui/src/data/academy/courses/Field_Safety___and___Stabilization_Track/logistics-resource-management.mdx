## Overview

Actions and community defense centers can **fail or fracture without solid logistics**.  
Fuel shortages, missing supplies, or lack of fallback plans can leave pods stranded or unsafe.  
This course teaches how to plan, stage, resupply, and protect logistics chains for sustainable operations.

You will learn:
- How to select staging areas
- How to manage rotating supplies
- How to calculate and store fuel safely
- How to build fallback caches
- How to protect logistics from infiltration or surveillance

## Learn & Do

### 🏕 Staging Area Setup

**Criteria**
- Proximity: near enough for resupply, far enough for security.  
- Multiple exits: at least two routes for vehicles and people.  
- Utilities: access to water and electricity when possible.  
- Visibility: low profile from roads and drones.  
- Rotation: avoid reusing the same staging site repeatedly.

<Callout type="important">
OPSEC: Never store exact addresses or cache coordinates in shared systems. Use coded names and offline notes.
</Callout>

### 📦 Supply Chain Management

- **Minimum Stock**
  - Water: 1 gallon/person/day (double in heat)
  - Food: 2 meals/person/8 hours
  - Medical: 1 full kit/10 people
  - PPE: spare gloves, masks, and eyewear per role
- Rotate perishables every 7–14 days
- Vet all donations for tampering

<Callout type="hint">
Track only quantities and condition. Avoid storing donor data. Use paper or encrypted offline tools.
</Callout>

---

### ⛽ Fuel Planning

**Estimation**
- Gas generator: ~1 gal/hour @ half load  
- Cargo van: ~12 MPG with load  

**Storage**
- Use approved containers, stored across multiple sites  
- Keep 50+ feet from heat sources  
- Label clearly and train handlers  

**Alternatives**
- Solar banks, battery kits, or biofuel backup systems

<Callout type="warning">
Fuel = fire risk. Store in ventilated detached areas only.
</Callout>

---

### 🔒 Logistics Security

- Share site info strictly by role
- Rotate routes, drivers, and staging points
- Remove identifiable markings from vehicles
- Ensure at least three trusted people know all key logistics details

<Callout type="info">
ART + Region App Guidance:
- No geofences or public map layers for caches.
- Offline coded maps only.
- Logs retained server-side for abuse response.
- Restrict access via trust-based roles.
</Callout>

---

### 📍 Fallback Logistics

- Mark caches on **offline maps** and **encrypted backups**
- Store supplies in sealed, waterproof, rodent-proof containers
- Refresh every 30 days

<Callout type="hint">
Keep printed backups in sealed envelopes with rotating passphrases. Revoke access after events.
</Callout>

---

### ⚠️ Common Failure Points

| Failure | Prevention |
|----------|-------------|
| Power depletion mid-action | Solar/battery redundancy |
| Overreliance on one site | Two or more fallback hubs |
| Weather damage | Waterproof all storage |
| Lost institutional knowledge | Maintain role-based binder |

<Callout type="important">
Keep a shared binder (paper + offline file) with cache codes, refresh dates, and runner protocols. Avoid personal accounts.
</Callout>

---

### 🚨 Drill Scenario

**Situation:** Primary staging compromised.  
**Action Plan:**
1. Secure sensitive materials  
2. Redirect incoming runs  
3. Notify teams via secure comms  
4. Activate runner rotation  

<Callout type="hint">
Avoid naming new locations in chat. Use coded messages and clear retention timelines.
</Callout>
