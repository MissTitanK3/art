## Overview

Outreach can **strengthen trust or trigger fear** depending on how it’s done.  
Your goal is to **inform, empower, and connect**—not alarm.  
This training gives you structure, tone, and tools to engage safely while protecting both volunteers and community partners.

---

## Learn & Do

### Core Messaging Order
1. **Safety First** – Establish calm and purpose.  
2. **Local Updates** – Ground information in what’s real and near.  
3. **Tools & Demos** – Offer actionable help, not just warnings.  
4. **How They Can Help** – Empower without overloading.  
5. **Unapologetic Close** – End confident and clear about values.

### Practice Scripts
**30-Second Version**  
> “Hi, we’re with Always Ready Tools. ICE has been active nearby, and we want people to know their rights and stay safe. Here’s a flyer and card with reporting options. If you see anything, report through these tools. We look out for each other. Fuck ICE.”

**2-Minute Version**  
> “We’re with Always Ready Tools, helping keep our community safe. ICE has detained people locally—many don’t know it’s happening.  
We’re here to share rights info, safety resources, and alert tools.  
You can help by sharing flyers, staying alert, and reporting safely. Together we can protect one another. Fuck ICE.”

<Callout type="warning">
Avoid overwhelming details. Panic breaks trust. Keep outreach short, confident, and repeatable.
</Callout>

---

## Bailout Protocol

1. **Stay Calm:** Keep tone neutral.  
2. **Soft Exit:** “Understood, we’ll move along. Stay safe.”  
3. **Leave Immediately:** Do not debate or linger.  
4. **Log Interaction Securely:** Record location and behavior for internal review.  
5. **Optional Follow-Up:** Revisit later with a different volunteer if it seemed like misunderstanding.

## Tips for Field Teams

- **Match tone:** Calm for fear, confident for anger.  
- **Rotate roles:** Greeter, demo lead, observer, scout.  
- **Listen first:** Validate lived experiences.  
- **Stay consistent:** Use the same messaging sequence.  
- **Keep logs encrypted:** Outreach notes count as sensitive data.

<Callout type="hint">
Documentation hygiene: Use minimal, time-bound retention.  
Avoid storing raw outreach notes or chat logs in shared drives.  
Use encrypted, access-controlled storage and delete temporary data after review.
</Callout>
