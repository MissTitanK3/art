## Overview

Field operations depend on preparation, clarity, and communication.  
This module outlines how core field roles complement each other — from observation to care to de-escalation — ensuring that every pod can act with safety and purpose.

---

## 👁️ Legal Observer

**Purpose:** Document law enforcement or ICE activity, rights violations, and crowd behavior.  
**Focus:** note-taking, data integrity, non-interference, and evidence security.

<Callout type="warning">
Must complete [Know Your Rights](/courses/kyr-basics) before taking this role.  
Observers **record**, they do **not** intervene.
</Callout>

✅ **Success looks like:** complete, objective notes; accurate timestamps; safe handoff to legal or documentation teams.  
→ [Go to Legal Observer Training](/courses/legal-observer)

---

## 💧 Care Station Support

**Purpose:** Provide grounding, hydration, and emotional aid near action zones.  
**Focus:** accessibility, de-escalation, and trauma-informed peer support.

<Callout type="hint">
Perfect for volunteers who prefer lower-risk, people-focused roles.  
Pairs well with [Mental Health & Resilience Basics](/courses/mental-health-resilience).
</Callout>

✅ **Success looks like:** steady energy, stocked supplies, and calm communication.  
→ [Go to Care Support Training](/courses/care-support)

---

## 🕊️ De-escalation Lead

**Purpose:** Defuse tension, prevent escalation, and protect group stability.  
**Focus:** tone control, body language, and situational awareness.

<Callout type="info">
De-escalators operate through **presence**, not force.  
They focus on tension reduction, not confrontation.
</Callout>

✅ **Success looks like:** calm crowd flow, reduced volatility, safe exits for everyone.  
→ [Go to De-escalation Basics](/courses/deescalation-basics)

---

## 🔒 Security & Comms Hygiene

- Use **Signal** or **ProtonMail** for coordination.
- Strip metadata before sharing media.
- Keep mesh or radio backups ready.
- Never post live locations.
- Share post-action reports only via secure, consent-based channels.

<Callout type="info">
If interpretation is needed, collaborate with  
[Translator Micro-Badge](/courses/translator-micro-badge) or [ASL Interpreter Micro-Badge](/courses/asl-interpreter-micro-badge) holders.  
For movement and supply coordination, review [Runners & Rides](/courses/runners-and-rides).
</Callout>

## Know Before You Go

Each role carries distinct risks and emotional weight.  
You don’t need to fill every role — just do one **well** and know how to hand off effectively.

<Callout type="success">
Solidarity means **coverage through coordination**, not everyone doing everything.  
Focus on your lane, and trust your team to hold theirs.
</Callout>
