# 🧭 Field Role Training Hub
<TrackBadge variant="field-safety" />
<TrackBadge variant="direct-action" />

<Callout type="info">
This module is a hub for training in field deployment roles. Whether you’re observing, supporting, or de-escalating — we’ve got you covered.
</Callout>

<Callout type="hint">
Before taking a field role, complete <a href="/courses/kyr-basics">Know Your Rights (KYR) Basics</a>, review <a href="/courses/documentation-safety">Documentation & Safety</a>, and skim <a href="/courses/after-action-data-hygiene">After‑Action Data Hygiene</a>. If you expect to coordinate via ART, also see <a href="/courses/responding-to-dispatch-calls">Responding to Dispatch Calls</a> and <a href="/courses/dispatch-coordination">Dispatch Coordination</a>.
</Callout>

---

## Legal Observer
Legal Observers monitor law enforcement and ICE activity, take detailed notes, and document rights violations — without interfering or engaging.

<Callout type="warning">
You must complete **Know Your Rights** before taking on this role.
</Callout>

→ [Go to Legal Observer Training](/courses/legal-observer)

---

## Care Station Support
These team members create calming, supportive spaces at the edge of direct actions. They offer water, emotional care, and supplies.

<Callout type="hint">
This role is ideal for people who want to help but don’t want to confront police directly.
</Callout>

→ [Go to Care Support Training](/courses/care-support)

---

## De-escalation Lead
De-escalators are trained in verbal techniques, presence, and tension reduction. They are often front-line but non-confrontational.

<Callout type="info">
De-escalators need to stay cool under pressure and center community safety.
</Callout>

→ [Go to De-escalation Basics](/courses/deescalation-basics)

---

## Tips for All Field Roles

- Stay calm, hydrated, and alert
- Practice your role with others in advance
- Use encrypted comms (Signal, Proton) and minimize sensitive metadata. See <a href="/courses/digital-security-basics">Digital Security Basics</a>.
- Never promise more than you can deliver
- Report back via secure channels

<Callout type="info">
Language access matters. If interpretation is needed, consider teammates with the <a href="/courses/translator-micro-badge">Translator Micro‑badge</a> or <a href="/courses/asl-interpreter-micro-badge">ASL Interpreter Micro‑badge</a>. For handoffs/transport, coordinate with <a href="/courses/runners-and-rides">Runners & Rides</a>.
</Callout>

---

## Know Before You Go

Each of these roles has its own risks and responsibilities. You don’t need to do all of them — but you should do one *well*.

<Callout type="success">
Solidarity means filling roles *together*. Pick one, train deep, and trust your team.
</Callout>
