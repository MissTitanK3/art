## Overview

This guide helps communities stay functional and connected through system failure.  
You’ll learn how to assess risks, build supplies collaboratively, and create redundancy for communication, mobility, and care.

It’s designed for pods — small, trusted groups (2–10 people) that share survival responsibility and emotional grounding.  
See [Create a Pod](/courses/create-a-pod) if you’re starting fresh.

---

## Learn & Do

### Day 1 Quick Wins
<Callout type="success">
Complete three of these in your first session — you’re already 20% more prepared.
</Callout>

- Store **3 days of water** per person (1 gal/day)  
- Write down **emergency contacts** on paper (multilingual if needed)  
- Create a **Signal group** with your pod → [Signal Tips](/courses/signal-tips)  
- Save **KYR cards and hotline numbers** offline → [KYR Basics](/courses/kyr-basics)  
- Identify **one backup power source** (battery, solar, inverter)

> Fast wins reduce anxiety and build trust immediately.

### Trigger Events & Red Lines

These are **signals to activate** your pod plan. Add local ones (fires, floods, grid collapse).

| Trigger Event | What It Signals | Immediate Action |
|----------------|----------------|------------------|
| Grid failure | Infrastructure instability | Charge devices, fill water, gather pod |
| ICE or police raids | Targeted community threat | Activate Signal, protect vulnerable members |
| Internet blackout | State suppression | Shift to radio/offline ops → [Comms Collapse](/courses/comms-collapse) |
| Curfews or militarization | Civil restriction | Confirm bug-in/bug-out plans |
| Water contamination | Utility failure | Filter, ration, communicate |
| Disabled care crisis | Vulnerable support loss | Assign buddy, confirm cold storage |
| Government disinfo | Institutional breakdown | Cross-check reports securely |

<Callout type="hint">
Review triggers each year.  
Add climate-specific and local context.
</Callout>

### The Rule of 3s: Priority Pyramid

| Need | Survival Without | Focus |
|------|------------------|--------|
| Air | 3 minutes | Mask, ventilation, calm |
| Shelter | 3 hours | Warmth, protection |
| Water | 3 days | Storage, filtration |
| Food | 3 weeks | Calories, rotation |
| Connection | 3 months | Maintain contact |
| Meaning | 3 years | Art, joy, culture |

> Logistics without meaning collapses. Meaning sustains readiness.

### Communication Failure Protocol

1. **Mesh tools** → Briar, Bridgefy  
   See [Mesh Networks Intro](/courses/mesh-networks-intro).  
2. **Fallback meet point** → pre-set time/place.  
3. **Radios** → pre-tuned frequencies.  
4. **No contact ≠ no action** → follow plan autonomously.  

<Callout type="important">
Don’t wait for permission. Coordination over perfection.
</Callout>

### Mental Health & Stress Protocol
