## Overview
Digital resilience is about **continuity** when connectivity collapses.  
From disaster response to protest suppression, teams must move from phone-based tools to local, peer-to-peer, or analogue systems instantly.  
You’ll learn how to design, test, and drill a layered communications stack that survives power loss, censorship, or surveillance.

---

## Learn & Do

### 1. Mesh Networking (Local Offline Mesh)
**Use for:** Short-range messaging when Wi-Fi and cell towers fail.

**Tools:**
- Bridgefy (Bluetooth mesh, Android/iOS)  
- Briar (P2P via Wi-Fi or Bluetooth)  
- Serval Mesh (legacy, stable basic text)

**Setup Tips:**
- Identify by **role** not name (e.g., “Medic1”, “Dispatch3”).  
- Preload offline maps (Organic Maps).  
- Test with **airplane mode ON** to confirm offline delivery.

---

### 2. LoRa Networks (Long-Range Text Relay)
**Hardware:** Meshtastic + T-Beam or Heltec (~$250 starter kit)  
**Range:** 1 km urban | 10 km rural  
**Encryption:** Always enable **AES-128**  
**Message Format:**
```
[PRIORITY] [LOCATION] [ACTION]
"URGENT – 5th/Broadway – Need extraction"
"INFO – Water restock SE park"
"CHECK-IN – Safe at base"
```


---

### 3. Radio Hybrid Protocols
Use pre-set **VHF/UHF** channels when digital systems die.

**Codewords Example**
| Code | Meaning |
|------|----------|
| Charlie 4 | Medical backup |
| Foxtrot 1 | Move to fallback site |

- Maintain 30-minute check-ins.  
- Keep at least **3 trained operators per pod**.  
- Assume all traffic is public. Avoid personal or tactical details.

---

### 4. Comms Blackout Drill (Monthly)
1. Disable all internet and cellular.  
2. Deploy 1 LoRa node + 2 mesh relays.  
3. Transmit coordinates and confirm receipt.  
4. Send a mock “Medic to NW corner” request.  
5. Record response times and signal coverage.

Practice movement drills with [Runners & Rides](/courses/runners-and-rides) and your local [Dispatch Calls](/courses/responding-to-dispatch-calls) protocol.

---

### 5. Comms Hierarchy by Duration

| Outage Duration | Tools | Message Scope |
|-----------------|-------|----------------|
| 0–2 hrs | Mesh apps + hand signals | Critical only |
| 2–6 hrs | LoRa + encrypted radio | Scheduled check-ins |
| 6 + hrs | Runners + dropboxes | Pre-coded notes |

<Callout type="info">
Every pod must define one **non-digital fallback**—dropbox, runner, or coded signage.
</Callout>

---

### 6. Equipment Loadout
- ≥ 2 LoRa nodes / 10 people  
- Extra power banks & solar chargers  
- Faraday bags for backups  
- Laminated frequency & code sheets  
- Multi-cable charging kits

---

### 7. Security Audit Checklist
**Radios**
- Encryption active?  
- License compliance (FCC or local)?  

**Mesh**
- Devices non-discoverable?  
- History cleared post-action?  

**Field Ops**
- Antennas concealed?  
- No uncoded radio checks?  

---

### 8. Final Practice Goals
- [ ] Completed 3 + blackout drills  
- [ ] Established analogue fallbacks (chalk marks, paper maps)  
- [ ] Preloaded offline guides (KYR, medic, safety)  
- [ ] Can replace or reflash LoRa nodes in field  

<Callout type="success">
Resilience isn’t gear. It’s **practice under stress**.
</Callout>
