# Heatmap Verification
<TrackBadge variant="tech-comms" />
<TrackBadge variant="direct-action" />

Verifying reports before publishing them to the ART heatmap is a critical responsibility. This guide will walk you through the process of ensuring that public alerts are reliable, timely, and do not put anyone at risk.

<Callout type="info">
Verification is only available to trained, certified users. Your decisions directly impact public visibility and safety.
</Callout>

<Callout type="hint">
Center consent, privacy, and retention. Review <a href="/academy/community-consent">Community Consent</a>, <a href="/academy/data-ethics-pledge">Data Ethics Pledge</a>, <a href="/academy/documentation-safety">Documentation & Safety</a>, and <a href="/academy/after-action-data-hygiene">After‑Action Data Hygiene</a>.
</Callout>

---

## Why Verification Matters

The ART heatmap displays recent ICE or law enforcement presence reported by the community. It helps people:

- Avoid danger zones
- Mobilize support quickly
- Monitor patterns of state violence

> Verified reports reduce false alarms by over **80%** in our network.

<Callout type="warning">
Unverified or misleading reports can create panic, sow mistrust, and put people in harm’s way.
</Callout>

---

## The Verification Pipeline

Every report follows this path:

1. **Automatic Filter**
   - Flags spam, vague entries, or duplicates

2. **Human Review**
   - A certified verifier evaluates the report
   - If clarification is needed, contact the reporter using end‑to‑end encrypted channels only (e.g., Signal)
   
<Callout type="info">
Language access matters. When requesting clarification, consider interpreters via the <a href="/academy/translator-micro-badge">Translator Micro‑badge</a> or <a href="/academy/asl-interpreter-micro-badge">ASL Interpreter Micro‑badge</a>.
</Callout>

3. **Decision Point**
   - ✅ Verify & Publish
   - 🟡 Request Clarification
   - ❌ Mark as Unverifiable
   - 🟢 Escalate to Senior Verifier (for edge cases)

---

## Step-by-Step: Verifying a Report

### 1. Read Carefully

Check for:

- Specific location (address, landmark, cross-street)
- Clear timestamp (check if it’s local time or UTC)
- Descriptions of agents, vehicles, or uniforms
- Attached media, if present

<Callout type="hint">
Avoid retyping or rewriting user content. If minor edits would change meaning, request the reporter to update instead.
</Callout>

### 2. Check for Red Flags

Examples:

- “ICE is everywhere today” (too vague)
- Timestamp is in the future
- Secondhand claims (“my friend said…”)
- Reused or suspicious media

---

## Map & Location Check

Use OpenStreetMap, Google Maps, or our internal tools to:

- Confirm the location is real and specific
- Spot implausible entries (e.g., ocean coordinates)
- Use Street View to validate storefronts or landmarks
- Compare with recent verified reports

<Callout type="warning">
Beware of spoofed coordinates. Look for patterns of GPS anomalies or recycled phrasing.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
If the location is highly sensitive (e.g., shelters), coordinate with a Verified Dispatcher before publishing. See <a href="/academy/dispatch-coordination">Dispatch Coordination</a> and <a href="/academy/responding-to-dispatch-calls">Responding to Dispatch Calls</a>.
</Callout>

## Decision Table

| Action | Use When… |
|--------|-----------|
| ✅ **Verify & Publish** | Meets ART standards, confirmed location/time |
| 🟡 **Request Clarification** | Missing detail but not disqualifying |
| ❌ **Mark as Unverifiable** | Too vague, unverifiable, or misleading |
| 🟢 **Escalate to Senior Verifier** | Suspicious media, repeat reporter, or edge case |

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Rushing urgent reports** without checking facts
- **Trusting dramatic tone** over verifiable data
- **Skipping map review**
- **Modifying report text** instead of requesting edits

---

## Reviewing Media

When reports include photos or videos:

- Confirm alignment with time/place claims
- Reverse-search suspicious images
- Redact license plates or faces when needed
- Never download or redistribute media casually

<Callout type="important">
Media may contain metadata (EXIF) that reveals time or device. Use this ethically and internally only.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
Media handling: ART supports photo attachments. For video, use a secure link (no direct video uploads). Do not reshared raw media; follow <a href="/academy/documentation-safety">Documentation & Safety</a> and keep retention short per <a href="/academy/after-action-data-hygiene">After‑Action Data Hygiene</a>.
</Callout>

## What Counts as Valid?

| Field         | Must Be…                              |
|---------------|----------------------------------------|
| **Location**  | Specific, real, and verifiable         |
| **Time**      | Recent (ideally within 12 hours)       |
| **Observation** | Firsthand or directly witnessed        |
| **Media**     | (Optional) Supports the report claim   |

---

## Verifier Ethics

You are entrusted with sensitive user-submitted data. You must:

- Remain neutral, accurate, and fact-driven
- Uphold the <a href="/academy/data-ethics-pledge">Data Ethics Pledge</a> and practice device/metadata hygiene from <a href="/academy/digital-security-basics">Digital Security Basics</a>
- Reject unverifiable or biased reports—even if “they feel true”

Avoid:

- Editorializing
- Uploading verified content elsewhere
- Letting fear override procedure

<Callout type="warning">
Publishing must not increase risk for targeted people. Balance urgency with consent and privacy. See <a href="/academy/community-consent">Community Consent</a>.
</Callout>

## Example: You Try

> *"ICE at the Shell station on Broadway. 3 unmarked SUVs. Saw them detain someone at 3:00pm."*

✅ Confirm that location exists  
✅ Cross-check with other recent reports  
✅ Match any media to setting and timestamp  
✅ Verify and Publish

---

## Escalation Scenario

> *A report says: “Detention van outside Walmart.” Attached photo shows a random parking lot. Reverse image search shows the photo is from another state.*

🟢 Escalate to Senior Verifier  
📝 Flag as potential disinfo  
📁 Log metadata internally for pattern tracking

---

## Publishing to the Heatmap

Once verified, the report is:

- Added to the public heatmap
- May trigger region‑configured notifications
- Logged for internal review and training (server‑side records; in‑app viewers may be limited)
