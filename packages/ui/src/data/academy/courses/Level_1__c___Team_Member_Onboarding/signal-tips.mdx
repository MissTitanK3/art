## Overview

Signal is free, open source, and end-to-end encrypted.  
It replaces SMS, email, and unprotected chats with a **private, auditable, and proven protocol**.  
Used properly, it protects you from interception — but not from misuse or device seizure.  
This course focuses on both setup and behavior.

---

## Learn & Do

### Why Signal Matters

Signal can:
- **Encrypt everything** (texts, calls, media)
- **Work offline-first** — messages queue and send later
- **Erase trails** with disappearing messages
- **Run without personal accounts** via burner numbers
- **Authenticate trust** through safety number verification

> Encryption only protects content.  
> Your phone, name, or timing data can still expose you.  
> Pair Signal with good **digital hygiene and OPSEC**.

---

## Getting Started

1. **Install Signal**
   - [Android / iOS / Desktop](https://signal.org/download/)

2. **Register with a Burner Number**
   - Use a prepaid SIM or VoIP alias (e.g. MySudo).  
   - Avoid linking to your real identity or long-term number.  
   - Keep the SIM in a dedicated device if possible.

3. **Lock It Down**
   - Enable **Registration Lock PIN** (Settings → Privacy → Signal PIN).  
   - Turn on **Screen Security** to block screenshots.  
   - Set **Disappearing Messages** to 1–6 hours for sensitive chats.  
   - Disable “Show in recent apps” previews.

## Advanced Use for Pods & Dispatch

### 🔄 Linked Devices
You can connect Signal to desktop clients.  
Use **one admin burner number** if your pod shares a dispatch account, and link it only to pod-controlled hardware.  
> Linking personal laptops adds new exposure points — only link trusted, encrypted machines.

---

### 🗂️ Organization & Labeling
- Use emojis to tag pods or functions (🌲 Olympia Pod, 🚑 Medics).  
- Pin up to four key chats.  
- Keep “off-record” channels separate from planning groups.  

---

### 🕵️‍♀️ Disguised Notifications
- Rename Signal in phone settings (e.g., “Calendar”).  
- Disable content previews.  
- On Android, choose neutral icons and disable badges.  

---

### 📦 File & Media Handling
- Signal removes most metadata, but verify before sending.  
- Use **blur** or external scrubbers like **ScrambledExif**.  
- Avoid screenshots of chats or shared locations.  

---

### 🔄 Broadcasting Securely
For dispatch alerts that shouldn’t reveal all recipients:
- Use **Broadcast Lists** (or private forwarding from a dispatcher).  
- Pair with **ART Dispatch** for region-wide alerts.  
- Never mass-add volunteers to groups without consent.

---

## Security Habits

- Verify **Safety Numbers** with trusted contacts (hold → Verify).  
- Avoid chat backups (iCloud/Drive are unencrypted).  
- Restart Signal periodically to clear cache.  
- Rotate burner numbers every 6–12 months.  
- Power off devices at high-risk actions.

<Callout type="warning">
Signal is secure — your habits aren’t.  
Loss, confiscation, or careless sharing can still expose chats.
</Callout>

<Callout type="hint">
Documentation hygiene:  
No ART service stores Signal data or keys.  
Keep logs only if necessary and with consent.  
Delete them after debriefs.
</Callout>

---

## When *Not* to Use Signal

- If your device is at risk of seizure — use a burner.  
- When actions require deniability — use **offline mesh or radio** instead.  
- For long-term archives — export encrypted summaries; never rely on backups.
