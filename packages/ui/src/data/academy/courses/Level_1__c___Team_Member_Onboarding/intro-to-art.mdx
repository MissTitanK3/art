# 🌱 Introduction to ART Network
<TrackBadge variant="default" />

Welcome to the **Always Ready Tools (ART) Network** — a decentralized, abolitionist project protecting and supporting communities facing surveillance, raids, and criminalization.

<Callout type="info">
ART isn’t a single organization.  
It’s a distributed web of people, tools, and tactics for collective safety.
</Callout>

---

## Overview

ART helps communities self-organize when State systems fail to protect them.  
We do this through a mix of digital tools and human networks — **reporting**, **dispatch**, **training**, and **mutual aid** — so people can respond quickly, safely, and without relying on authorities.

---

## Learn & Do

### What Is ART?

ART is a **mutual aid infrastructure** that enables people to:

- 🚨 Report ICE and law-enforcement presence  
- 📟 Dispatch trusted responders to witness and support  
- 🎓 Train volunteers in field and digital safety  
- 🛠 Coordinate care, logistics, and legal follow-through  

The entire system is **secure**, **decentralized**, and **community-controlled** — built by grassroots organizers, not corporations.

---

### Our Core Values

We are:

- **Abolitionist** — dismantling systems of policing, detention, and borders  
- **Decentralized** — no central command, distributed trust  
- **Community-led** — locals define their needs and responses  
- **Security-driven** — safety and encryption are non-negotiable  
- **Mutual-aid rooted** — solidarity, not charity

### Who Makes Up ART

There is no “ART HQ.”  
The network is composed of people in different roles:

| Role | Description |
|------|--------------|
| 🧍‍♀️ **Field Responders** | Witness, observe, and de-escalate events |
| 🧠 **Dispatchers** | Coordinate reports, assign roles, and maintain situational awareness |
| 🛠 **Tool Stewards** | Maintain, secure, and improve ART platforms |
| 💞 **Care Teams** | Provide mutual aid and post-incident support |

Everyone is part of the ecosystem; what matters is **trust, discipline, and readiness**, not credentials.

---

### Roles Within the Network

ART operates through overlapping response layers:

- **Observers** — document ICE/police presence  
- **De-escalators** — verbally intervene to reduce tension  
- **Care Stations** — provide water, rest, and safety  
- **Runners / Rideshare** — move people or supplies  
- **Digital Support** — manage mapping, comms, verification  
- **Legal Coordination** — connect affected people to legal aid  

<Callout type="hint">
Learn more in the [Field Role Training Hub](/courses/field-role-training-hub).
</Callout>

### Trust and Access

Trust is earned through **training and demonstrated accountability**.  
Access expands as members complete certification stages:

- 💬 Role quizzes and scenario evaluations  
- 🔐 Digital security pledges  
- ✅ Verified and admin dispatcher certifications  

<Callout type="info">
Access grows via **trust signatures** and **role-based permissions**.  
See [Admin Tools & Access Roles](/courses/admin-tools) for details.
</Callout>
