# 🔐 Digital Security Basics
<TrackBadge variant="tech-comms" />
<TrackBadge variant="logistics" />

Digital security is not optional — it is the frontline of community safety.  
From the first report to the final debrief, your device and data can become targets.  
This course gives you the **habits and tools** to protect yourself, your pod, and the people we defend.

## Overview

Digital security means minimizing exposure and maximizing resilience.  
Good tools are part of it — but **discipline and culture** matter more.  
Everyone in the ART network should be able to communicate securely, even when systems fail.

---

## Learn & Do

### Core Practices

At minimum, every ART member should use:

- **📱 Signal** for messaging  
- **📧 Proton Mail** for encrypted email  
- **📷 Metadata removal** for photos and videos  
- **🛜 VPNs or local-only networks** (e.g., Tailscale) for coordination  

Start simple. Practice these together as team habits.

---

### Why Signal?

Signal is the backbone of ART communication because it is:

- **End-to-end encrypted** (no third party can read your messages)  
- **Open-source and audited** by independent researchers  
- Used globally by activists, journalists, and human rights defenders  

Use Signal for:
- Dispatch and alert coordination  
- Role assignment  
- Internal updates  
- Sensitive check-ins  

<Callout type="rabbit-hole">
💬 Learn how the Signal protocol protects your data.

</Callout>

---

### Why Proton Mail?

Proton Mail provides privacy without surveillance.

- Based in Switzerland, outside U.S. jurisdiction  
- End-to-end encrypted mail and drive  
- No ad tracking or metadata harvesting  

Only use Proton accounts for ART-related communication — keep Gmail and other cloud systems separate.

---

### Stripping Metadata from Media

Photos and videos often include invisible data like:
- GPS coordinates  
- Device model  
- Timestamps  

That metadata can expose people and locations.

**Safe methods:**
- Use “Export As” or “Save for Web” before sharing  
- Use **ScrambledExif (Android)** or **Metapho (iOS)**  
- Send via **Signal**, which removes metadata automatically  

<Callout type="hint">
Before sharing, ask:  
**“Could this image expose someone’s identity or location?”**
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
If you must store sensitive media, use encrypted drives and delete after review per [After-Action Data Hygiene](/courses/after-action-data-hygiene).
</Callout>

---

### What *Not* to Do

| ❌ Don’t… | Because… |
|-----------|-----------|
| Screenshot chat logs | Even encrypted chats leak context |
| Upload to Google Drive or YouTube | Metadata and IP exposure |
| Use unencrypted texting for logistics | SMS can be intercepted easily |

> Assume nothing is private unless encrypted and verified.

---

### Security Habits for the Field

- Lock your device with a passcode (no biometrics)  
- Disable cloud backups for Signal and Proton  
- Store notes in encrypted apps (Standard Notes, Proton Drive)  
- Practice working offline for when networks fail  

<Callout type="info">
During high-risk ops, use a separate device.  
See [Burner Phones & Security Culture](/courses/burner-phones-security-culture) for compartmentalization.
</Callout>
