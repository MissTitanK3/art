# 🛠️ Deploying a Community Defense Center
<TrackBadge variant="logistics" />
<TrackBadge variant="pod-leadership" />

<Callout type="info">
A **Community Defense Center (CDC)** is a mobile mutual aid hub — deployable at protests, food drops, or block events. It turns public space into community infrastructure: training, coordination, and direct support.

</Callout>

<Callout type="hint">
Coordinate roles and signals in advance via [Responding to Dispatch Calls](/courses/responding-to-dispatch-calls) and [Runners & Rides](/courses/runners-and-rides).
</Callout>

## Why It Matters

A CDC makes community safety visible and practical.  
It bridges people from “interested” to “involved” by providing tools, training, and a local contact point.

A well-run CDC:
- Demonstrates organized presence  
- Connects pods to real neighbors  
- Reduces fear and misinformation  
- Builds the public’s confidence in self-defense networks  

---

## CDC Quick Start

- Choose a visible, low-risk location  
- Bring signage, QR codes, KYR cards, and signup forms  
- Assign clear roles: Outreach, Safety, Logistics  
- Use short openers: “Want to learn to protect your community in five minutes?”  
- Log interest and follow up after deployment  

<Callout type="info">
If connectivity is weak, prepare offline systems using [App-Free Tactics](/courses/app-free-tactics), [Comms Collapse](/courses/comms-collapse), and [Mesh Networks Intro](/courses/mesh-networks-intro).
</Callout>

## Core Goals

A CDC is more than a booth — it is a **public node of collective defense**.  
Its purpose:
- Recruit and onboard to Watch, Dispatch, and Academy  
- Share KYR and safety resources  
- Form or strengthen pods  
- Provide mutual aid or communication support  
- Show that the community protects itself

## Setup Tiers

| Tier | Setup | Ideal Use |
|------|--------|------------|
| **Micro** | Backpack + flyers | Door-to-door or small events |
| **Mid** | Table + signage | Markets, rallies, community fairs |
| **Full** | Tent + tech + power | Mass gatherings, full operations |

---

## Gear Checklist

### Essentials
- Table or flat surface  
- “Community Defense” signage  
- Flyers with QR codes (Watch, Dispatch, Academy)  
- Pod builder and KYR materials  
- Stickers, pens, clipboards  
- One person trained in safety or de-escalation  

### Optional Tech
- Tablet/laptop with hotspot  
- Charging power bank or solar  
- Radios or scanner (per legal norms)  

<Callout type="warning">
Never show sensitive data in public demos. Review [Documentation Safety](/courses/documentation-safety).
</Callout>

## Choosing a Location

| Priority | Look For | Avoid |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Visibility | Foot traffic, known spaces | Dead zones, alleys |
| Accessibility | ADA, shade, restrooms | Crowded choke points |
| Safety | Known allies nearby | Cop or ICE presence |
| Power | Solar or nearby outlet | Gas generators |
| Connection | Familiar community hubs | Corporate or hostile areas |

<Callout type="hint">
Ask: *Who needs this space most — and where are they already gathering?*
</Callout>

## Deployment Models

### Micro (Backpack)
For protests or street patrols.  
Carry laminated QR cards, zines, and pocket KYR guides.  
Use a 2-minute script:  
> “Want to know how to keep neighbors safe from ICE without calling the cops?”

### Mid (Table)
For fairs or markets.  
Organize table zones:  
- Left: Tool demo (Watch, Dispatch)  
- Center: Rapid Academy signup  
- Right: Zines and pod cards  

### Full (Tent)
For full operations.  
Arrange stations around the edges:
1. 🧭 Tool Table  
2. 📚 Rapid Academy  
3. 🛡️ KYR Defense  
4. 🌱 Pod Builder  

Keep the center open for crew and visitors.

---

## CDC Safety Protocols

- Create a safety plan and comms channel before setup  
- Rotate staff and check heat/hydration  
- Never collect unnecessary personal info  
- Frame the space around care, not confrontation  

<Callout type="warning">
Never leave the CDC unattended. If you must move, pack and clear all materials.
</Callout>

## Follow-Up System

- Collect minimal info: first name, Signal/email, zip  
- Tag interests (Watch, Dispatch, Academy)  
- Automate follow-ups with Make.com or Supabase  
- Review retention practices per [After-Action Data Hygiene](/courses/after-action-data-hygiene)

## Simulation Training

Before live deployment:
1. Assign roles  
2. Practice setup & breakdown  
3. Run mock interactions  
4. Debrief as a team  

Include a **de-escalation practice** block using [Bystander Support Tactics](/courses/bystander-support) and [Care & Emotional Support](/courses/care-support).

---

## De-escalation Example

If challenged with hostility (e.g., “Why defend undocumented people?”), respond calmly:
> “Everyone deserves safety and dignity — no exceptions.”

---

## CDC Stamp Program

Encourage return participation:
- Visiting CDC = 1 stamp  
- Joining a pod = 1 stamp  
- Completing training = 1 stamp  
- Serving as role lead = 1 stamp  

Five stamps = **Verified Community Defender**.
