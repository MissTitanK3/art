# 🎥 Pod Creator Video Content Framework
<TrackBadge variant="pod-leadership" />

<Callout type="info">
Language access: Pair this guide with [Translator Micro-Badge](/courses/translator-micro-badge) and [ASL Interpreter Micro-Badge](/courses/asl-interpreter-micro-badge).  
Use subtitles, transcripts, or interpreters to make videos accessible across languages and abilities.
</Callout>

This framework provides **30 adaptable video prompts** to help you **recruit, educate, and organize** as a Pod Creator.  
Use them individually, in sequence, or grouped into short campaigns. Each clip can strengthen local trust and regional visibility without relying on centralized infrastructure.

---

## 🔹 ROOTS & REASON
> Build legitimacy and emotional grounding for why pods exist.

1. **Why I’m Creating a Pod** — your motivation and local context.  
2. **What a Pod Actually Is** — simple breakdown: *not an app, a trust cell.*  
3. **When the System Fails, We Show Up** — real or hypothetical story of action.  
4. **Start With Two People** — normalize micro-beginnings.  
5. **Pods Are Not Branches** — clarify decentralized values.  
6. **We Learn Together** — model collective learning and humility.  

---

## 🔹 TOOLKIT & SETUP
> Teach practical setup and safety.

7. **How to Set Up a Signal Pod** — step-by-step visual guide.  
8. **Quick Tour of ART** — link Dispatch, Watch, and Academy.  
9. **What Is a General Pod?** — entry-level, not hierarchical.  
10. **Why We Don’t Use Flashy Names** — OPSEC and calm visibility.  
11. **Pod Agreements and Boundaries** — define hours, escalation, rest.  
12. **Pod Roles Breakdown** — Stabilizer, Witness, Driver, Comms, Coordinator.  

---

## 🔹 RECRUITMENT & INVITATION
> Grow through values-based messaging.

13. **Who I’m Looking For Locally** — invite by geography and trust.  
14. **3 Ways to Plug In** — small, medium, full involvement tiers.  
15. **Pod Work Isn’t Just for Protesters** — broaden who’s included.  
16. **Quiet Work Counts** — validate offline support roles.  
17. **DM Me — I’ll Help You Start Yours** — inter-pod solidarity.  
18. **I’m Not a Leader, I’m a Link** — emphasize horizontality.  

---

## 🔹 CONNECTION & CULTURE
> Center community care and conflict resilience.

19. **Why Local Is Powerful** — emphasize autonomy and presence.  
20. **Meet the Pod (or Dream Team)** — humanize your network.  
21. **What’s Already Happening Here** — weave in local allies.  
22. **Handling Conflict or Confusion** — demonstrate repair over blame.  
23. **Pod Moments That Give Me Hope** — small, meaningful wins.  
24. **We’re Not Ready — And That’s Okay** — normalize imperfection.  

---

## 🔹 VISION & EXPANSION
> Inspire replication and regional growth.

25. **What If Every City Had a Pod?** — collective imagination.  
26. **Mapping Coverage Gaps** — use [Pod Directory](/courses/pod-directory).  
27. **Connecting with the Network** — “No HQ. Just trust.”  
28. **How My Pod Is Growing** — milestone storytelling.  
29. **What I Need Help With** — call for translators, medics, etc.  
30. **From One Person to a Node in the Network** — model progression.

## ⚖️ Ethical & Legal Considerations

<Callout type="important">
- Always get verbal or written consent before sharing stories or images.  
- Blur faces or voices if discussing sensitive events.  
- Delete raw media after editing or publication.  
- Never include minors, undocumented individuals, or trauma scenes without explicit, informed consent.  
Pair this with [Documentation Safety](/courses/documentation-safety), [Data Ethics Pledge](/courses/data-ethics-pledge), and [After-Action Data Hygiene](/courses/after-action-data-hygiene).
</Callout>
