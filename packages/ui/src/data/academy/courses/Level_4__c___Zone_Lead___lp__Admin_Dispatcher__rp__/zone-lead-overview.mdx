## 🧭 Overview

Zone Leads act as **network stewards**, ensuring pods and regions remain aligned, ethical, and functional during high-stress operations or large-scale crises.

Training emphasizes:
- Strategic coordination without centralization  
- Trust signature and key management  
- Data governance and privacy law equivalence  
- Ethical inter-region diplomacy  
- Offline continuity and crisis escalation protocols  

Each course builds on the previous one, moving from technical administration to multi-zone strategy.

## 🧠 Skill Outcomes

After completing this track, you can:

- Administer and audit a **regional node** safely.  
- Rotate keys and validate trust signatures across regions.  
- Design **data minimization** and privacy compliance workflows.  
- Lead multi-pod coordination during crises.  
- Represent your zone in **cross-region federations** ethically.  

> “True leadership in decentralized systems is invisible — you know it’s working when the network runs itself.”
