## 🧭 Overview
Decentralized systems thrive on trust and interoperability.  
Cross-zone coordination ensures that when crises expand beyond one region, pods can **share resources, data, and strategy** safely and efficiently.  
This module outlines how to build and maintain that federation ethically and technically.

> “We don’t scale by control — we scale by cooperation.”

---

## 🧩 Learn & Do

### 1. Federation Fundamentals
Each zone remains autonomous but interoperable.  
Effective federation depends on:

- **Mutual recognition:** zones verify each other via signed keys.  
- **Shared protocol:** same message formats, database schemas, and role definitions.  
- **Redundancy:** no single zone controls coordination servers.  
- **Graceful isolation:** any zone can disconnect without disrupting others.

<Callout type="hint">
Autonomy is protection. Coordination is amplification.
</Callout>

---

### 2. Global Readiness Framework
Establish a **multi-zone incident protocol** that defines:
- Activation thresholds (e.g., disaster, mass raid, or blackout).  
- Contact escalation tree (who reaches out to whom, by what channel).  
- Resource and logistics pooling methods.  
- Verification flow for incoming alerts.  
- Cross-zone debrief schedule and secure report handling.

Use offline-first tools (PocketServer nodes, encrypted bundles) for continuity under comms loss.

---

### 3. Secure Inter-Zone Communication
- Use **signed and timestamped** messages between dispatch servers.  
- Employ **rotating encryption keys** at least every 90 days.  
- Maintain **cross-zone key registries** using hashed fingerprints, not raw keys.  
- Designate **two trusted human verifiers** per zone to confirm authenticity of new nodes.  
- Log verifications offline, never in shared cloud systems.

<Callout type="warning">
Never forward other zones’ data without explicit consent.  
Each dataset belongs to its origin community.
</Callout>

---

### 4. Shared Resource Planning
Coordinate surplus and shortage transparently:

| Resource Type | Example | Exchange Method |
|----------------|----------|----------------|
| Physical | Fuel, water, radios | Mutual-aid convoys or courier pods |
| Digital | Training kits, map data | Encrypted file bundles |
| Human | Trainers, techs, medics | Temporary cross-zone deployment |
| Financial | Emergency funds | Pre-approved micro-grants between treasuries |

Use **ledger mirrors** or **trust signatures** to validate transfers without central accounts.

---

### 5. Crisis Synchronization Protocol
When a crisis spans zones:
1. Confirm source zone and affected regions.  
2. Establish encrypted comms bridge (LoRa, radio, or relay server).  
3. Assign a neutral liaison pod for information flow.  
4. Share situation summaries every 6 hours.  
5. End coordination with joint AAR and trust re-verification.

> Synchronization ≠ subordination.

---

### 6. Inter-Zone Ethics Charter
Maintain solidarity without overreach by codifying:

- Informed consent for data sharing and joint ops.  
- Equal representation in decision-making.  
- Transparency on funding and resource flow.  
- Zero tolerance for exploitation or surveillance partnerships.  
- Conflict resolution through mediation, not exposure.

<Callout type="important">
Global coordination must never evolve into hierarchy.  
Structure protects autonomy — it should never replace it.
</Callout>

---

### 7. Resilience Benchmarks
Evaluate readiness quarterly:

| Category | Benchmark | Target |
|-----------|------------|--------|
| **Comms Continuity** | Can sustain offline ops 72h | ✅ |
| **Trust Sync** | All zone keys verified & current | ✅ |
| **Redundant Leadership** | 2+ deputies per region | ✅ |
| **Cross-Zone Backup** | Secure data relay active | ✅ |
| **Care Protocols** | Debrief cycle & trauma support | ✅ |

Document audits privately and rotate reviewers between zones to maintain impartiality.
