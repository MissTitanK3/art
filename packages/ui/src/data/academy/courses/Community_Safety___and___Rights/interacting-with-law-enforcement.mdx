# ⚖️ Interacting with Law Enforcement
<TrackBadge variant="observation-legal" />
<TrackBadge variant="field-safety" />

<Callout type="info">
Use clear, grounded language. This course prepares you to respond calmly and lawfully when encountering police or ICE, whether you’re approached, observing, or providing support.  
Applies to U.S. law—always verify local regulations and connect with legal observers when possible.
</Callout>

---

## Overview
Law enforcement encounters are high-stress and high-risk. Preparation and composure can prevent escalation and reduce harm.  
You’ll learn how to assert your rights, maintain composure, protect vulnerable people, and document responsibly without endangering yourself or others.

---

## Learn & Do

### 1. Core Principles
- **Stay calm:** Controlled breath, relaxed posture, minimal motion.  
- **Assert rights clearly:** Silence and consent boundaries protect you.  
- **De-escalate:** The goal is to cool tension, not win arguments.  
- **Protect others:** Avoid drawing attention to vulnerable individuals.  
- **Document carefully:** Footage is evidence — treat it as such.

---

### 2. Know Your Rights

| Right | Meaning |
|-------|----------|
| **Remain Silent** | You don’t have to answer police or ICE questions. |
| **Request a Lawyer** | Say so and stop talking immediately. |
| **Refuse Search** | You can decline bag, car, or phone searches. |
| **Film in Public** | You may record officials in public places. |
| **Leave if Free** | Ask, “Am I being detained, or am I free to go?” |

<Callout type="warning">
Asserting rights can’t guarantee safety. Assess your surroundings and balance assertiveness with caution.
</Callout>

---

### 3. Police vs. ICE

| Agency | Focus | Common Tactics | Legal Limits |
|--------|--------|----------------|---------------|
| **ICE** | Immigration enforcement | Raids, traffic stops | No home entry without judicial warrant |
| **Police** | Criminal enforcement | Patrols, arrests | Must have reasonable suspicion to detain |

**Always ask:** *“Can you slide the warrant under the door?”* — only a **judicial warrant** (signed by a judge) grants entry.

---

### 4. If You’re Approached
1. Breathe. Don’t run or argue.  
2. Ask: *“Am I being detained?”*  
   - If yes → Remain silent, ask for a lawyer.  
   - If no → Walk away calmly.  
3. Say: *“I do not consent to a search.”*  
4. Avoid sudden movement or raised tone.  

✅ You’re only required to show ID when driving or in states with stop-and-identify laws.

---

### 5. If You Witness an Encounter
- Record from a safe distance. Narrate time, location, agent description.  
- Avoid physical interference.  
- Use team codes (e.g., “Blue umbrellas up”).  
- Share securely via encrypted channels.

---

### 6. De-escalation Cues
**Phrases:**  
- “I’m observing for everyone’s safety.”  
- “We’re staying calm and legal here.”  
- “Am I free to go?”  

**Body:**  
- Palms visible, movements slow.  
- Step aside, not forward.  
- Maintain even tone.

<Callout type="tip">
Avoid combative phrases like “You’re violating my rights.” Use neutral, factual language instead.
</Callout>

---

### 7. Role-Specific Guidance

**Legal Observers:**  
- Remain neutral. Record badge numbers, timestamps, statements.

**Field Support / Marshals:**  
- Speak calmly. Create buffer space. Support others’ grounding.

**Runners / Techs:**  
- Use encrypted storage or burner phones.  
- Upload immediately to secure storage (never social media).  

**Medics:**  
- Ask for consent before touch.  
- Record observations, not assumptions.  
- Use implied consent only for unconscious or critical cases.

<Callout type="warning">
Balance care with consent. Physical contact must be justified by need or explicit permission.
</Callout>

---

### 8. Home & Raid Encounters
Never open the door unless shown a **judicial warrant**.  
ICE often presents **administrative warrants** — these do **not** permit entry.

**Script:**  
> “I do not consent to entry. Please slide the warrant under the door.”

<Callout type="hint">
Harden devices and minimize stored data before travel or border crossing. See <a href="/courses/digital-security-basics">Digital Security Basics</a> and <a href="/courses/burner-phones-security-culture">Burner Phones & Security Culture</a>.
</Callout>

---

### 9. Grounding Under Stress
If you feel panic or freeze:
- Grip an object (belt loop, necklace).  
- Do the **5-4-3-2-1** method: 5 things you see → 1 you taste.  
- Say silently: *“I am here. I am safe.”*  
Take time to reset — mental steadiness is safety.

---

### 10. If It Escalates
- Don’t resist. Say:  
  > “I do not consent. I want a lawyer.”  
- Stay silent afterward.  
- Don’t sign documents.  
- Memorize or write your legal contact on your arm.  
- If arrested, notify dispatch and legal observers as soon as possible.
