# 🔌 Mesh Network Hardware & Power Guide
<TrackBadge variant="tech-comms" />

<Callout type="info">

This guide lists **recommended gear** for running Meshtastic networks from small pods (3–15 nodes) up to **large, multi-day deployments (15–100+ nodes)**. Prices are typical as of 2025 and may vary by supplier.

## Overview
This guide helps pods choose reliable and safe hardware for Meshtastic mesh networks.  
You’ll learn how to select devices, antennas, and power systems to match the scale of your deployment, from a handful of handheld nodes to large regional coverage networks.

---

## Learn & Do

### 1. Core Meshtastic Devices
| Device | Role | Typical Price | Notes |
|--------|------|---------------|-------|
| **LilyGO T-Beam V1.1** | Standard handheld node | $60–$80 | Built-in GPS, USB-C, long range (5–10 km). Best for most pod members. |
| **Heltec V3 LoRa** | Compact node/relay | $35–$50 | No GPS, good for scouts or hidden stationary relays. |
| **RAK WisBlock / ESP32 Relay** | Fixed coverage relay | $80–$120 | Needs steady power (AC or solar). Ideal for rooftops or permanent installs. |

<Callout type="hint">
Each pod should have at least **one relay-capable node** for consistent coverage.
</Callout>

---

### 2. Antennas & Range Boosters
| Antenna Type | Use Case | Price Range | Range Impact |
|---------------|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| **3–5 dBi whip** | Handheld upgrades | $10–$20 | Balanced for urban pods. |
| **6–9 dBi directional (Yagi/panel)** | Rooftop relays | $20–$50 | Extends rural range to 15+ km. |
| **Mag-mount antennas** | Vehicle relays | $25–$40 | Strong mobile performance. |

**Tips:**
- Keep antennas vertical and clear of metal.
- Use tripod mounts or housings for rooftops.

---

### 3. Battery Packs & Solar
**For Pod Members:**
- 10,000–20,000 mAh USB-C bank ($20–$40) → 12–24 hrs runtime.
- Carry 2 per active member.

**For Relays:**
- 20,000–50,000 mAh banks ($50–$120).
- 20–40 W solar panels ($60–$150) for daytime charging.

### 4. Accessories & Spares
- Weatherproof cases (IP65+) – $20–$60  
- USB-C multi-chargers (5–10 port) – $25–$60  
- Quick-disconnect mounts – $20–$40  
- Code labels (no real names)  
- Reinforced USB-C cables – $10–$15  

<Callout type="hint">
Avoid storing raw device logs or inventory in shared apps.  
Prefer encrypted, access-controlled notes and delete temporary files after review.
</Callout>

---

### 5. Cost Planning
**Small Pod (5–8 Nodes)** → ~$750–$850  
**Medium Pod (10–15 Nodes)** → ~$1,500–$1,700  
**Large Network (30–50 Nodes)** → ~$2,500–$4,000  
Include spare relays, extra batteries, and dedicated solar kits for resilience.

---

### 6. Sourcing & Legal Basics
- Use **official Meshtastic forums/GitHub** for vendors.  
- Ensure 915 MHz compatibility (US use).  
- Avoid unverified resellers.  
- Always buy extra cables and power banks.  

---

### 7. Deployment Checklist
- [ ] Spare battery and tested antenna for each node  
- [ ] Weatherproof relays with backup power  
- [ ] Solar panels in 6+ hr sunlight  
- [ ] Devices labeled with code names only  
- [ ] At least 1 spare relay and 2 spare power banks per pod  

<Callout type="warning">
Respect safety, consent, and privacy. Always get permission before sharing device or location data. Pair this with Documentation Safety, Data Ethics Pledge, and Legal Observer courses.
</Callout>
