# 🎓 Training the Trainers
<TrackBadge variant="pod-leadership" />
<TrackBadge variant="movement-strategy" />

<Callout type="info">
**Level 4: Zone Lead (Admin Dispatcher)**  
Prepares trainers to **teach Academy content**, **mentor new members**, and **build redundancy** so pods scale knowledge while staying consistent and secure.
</Callout>

---

## Why It Matters

Movements fail when knowledge bottlenecks.  
Strong networks need **distributed teaching capacity** and **consistent, trauma-aware instruction**.  
Trainers preserve continuity through turnover, repression, or burnout.

---

## What You'll Learn

1. **Lesson Planning Tools** – Build and adapt accessible lessons.  
2. **Peer Mentoring** – Deliver feedback and develop co-trainers.  
3. **Version Control** – Teach aligned content across pods.  
4. **Training Modalities** – Facilitate online, in-person, or hybrid classes.  
5. **Trainer Pathway** – Certify new trainers and prevent skill loss.

---

## Curriculum Delivery Tools

- **Lesson Templates:** 30–60 min format with learning goals, key ideas, and wrap-up questions.  
- **Adaptation Guides:**  
  - Adjust for learning style, culture, or access.  
  - Offer printed, captioned, or simplified formats.  
- **Assessment Rubrics:** Track participant comprehension and trainer performance.

<Callout type="hint">
All official templates and guides are stored in the **Academy Shared Drive**.  
Always verify version before teaching.
</Callout>

---

## Peer Mentoring & Feedback

- Run short **check-ins and reflections** after each session.  
- Use **Keep / Start / Stop** or “I notice / I wonder” feedback models.  
- Pair experienced and emerging trainers for redundancy.  
- Document insights briefly in encrypted logs or shared drives.

---

## Consistency & Version Control

- Always teach the **latest certified version**.  
- Non-negotiables: security, ethics, and legal guidance.  
- Adaptable: regional examples or language localization.  
- Document any deviations and submit to Zone Leads for review.

---

## Training Modalities

### Hybrid & Online
- Preferred: **Jitsi**, **Signal video**, or **Matrix/Element**.  
- Disable cloud recording or AI transcription.  
- Use breakout groups or chat prompts to retain engagement.

### Field & Offline
- Use **printed modules**, **signal flags**, or **buddy systems**.  
- Teach near quiet public areas or safehouses.  
- Have **safety roles** for lookout and de-escalation.  
- Maintain awareness of nearby surveillance or police.

## Quick Action Steps

1. Review all modules in your teaching track.  
2. Customize lessons using templates and accessibility guides.  
3. Co-train whenever possible.  
4. Debrief every session and identify potential new trainers.  
5. Log teaching updates in regional records (no PII).

---

## Risks & Red Lines

| Risk | Prevention |
|------|-------------|
| Burnout | Limit to 2 major trainings / month; 3-month break yearly |
| Bottlenecks | Always have co-trainers and backups |
| Quality drift | Maintain version control and audits |
| Security lapses | Avoid unsanctioned uploads or recordings |

---

## Scenario Drill

*A hybrid class loses internet mid-lesson and tension rises.*  
1. Switch to printed handouts or offline practice.  
2. Assign breakout exercises to maintain focus.  
3. Resume digital connection calmly without apology loops.
