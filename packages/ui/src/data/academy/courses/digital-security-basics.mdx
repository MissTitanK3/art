# 🔐 Digital Security Basics
<TrackBadge variant="tech-comms" />
<TrackBadge variant="logistics" />


Digital security isn’t a nice-to-have — it’s the frontline of community safety. From the moment a report is made to the time a responder takes action, our data can be targeted.

This module gives you the tools and habits needed to protect yourself, your team, and the people we show up for.

## Core Practices

At a minimum, everyone participating in ART field or digital roles should be using:

- **📱 Signal** for all messaging  
- **📧 Proton Mail** for secure email  
- **📷 Metadata removal** from all shared photos or videos  
- **🛜 VPNs** or local-only tools like **Tailscale** for coordination

If any of these are new to you — don’t stress. Start where you are. Level up together.

---

## Why Signal?

Signal is:

- **End-to-end encrypted** (even Signal can’t read your messages)
- **Open-source and vetted** by privacy orgs
- Used by everyone from journalists to dissidents to ART teams

Use Signal for:

- Dispatch alerts  
- Role assignment  
- Internal check-ins  
- Sharing sensitive observations

## Why Proton?

Proton Mail is a secure email service based in Switzerland (outside U.S. jurisdiction) that offers:

- End-to-end encrypted inboxes  
- No ads, tracking, or metadata harvesting  
- Calendar + Drive options for future collaboration

Only use Proton accounts for any ART-related messages, photos, or docs. Keep Gmail out of it.

---

## Stripping Metadata from Media

Every photo you take includes hidden data like:

- Location (GPS)
- Timestamp
- Device info

That data can expose people — or places they’re protecting.

### Easy Ways to Remove Metadata:

- Use **built-in “Export As”** or “Save for Web” on mobile  
- Use apps like **ScrambledExif (Android)** or **Metapho (iOS)**  
- If needed: send via Signal, which strips metadata automatically

<Callout type="hint">
Before sharing a photo or video, ask:  
**“Could this expose someone’s location, face, or phone info?”**
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
If you must retain sensitive media, prefer encrypted storage, keep retention short, and follow <a href="/academy/after-action-data-hygiene">After‑Action Data Hygiene</a>.
</Callout>

---

## What *Not* to Do

- ❌ Don’t share screenshots of chat logs  
- ❌ Don’t upload videos to public Google Drives or YouTube  
- ❌ Don’t use unencrypted texting for field logistics

Even “private” groups can be subpoenaed or leaked. Assume nothing is safe unless it’s encrypted.

---

## Security Habits for the Field

- ✅ Lock your device with a passcode (not fingerprint or face unlock)
- ✅ Turn off auto cloud backups for Signal and Proton
- ✅ Use encrypted storage for notes or observations (Standard Notes, Proton Drive)
- ✅ Practice offline mode in case of no signal
