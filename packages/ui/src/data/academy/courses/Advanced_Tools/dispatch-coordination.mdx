## Overview

Coordinating responders in urgent, high-stakes environments requires calm judgment, clear tools, and earned trust.  
This course prepares you to use the ART Dispatch platform to activate field support, maintain situational awareness, and uphold community safety.

Volunteers who complete this course become certified dispatchers able to lead response coordination during field operations.

---

## Learn & Do

### What Is Dispatch?
Dispatch is the real-time coordination of field teams in response to verified threats, arrests, and other state actions.  
Dispatchers act as:
- Signal routers  
- Trust anchors  
- Strategy filters  
- Emotional stabilizers  

---

### Core Responsibilities

| Duty | Description |
|------|--------------|
| **Receive reports** | Monitor and verify incoming ICE or law enforcement sightings |
| **Activate roles** | Assign medics, legal observers, and de-escalators |
| **Track and update** | Maintain real-time visibility of each dispatch |
| **Protect info** | Handle encrypted reports responsibly |
| **Debrief & learn** | Log outcomes, assess gaps, update playbooks |

---

### Dispatcher Progression Path

| Level | What They Can Do | Requirements |
|--------|------------------|---------------|
| 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Team Member** | Observe alerts, submit reports, attend intro calls | Complete orientation |
| 🟢 **Basic Dispatcher** | View internal notes, assist coordination | Qualified in Community Watch, Reporting, and Ethics |
| ✅ **Verified Dispatcher** | Create/close dispatches, assign roles, contact teams | Certified dispatcher + shadowed shift |
| 🔒 **Admin Dispatcher** | Coordinate shifts, override dispatches, audit logs | Nominated by team + advanced trust certification |

<Callout type="info">
Access grows with demonstrated accountability. Trust is earned through consistency, not speed.
</Callout>

---

### Dispatcher Access Levels

| Level | Capabilities |
|--------|--------------|
| 🟢 Basic | View active dispatches, assist verified dispatchers |
| ✅ Verified | Full coordination rights: assign roles, update logs, contact teams |
| 🔒 Admin | Manage users, override errors, lead training, review logs |

---

### Dispatch Lifecycle

1. **Report submitted** — May be encrypted. Await verification.  
2. **Dispatcher triage** — Confirm credibility and identify coverage needs.  
3. **Team roles filled** — Choose trusted volunteers based on risk, skill, and proximity.  
4. **Live coordination** — Monitor and relay ground updates.  
5. **Close & archive** — Final status, AAR, and audit log updates.

<Callout type="success">
You are never alone. Ask for admin review or backup when uncertain.
</Callout>

---

### Role Assignment

Balance:
- ⚠️ Risk level  
- ✅ Skills and certifications  
- 🕒 Availability  
- 📍 Geography  
- 🏷️ Identity tags (language, trauma-informed care, etc.)

<Callout type="warning">
Avoid assigning based on familiarity. Match volunteers to needs, not comfort.
</Callout>

### Encryption & Safety

Sensitive information should **always** be encrypted before it enters digital systems.  
If you must send decryption keys or sensitive context, use controlled, time-limited methods.

---

#### Safe Ways to Send Encrypted Information

1. **Signal (preferred)**  
   - Send short-lived notes or disappearing messages.  
   - Verify contacts with safety numbers.  
   - Use Notes-to-Self for key storage if working alone.

2. **Encrypted Archives (ZIP, 7z, age, or GPG)**  
   - Encrypt files locally before upload.  
   - Example: `7z a report.7z report.txt -p"five.random.words"`  
   - Share the archive file via ART or a secure link.  
   - Share the password separately through Signal or in person.

3. **Password-Protected Documents**  
   - For short written reports, create a password-protected PDF or document.  
   - Avoid cloud editors; send static files only.

4. **One-Time Links**  
   - Use self-destructing message tools like *Privnote*, *Onetimesecret*, or *Proton Pass Notes*.  
   - Message vanishes after first view.

5. **Pre-Shared Encryption Keys**  
   - Teams can agree on a shared key ahead of operations (using `age`, `GPG`, or `Cryptomator`).  
   - Encrypt files offline, then delete plaintext copies.  
   - Rotate or retire keys regularly.

6. **Secure Email for External Partners**  
   - Services like ProtonMail or Tutanota allow sending encrypted messages to non-users via password.  
   - Share the password through a different verified channel.

7. **Local ART Uploads (if supported)**  
   - Upload encrypted attachments directly within the ART platform.  
   - System auto-expires uploads and reduces metadata exposure.

---

<Callout type="important">
Never send passwords or keys in the same message as the encrypted file.  
Always verify identity before key exchange and delete temporary files after verification.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
🔐 **Quick Encryption Checklist**
- File encrypted before upload  
- Key shared via verified contact  
- Message deleted after delivery  
- Logs reviewed for cleanup
</Callout>

### Urgent Coordination Flows

| Event Type | Dispatcher Action |
|-------------|------------------|
| Active raid | Deploy safety, legal observer, and medic teams immediately |
| Checkpoint | Use rapid-response configuration |
| Court support | Notify care/presence teams early |
| Child separation | Prioritize trauma-informed volunteers |

### Ethical Dispatcher Mindset

- Stay calm and transparent  
- Seek help when uncertain  
- Respect consent and boundaries  
- Step back if another is better suited  

<Callout type="warning">
Never assign someone without consent, capacity check, and risk briefing.
</Callout>

<Callout type="hint">
Coordinate follow-through using  
[Community Consent](/courses/community-consent),  
[Care & Emotional Support](/courses/care-support), and  
[Legal Follow-through & Jail Support](/courses/legal-followthrough-jail-support).
</Callout>

### Practice Scenarios

#### Scenario 1: Urgent Checkpoint
- Verify via photo or second source  
- Choose team config and assign roles  
- Notify and track progress in real time  
Reflect: Would you request backup or archive?

#### Scenario 2: Unverified Social Media Rumor
- Flag “Unverified”  
- Check with trusted monitors  
- Archive if unconfirmed after 30 minutes
