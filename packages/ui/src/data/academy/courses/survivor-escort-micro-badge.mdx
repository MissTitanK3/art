# 🛡️ Survivor Escort Micro-Badge
<TrackBadge variant="court-support" />
<TrackBadge variant="community-care" />

<Callout type="info">
This **Micro-Badge** certifies volunteers to **escort and support survivors or key witnesses** through courthouses, legal appointments, or tense public environments — ensuring safety, calm, and consent at every step.
</Callout>

---

## Why It Matters

Survivors and witnesses often face **intimidation, retraumatization, or confusion** in legal spaces.  
A trained escort provides **calm navigation, emotional grounding, and discreet support** so they can move safely and confidently without drawing attention.

---

## What You'll Learn

1. **Soft Navigation** – Guiding through checkpoints, security, and waiting areas with minimal visibility.  
2. **Trauma-Informed Presence** – Recognizing stress signs and using grounding techniques.  
3. **Boundary & Consent Protocols** – Always offering choices, never imposing support.  
4. **Coordination with Legal Teams** – Working seamlessly with attorneys and marshals.

---

## Trauma-Informed Techniques

- **Grounding Phrases:**  
  *“Would you like me beside or behind you?”*  
  *“We can stop or leave at any time.”*

- **Non-Verbal Comfort Cues:**  
  - Hand squeeze or nod to signal pause.  
  - Pre-agreed exit phrase or gesture.

- **De-escalation Readiness:**  
  - Calm tone, minimal words.  
  - Position between survivor and perceived threat if needed.

---

## Courthouse Navigation Basics

- **Before Entering:**  
  - Review prohibited items (no weapons, certain electronics).  
  - Locate **bathrooms, quiet spaces, emergency exits**.  
  - Confirm **meeting point with attorney**.

- **During Escort:**  
  - Walk at a calm, steady pace.  
  - Avoid loud conversations or visible displays that draw attention.  
  - Keep awareness of exits and possible safe zones.

---

## Threat & Stress Response Protocols

1. **Comfort Check:** Notice shaking, shallow breathing, blank stare.  
2. **Grounding:** Apply 5-4-3-2-1 method or breathing cues if consented.  
3. **Positioning:** Place yourself as a buffer if approached.  
4. **Early Exit:** If safety or emotional state declines, initiate pre-agreed exit plan and notify legal team.

## Quick Action Steps

1. Always **ask for consent** before physical or verbal guidance.  
2. Know **layouts and quiet areas** in advance (bathrooms, exits, regroup points).  
3. Stay **neutral and steady**, even in confrontational environments.  
4. Debrief after escort with the survivor (and legal team if needed) to identify follow-up needs.

---

## Risks & Red Lines

- Overstepping can **disempower or embarrass the survivor**.  
- Drawing attention can **escalate risks or intimidation**.  
- Never **share personal or case information** with anyone outside the legal team.

---

## Scenario Drill

*"During a hearing, the opposing party approaches aggressively while the survivor freezes:*  
1. Move calmly between them.  
2. Use grounding phrase: *“We can leave now if you’re ready.”*  
3. Guide to a quiet space or exit as pre-planned.  
4. Notify legal team discreetly and reassess safety.

---

## Certification Checklist

- [ ] Can **escort survivors or witnesses discreetly** through legal/public spaces.  
- [ ] Provides **trauma-informed, consent-based support** consistently.  
- [ ] Knows **local courthouse layout and security protocols**.  
- [ ] Can execute **grounding techniques and exit plans** under stress.  
- [ ] Debriefs after each escort to refine support.
