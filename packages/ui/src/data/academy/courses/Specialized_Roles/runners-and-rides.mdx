# 🚗 Runners & Rides Coordination

---

## Who Are Runners?

**Runners** are trusted couriers who physically move items or information when digital channels are unsafe.

Used for:
- Delivering legal or logistical documents  
- Transporting sensitive tools (burners, jammers, encrypted drives)  
- Carrying verbal messages between field teams  

<Callout type="warning">
Runners face elevated surveillance risk.  
Keep all routes **need-to-know**, avoid patterns, and use controlled anonymity.
</Callout>

---

## What Is a Rides Role?

**Rides coordination** ensures safe, discreet transport for:
- Exiting hot zones  
- Delivering supplies  
- Relocating vulnerable community members (elders, children, or detainee families)

Balance **urgency, discretion, and safety** at all times.

---

## Safety Protocols for Runners

- Leave personal phones behind  
- Use drop points, never homes  
- Avoid predictable routes or landmarks  
- Seal documents (tamper-evident tape, numbered envelopes)  
- Maintain realistic, simple **cover stories**  

> Example: “Delivering groceries to a friend recovering from surgery.”

---

## If a Handoff Is Compromised

🛑 **Abort immediately. Do not improvise.**

1. Divert to neutral safe zone.  
2. Send abort signal (e.g., hat reversal, location pivot).  
3. Destroy sensitive papers safely (tear, soak, burn pouch).  
4. Avoid any discussion by phone.  
5. Report to coordinator and flag route as **compromised**.  

<Callout type="hint">
Never attempt recovery. A failed handoff is safer than a traced one.
</Callout>

## Rides Best Practices

- Use **burner phones** for coordination only  
- **Pre-stage fuel, maps, and cash**  
- Avoid discussing identities  
- Rotate vehicles, routes, and pickup points  
- Confirm drop zones as **neutral** and low-visibility  

<Callout type="warning">
Never use personal phones, vehicles, or legal names if avoidable.
</Callout>

---

## Coordinating Rides

Rides leads must:
- Stay calm during shifting risk  
- Validate participants through **indirect identity checks**  
- Track vehicles, not people  
- Abort or reroute without hesitation if signs appear unsafe  

| Coordination Step | Description |
|--------------------|-------------|
| Pre-run brief | Verify cover story, comms channel, and abort cue |
| Mid-run tracking | Checkpoints through coded phrases or radio ping |
| Handoff | Eye contact + simple confirm signal |
| Post-run | Debrief privately and log route notes securely |

| Water/snacks | Endurance & calm |
| Mask & gloves | Anonymity & health |
| Cash or gas card | No trace payments |
| Paper route map | Offline navigation |
| Radio or LoRa device | Offline comms backup |

---

## Emotional & Physical Aftercare

Runners and drivers often carry silent tension.  
- Debrief within 10 minutes of return  
- Hydrate and eat immediately  
- Avoid solo travel home after stressful missions  
- Keep decompression confidential  

<Callout type="info">
Fear is normal; secrecy magnifies it.  
Brief, factual debriefs prevent spiraling.
</Callout>

## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|-------------|
| **Burner Phone** | Prepaid, disposable phone used temporarily |
| **Tamper-evident** | Packaging that clearly shows access |
| **Air-gapped Device** | Computer isolated from any network |
| **Cover Story** | Innocent, consistent explanation for movement |
| **Abort Signal** | Pre-arranged gesture signaling mission stop |
