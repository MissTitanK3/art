# 🛰️ Tech Jamming & Signal Defense
<TrackBadge variant="direct-action" />
<TrackBadge variant="tech-comms" />

<Callout type="info">
This module trains **trusted technical operators** to defend communications under surveillance pressure.  
Focus: **detect, shield, and adapt** — not disrupt.
</Callout>

---

## Purpose of This Role

The **Tech Jamming Operator** safeguards network integrity by:

- Detecting surveillance and interference  
- Preserving comms under hostile conditions  
- Shielding or redirecting traffic safely  
- Supporting dispatch and field tech response  

It is a **high-trust**, **high-skill**, **defensive role** — never offensive.

---

## Threat Landscape

| Threat | Description |
|---------|--------------|
| **Cell tower spoofing** | Fake towers intercept calls or SMS |
| **IMSI catchers / Stingrays** | Capture device identifiers and metadata |
| **WiFi injection** | Coerces connections to spy networks |
| **Bluetooth sniffing** | Tracks presence via active BT signals |
| **Geofencing** | Alerts when a target enters defined zones |
| **Radio scanning** | Monitors unencrypted bands (FRS/GMRS) |

<Callout type="warning">
Treat all radio, LTE, and WiFi traffic as **observable**.  
Encryption adds friction, not invisibility.
</Callout>

---

## Defense Methods: Compare Approaches

| Method | Use | Legal Risk |
|---------|-----|-------------|
| **Jamming** | Block signals (radio, WiFi, LTE) | 🔥 Very high – illegal in most areas |
| **Shielding** | Physically block via Faraday enclosures | ✅ Safe |
| **Redirecting** | Re-route via mesh or VPN | ⚠️ Moderate |
| **Detection** | Identify active surveillance | ✅ Safe |

<Callout type="important">
Never transmit interference beyond your authorized frequencies.  
Defense ends where disruption begins.
</Callout>

## Compromised Gear Response

1. 🧹 **Wipe:** Secure-erase logs, SIMs, temp files.  
2. 🔒 **Lock:** Enable passcodes, remote kill.  
3. 📦 **Contain:** Seal in Faraday bag.  
4. 🧠 **Log:** Record time, device, and symptoms.  
5. 📡 **Escalate:** Notify dispatch / digital ops for audit.  

Never attempt self-repair on-site. Assume full compromise.

## Multi-Layer Comms Strategy

Follow **Least Privilege Principle**: only those who must know, do.

| Tier | Channel | Use |
|------|----------|-----|
| 1 | Encrypted apps (Signal, Session) | Primary messaging |
| 2 | Mesh (Meshtastic, goTenna) | Short-range fallback |
| 3 | QR, visual codes, paper maps | Last resort |

Assume surveillance on all public networks.

---

## Counter-Surveillance Habits

- 🔐 Always use device locks  
- 📴 Power down when idle  
- 🎭 Rotate burners and SIMs  
- 🚫 Avoid GPS-heavy apps  
- 👁️ Don’t leave gear unattended  

<Callout type="hint">
Log proximity events. Repeated RF anomalies may indicate a Stingray or rogue access point.
</Callout>

---

## Situational Response Guide

### 🚨 IMSI Catcher Detected
- Alert ops + dispatch  
- Move devices to Faraday containment  
- Switch to mesh or offline  
- Record location and time  

### 📵 Network Drop
- Assume tower spoofing or localized jamming  
- Switch to analog or mesh fallback  
- Execute regroup signal  
- Reconnect only via pre-vetted networks  

### 🎯 Volunteer Being Tracked
- Power down or isolate device  
- Place in Faraday bag  
- Escalate to digital security team

## Legal & Ethical Boundaries

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don’t |
|-------|----------|
| Shield or detect signals | Jam public or emergency frequencies |
| Operate within legal bands | Deploy interference near hospitals or airfields |
| Log every tool use | Hide deployments from dispatch |
| Educate others | Escalate conflict with counter-jamming |

<Callout type="warning">
RF interference on public channels is a federal offense.  
Defense tools must **shield**, not **suppress**.
</Callout>
