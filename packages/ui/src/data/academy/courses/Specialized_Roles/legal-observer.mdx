# 🕵️ Legal Observer Basics
<TrackBadge variant="observation-legal" />

<Callout type="info">
Legal Observers (LOs) are **neutral witnesses**. Their task is to **document law enforcement behavior**, not participate. They generate **legal records**, not political statements.
</Callout>

<Callout type="hint">
Build your foundation with:  
- [Know Your Rights (KYR) Basics](/courses/kyr-basics)  
- [Fourth Amendment Rights](/courses/fourth-amendment-rights)  
- [Interacting with Law Enforcement](/courses/interacting-with-law-enforcement)
</Callout>

---

## Role Overview

A Legal Observer:

- Remains impartial and visible  
- Documents police actions precisely  
- Records misconduct without intervention  
- Strengthens legal defense through factual evidence  

LOs **do not**:
- Engage protest chants or formations  
- Offer legal advice (unless licensed)  
- Interfere in arrests or tactics  

<Callout type="important">
Your credibility is your protection. Stay neutral, visible, and factual.
</Callout>

- [ ] Ink notebook (no pencil or erase tools)  
- [ ] Watch or phone with time visible  
- [ ] Legal Observer vest or ID  
- [ ] Fully charged device, tracking disabled  
- [ ] Optional bodycam or recorder (check legality)  
- [ ] Knowledge of local recording laws  

<Callout type="info">

Some states require dual consent for recording. Know your jurisdiction’s audio laws before activating any device.
</Callout>

## On-Site Protocol

1. **Check in** with LO coordinator or dispatch.  
2. Stay at **edges**, never embedded in crowds.  
3. Focus on **police actions**, not protestors.  
4. Record *time, place, identifiers, actions, impact*.  

| Field | Example |
|-------|----------|
| 📍 Location | “5th & Broadway, NW corner” |
| ⏱️ Time | “2:47 PM” |
| 👮 Officer ID | “Badge #4421, Officer Jameson” |
| 📹 Action | “Used baton on seated protestor” |
| 👤 Result | “Maria Lopez detained, visible arm injury” |

---

### Example Log
<div className="p-4 rounded bg-muted">
<p><strong>2:47 PM</strong> – Officer #4421 shoved protestor without warning.</p>
<p><strong>2:49 PM</strong> – Police deployed pepper spray; no dispersal order heard.</p>
<p><strong>2:53 PM</strong> – Three detained: Maria Lopez, Juan Rivera, unknown male.</p>
</div>

**Tips:**
- Use clear ink, no corrections by erasure.  
- Describe *actions*, not opinions.  
- Avoid adjectives like “brutal” or “peaceful.”  
- Maintain consistency between time, page, and entry format.

<Callout type="info">
If interpretation is needed for names or testimony, work with certified interpreters holding the [Translator Micro-Badge](/courses/translator-micro-badge) or [ASL Interpreter Micro-Badge](/courses/asl-interpreter-micro-badge).
</Callout>

## Recording Video

When legal:
- Film from safe, stable distance  
- Narrate neutrally: “Officer raises baton, crowd backing away”  
- Do not edit or cut files  
- Label and secure immediately  

<Callout type="warning">
Police cannot compel you to hand over footage without subpoena or warrant.  
Decline politely and notify your LO coordinator or legal counsel.
</Callout>

<Callout type="hint">
Follow [Documentation & Safety](/courses/documentation-safety) and [After-Action Data Hygiene](/courses/after-action-data-hygiene).  
Keep only essential footage, blur faces where possible, and encrypt storage.
</Callout>

---

## Dispersal Orders

- Comply unless explicitly cleared by legal supervisor.  
- Continue documentation only if safe.  
- Never block law enforcement or crowd movement.  

---

## Chain of Custody

Maintain admissibility by:

1. Labeling each file (time, date, observer, location).  
2. Logging each person who handles or copies it.  
3. Storing on encrypted drives.  
4. Transferring to legal coordinator only.

<Callout type="info">
If operating within an ART dispatch, align with [Responding to Dispatch Calls](/courses/responding-to-dispatch-calls) and [Dispatch Coordination](/courses/dispatch-coordination).  
Route arrests to [Court Support](/courses/court-support) or [Legal Follow-through & Jail Support](/courses/legal-followthrough-jail-support).
</Callout>

---

## After the Event

- Deliver logs to assigned legal partner.  
- Join debrief for accuracy review.  
- Destroy duplicate or unsecured files.  
- Decompress — observation can be emotionally heavy.

---

## Managing Stress

Observation means witnessing without stopping harm. It can be painful.

- Debrief with trusted peers.  
- Rest, hydrate, stretch, and eat.  
- Use 4-4-4-4 breathing (inhale, hold, exhale, hold).  
- Take breaks between assignments.

<Callout type="info">
You’re part of the safety net. Documentation prevents erasure, even if you couldn’t intervene.
</Callout>
