# Pod Creator Video Content Framework
<TrackBadge variant="pod-leadership" />

This guide includes **30 modular video ideas** for Pod Creators. Use them in any order — post solo, in batches, or as part of a storytelling series.

The goal: help you **build trust, grow locally, and weave into the network**.

---

## 🔹 ROOTS & REASON

> Build legitimacy and emotional grounding in why pods matter.

1. **Why I’m Creating a Pod**  
   Your motivation. Community need, lived experience, or systemic failure.

2. **What a Pod Actually Is**  
   Simple breakdown: not an app, not a group chat — a safety unit.

3. **When the System Fails, We Show Up**  
   Real or hopeful moment: “There was no one else to call. So we built something.”

4. **Start With 2 People**  
   Normalize tiny starts. Signal group, DMs, kitchen table chats.

5. **Pods Are Not Branches**  
   Clarify: “We’re not a franchise. We’re nodes in a network. No approval required — just values.”

6. **I Don’t Know Everything. That’s Why We Learn Together.**  
   Normalize imperfect action. Invite others in.

---

## 🔹 TOOLKIT & SETUP

> Teach the basics of how to create and run a pod.

7. **How to Set Up a Signal Pod**  
   Visual guide: group creation, disappearing messages, admin settings.

8. **Quick Tour of ICE Tea Tools**  
   Dispatch, Watch, Academy — show how they support pod creation.

9. **What Is a General Pod?**  
   “This is your entry point. Not command — connection.”

10. **Why We Don’t Name Groups ‘ICE Watch’**  
   Subtle opsec: safe naming, quiet signals, calm comms.

11. **Boundaries & Agreements in Our Pod**  
   Who’s on call, what’s urgent, what gets escalated, when do we sleep?

12. **Pod Roles Breakdown**  
   Stabilizer, Witness, Driver, Signal Comms, Coordinator — everyone fits.

---

## 🔹 RECRUITMENT & INVITATION

> Grow your pod through authentic outreach and values.

13. **Who I’m Looking For in My Area**  
   Call in folks based on location, trust, and shared values.

14. **3 Ways to Plug Into a Pod**  
   Light touch, medium lift, full involvement.

15. **Pod Work Isn’t Just For Protesters**  
   Highlight support roles: kitchen crew, legal follow-up, digital ops.

16. **You Can Be in a Pod Without Posting About It**  
   Normalize offline, quiet work. Everyone has a lane.

17. **DM Me. I’ll Help You Start Yours.**  
   Extend support across regions. Build the web.

18. **I’m Not a Leader. I’m a Link.**  
   Decentralized. Horizontal. Movement-based.

---

## 🔹 CONNECTION & CULTURE

> Anchor the pod in local, real-world relationships.

19. **Why Local Is Powerful**  
   “We don’t need to wait on national orgs. We defend our own blocks.”

20. **Meet the Pod (or My Dream Team)**  
   Introduce your team — or describe the team you’re dreaming of.

21. **What’s Already Happening Here**  
   Shoutout mutual aid orgs, court support, medics. Weave in, don’t overwrite.

22. **How We Handle Conflict or Confusion**  
   Model conflict without collapse. Embrace repair culture.

23. **Pod Moments That Give Me Hope**  
   “Last night someone stayed up just in case…” Small wins, big impact.

24. **We’re Not Ready. And That’s Okay.**  
   Name the fear. Name the courage. Keep building anyway.

---

## 🔹 VISION & EXPANSION

> Inspire regional growth and alignment with the network.

25. **What It Would Look Like If Every City Had a Pod**  
   “Imagine if no one had to face ICE alone…”

26. **Mapping Coverage Gaps in My Region**  
   Use the [Pod Directory](/courses/pod-directory) to show where help is needed.

27. **Connecting with the Network**  
   “No central office. Just mutual trust, shared tools, and constant learning.”

28. **How My Pod Is Growing**  
   Share milestones: “We had our first call tonight.”

29. **What I Need Help With**  
   Call for support: translations, roles, regional Signal support, etc.

30. **From 1 Person to a Node in the Network**  
   “I started this solo. Now we’re a team.”
